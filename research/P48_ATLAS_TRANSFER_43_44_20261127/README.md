# Move one exact phase atlas through a different section: 43D and 39D

**No new density record.** Dorofeev–Sun–Wang's [43D antipode packing](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.20359v4#S4) remains the applicable comparator (center density `137^(43/2)/(2^(223/2)·3^21) ≈ 226.517274889`, occupied fraction `4.64232679388410e−8` in the project's live table). We attempted to use additional, genuinely deep, canonical shifts in two denser horizontal carriers, without relying on the normal-diameter proxy. The result gives short, independent candidates and exact optima for these **specified** canonical-motif families.

| Normal section in the archived P48p | determinant | exact best singleton center density | exact best non-singleton squared minimum, size, center density |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `√3 D5` on standard coordinates 1–5 | 972 | `(3/2)^(43/2)/√972 ≈195.942632` | `335/60, 4, 4(335/240)^(43/2)/√972 ≈166.781745` |
| `√3 A5` on coordinates 1–6 of sum zero | 1458 | `(3/2)^(43/2)/√1458 ≈159.986489` | `990/180, 6, 6(990/720)^(43/2)/√1458 ≈147.836101` |

Here a canonical motif is an arbitrary set of complete translates in `L*/L`, not necessarily a subgroup. Any radius is allowed. Both runners-up have literal motifs below; neither beats the singleton. This says nothing about arbitrary real phases in the full 43D torus, switching a fraction of a translate, or a different embedding/carrier. As a separate check, the **normal-diameter guarantee** for the published det-3072 section cannot be improved by replacing its Euclidean diameter with the wrapped metric in its normal torus; this is **not** an assertion about the true horizontal metric for that section.

## Reuse of actual distances, rather than a new 43D closest-vector calculation

Let `Λ=P48p` be the specified even unimodular ambient of minimum 6. Use the published [P48p-adc3124f normal embedding](https://github.com/wcgbg/sphere-packing/blob/main/packings_out/records/dim39-delta18/P48p-adc3124f.cert.txt) and the [pinned, exactly replayed archive](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/tree/a3d8d1a49b771107ccf3245e64f54d4a750a196d/research/p48p_k9_actual_39_20261126) for the 39-dimensional complement of its primitive normal `K9`. That archive certifies the actual minima of every one of the 21,870 horizontal phases; one does not assume that the normal antipode inequality is attained. The present checker recomputes all of its literal short-vector lengths, enough for every *upper* bound here; the archived lower replay is needed only to interpret the full histograms as actual depths. The two runner-up lower witnesses have independent direct antipode certificates.

For a primitive rank-five section `K⊂K9`, put `V=span(K)^⊥`, `W=span(K9)^⊥`, `T=span(K9)∩V`. Thus `V=W⊥T`, with `dim(T)=4`. The unimodular gluing map gives a horizontal phase in `(Λ∩V)*/(Λ∩V)` for each `c∈K*/K`. A lift of its normal coordinate `y∈K9*` exists by primitivity and unimodularity, and has 39D horizontal component in the paired phase `a(y)`. Every member of that 39D translate can be used while keeping `y` fixed. Conversely any ambient lift of a point in the 43D phase projects to such a `y`. Therefore the true squared minimum of the 43D phase is exactly
```
h_43(c) = min { h_39(a(y)) + ||π_T y||² :
                 y∈K9*,  π_K y ≡ c mod K } .
```
Fixing an actual representative of `c` is harmless, because subtracting a vector of `K` leaves the other coordinates and 39D phase unchanged. The projection records distances on a torus, not distances between arbitrary chosen representatives. The ranks five and nine are primitive: the latter is certified by DSW, and within its first fixed `√3 E8` the stated intersections are exactly the standard `√3 D5` and `√3 A5`. The ambient intersection with the eight-space already equals `√3 E8` (as in the archive; alternatively `E8` is exactly the axial-zero sublattice in that primitive K9 formula). Unimodularity gives horizontal covolume `√det(K)`. Each carrier contains the archived L39 lattice (which has norm-6 vectors), so the self minimum is exactly 6.

The [39D replay's coordinate model](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/blob/a3d8d1a49b771107ccf3245e64f54d4a750a196d/research/p48p_k9_actual_39_20261126/README.md) writes dual normal vectors as `(x/√3,j/√30)`, where `x∈E8`, the axial digit `j∈Z`, and `j+x·α≡0 mod 3` with `α=(8,−1,…,−1)/3` in the A8-weight realization. If `X=3x` in that realization, then `x·α=X[0]`. The seven ternary digits of the archived group index are `(X[i]−X[0])/3 mod 3`, `i=1,…,7`; the final cyclic digit is `j mod 10`. The checker explicitly changes basis from standard `E8=D8∪(D8+½·1)`: it verifies the two root-basis Gram identities, the determinant, and the literal integer map `X=P·d/4`, with `d=2x` in standard coordinates. It uses only exact integer arithmetic for distances and BigInt for density.

- **D5.** Standard doubled `x` has equal coordinate parities and sum 0 mod 4. Its first five values are `d_i=2p_i` for `p∈D5*`. The 972 classes modulo `6D5` are represented by same-parity independent residues in 0…5 plus a carry 0 or 6 on the first coordinate. Fix them; let the three remaining coordinates be `u,v,w`. The transverse squared cost, in numerator units of 1/60, is `5(u²+v²+w²)+2j²`. Costs ≥6 cannot improve a witness already below 6. Thus the complete bounds are `|u|,|v|,|w|≤8`, `|j|≤13`. The four phases `[0,971,966,325]` in the checker's coordinate order have all pairwise normal squared distances at most `5/12`, and give the stated packing independently of the archive's lower claims.
- **A5.** Represent `p∈A5*` as `p_i=a_i−s/6`, `a_6=0`, `s=Σa_i`. The class modulo `3A5` is specified by the five residues `a_i mod3` and the sum of carries mod6, giving 1458 classes. Standard doubled `x` on the first six coordinates is `2a_i+t`; the two remaining coordinates are `u,v`. The transverse cost in units of 1/180 is `10(s+3t)²+15(u²+v²)+6j²`, so check only `|s+3t|≤10`, `|u|,|v|≤8`, `|j|≤13`. The six phases `[0,1453,1442,1407,1300,977]` have all pairwise normal squared distances at most `1/2`, and give the stated packing with an independent normal certificate.

The horizontal center density of `M` distinct phases of squared minimum `h` is `M(h/4)^(43/2)/√det(K)`. To prove the two sharp upper bounds, the checker loops over all witnessed depth values (any motif has some worst witnessed pair, and its actual separation can only be smaller). It anchors the motif at 0 by translation. At each level, a legal motif at that cutoff must be a clique among the neighbors of 0 in the difference graph. The first forbidden motif size is calculated by exact comparison to `m² h₀^43`, where `(m,h₀)` is its known runner-up in the same units. Greedy coloring of independent sets is used only as an upper bound; every remaining search branch is handled, and a node-limit exit would be a failure rather than a certificate. This took at most 162 branching nodes for the nontrivial runner-up tests. For the det-3072 normal-only check, a phase is represented by `48G⁻¹ u mod48` for integer dual coefficients `u`, with the matrix printed in DSW §4. The density threshold exceeds all 3072 possible phases for normal numerator ≥86; for numerator ≤85, `u_i² ≤ (85/48) G_ii` bounds the exhaustive search. The same clique proof compares strictly to DSW.

**Replay:** `node verify.js` after placing this directory beside the archived 39D directory, or `node verify.js /path/to/p48p_k9_actual_39_20261126`. The four required archive files are `embedding.json`, `gram.json`, `gens.json`, and `witness.gz`. All are unchanged in the pinned archive. The transfer principle also applies to a lower-rank section inside any previous actual-distance atlas; a good future target is a smaller 43- or 44-dimensional normal with projected deep classes that align, not another arbitrary full-shift search on these two.

## Separate analytical gate: no better 39D bare section with ≥6 fixed directions

Suppose `Λ` is any even unimodular 48D lattice of minimum 6 with an order-three isometry `g` whose fixed lattice is `F=√3(E8⊥E8)` (the structure used in [DSW §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.20359v4#S3), with the ambient occurrences stated there). For any primitive rank-nine normal section `B`, let `j=rank(B∩F)`. The inherited radius of its complementary 39D packing is `√6/2`. The DSW comparator is `(3/2)^(39/2)/√21870`, center density `18.35925948…` (occupied `1.68028177365432e−7`). A strict gain thus needs Gram determinant `det(B)<21870`. The theoretical window from the 9D Hermite constant `γ9=2` ([Dutour Sikirić–van Woerden](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20719)) starts at `3^9=19683`, allowing at most `√(10/9)−1≈5.41%` gain in the unrestricted bare route.

For `A=B∩F`, the projected quotient `π_{A^⊥}B` is a rank-`9−j` lattice: the intersection is primitive, and a basis extends to one of `B`. Its projection onto the moving space has the same rank, and the determinant can only decrease under this additional projection (the Gram matrices differ by a positive semidefinite matrix). Since `g` is order three, a nonzero moving projection `z` of any ambient vector satisfies `||v−gv||²=3||z||²≥6`. Hence this latter lattice has squared minimum ≥2, and `det(B)` is at least `det(A)` times the corresponding Hermite determinant floor. Also `det(A)` is `3^j` times a positive even-integral Gram determinant in `E8⊥E8`.

A stronger gate applies wherever `A` is contained in one fixed E8 factor. For the full factor `F_1=√3 E8`, every nonzero projection perpendicular to `F_1` has squared norm ≥`10/3`. Indeed, its component in `F_1` belongs to `F_1^*=(1/√3)E8` and can be reduced by `F_1` to squared norm at most 3 ([Conway–Sloane, Voronoi regions of E8](https://neilsloane.com/doc/Me82.pdf)); minimum 6 gives the other component squared ≥3. Its squared norm is in `(2/3)Z`, by evenness of Λ and of E8, hence ≥10/3. A nonzero class in `B/A` cannot lie in `F_1`; this stronger squared minimum applies directly to `π_{A^⊥}B` whenever `A⊂F_1`.

- `j=9`: the scaled determinant is even by the alternating mod-2 Gram property in odd rank, so `det(B)≥2·3^9`.
- `j=8`: a competing unscaled even Gram determinant is <`21870/(2·3^8)=5/3`, so it must be 1. Then `A=√3 E8` in a single factor; the sharper one-dimensional bound gives `det(B)≥3^8·10/3=21870`.
- `j=7`: the determinant is even after unscaling. An unscaled determinant ≥4 gives `det(B)≥3^7·4·3>21870` by the rank-two Hermite bound. Determinant 2 is `E7`, in one factor. The sharper rank-two bound gives `det(B)≥2·3^7·(3/4)(10/3)^2>21870`.
- `j=6`: an unscaled determinant ≥8 gives `det(B)≥3^6·8·4=23328`. Below 8 the even rank-six possibilities are `E6,D6,A6`, all root-generated and irreducible. Use [Conway–Sloane's small determinant classification](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1988.0072), also reproduced as [Table 2 here](https://www.math.miami.edu/~cscaduto/papers/nonunimlattices.pdf). A decomposable even rank-six lattice has determinant ≥8 by the low-rank Hermite bounds. The det-7 listed alternative with root system `D5` is odd: a generator projecting perpendicular to its primitive D5 must have squared projection `7/4`, while the possible D5 dual-component squared residues are `0,1,5/4mod 2`, none making the total even. Primitivity here follows since a proper integral overlattice of D5 would have determinant 1 in rank five, impossible for an even lattice. All candidate intersections are in one factor: each root is in one of the two E8 factors, so nonorthogonal connected simple roots stay in the same factor. Applying the sharper rank-three bound gives `det(B)≥3^6·3·(10/3)^3/2=40500`.
- The same proof rules out `j=5` **when the intersection is contained in one fixed E8 factor**, even without a small determinant classification: the bounds `det(A)≥3^5·4` and `det(π_{A^⊥}B)≥(10/3)^4/4` give 30000.

This removes the entire high-overlap route, not just one list of candidate normal Gram matrices. In particular changing the last normal vector, or even replacing one or two of the fixed directions of that standard chain while still meeting the fixed space in six dimensions, cannot beat the benchmark. A putative record through this ambient symmetry needs a rank-nine section with at most five fixed directions, not contained in one factor if five. The theorem only concerns the **bare section at inherited radius**; it does not bound larger intrinsic radius of a specially constructed section or arbitrary multi-translate motifs.
