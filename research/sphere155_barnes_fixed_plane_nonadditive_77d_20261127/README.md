# Sphere155: the new 64-phase Barnes plane cannot beat the 77D density with complete D4 layers

**No new density record.** The construction below gives an independent tie in 76D. The main result is a uniform 77D no-gain theorem for all shifts, layer-dependent nonlinear colorings, and arbitrary heights in this particular carrier. It does not exclude a palette spanning several coefficient planes, mixed A/B half phases, larger normal periods, or variable layer occupancies. The project benchmark was pinned at [main 01a3209](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/blob/01a3209f43c7cebfe1f564976a907b7735d3d717/LOWER_BOUNDS_1_96.md). The actual 77D comparator is the [project's stop-checked half-root-tilt lattice, Gram determinant 6](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/blob/01a3209f43c7cebfe1f564976a907b7735d3d717/research/gamma72_root_tilt_20261122/README.md), not an unrelated quoted literature decimal. Horizontal base is [Nebe's extremal Γ72](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2862), with squared minimum 8 and covolume 1.

## The 64-phase depth library

Use Barnes coefficient triples for Γ as in Nebe §2.1: the three raw Leech blocks of a point of Γ are `(x,x+βy,αx+βy+2z)`, where αβ=2, α+β=1, and the metric is half the raw direct-sum squared norm. Let A be the α image on the Leech parity module: on A, α=1 and β=0 modulo two. Fix the good two-dimensional binary plane U=<720,8359937> in the reduced Leech parity coordinates of the [pinned source package](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/tree/a0875c99/verifications/improved/dim73-82-gamma72-depth6-lattice). The same plane test works for any good plane and nothing in the packing proof uses the bit labels. The depth of a shift is its minimum squared distance to Γ. The library consists of 64 half phases, given by all triples in U³; differences stay in the library. In the bits of a fixed basis of U, identify a triple with a 2×3 matrix, or equivalently a pair (a,b) in F₂³ × F₂³.

Every nonzero pure-A coefficient half phase r has depth ≥4, even across all possible U: write 2r=αy modulo 2Γ and choose the representative αy/2. For λ∈Γ, r+λ=(α/2)(y+βλ); α is a similitude of squared factor 2, and y+βλ is nonzero when the coset is nontrivial. Thus the squared norm is ≥8/2=4.

For completeness, the reason the depth calculation transfers out of the integral chart is the Barnes action. The Barnes isometry group ±PSL₂(7) reduces on the A component to GL₃(F₂): the reflection in the norm-two first Barnes basis vector sends b₂ to b₂+αb₁ and acts nontrivially after reduction, and simplicity of PSL₂(7) and order 168 give surjectivity. Thus row-mixing moves a coefficient triple of rank at most two to (0,y,z) without changing the coefficient span. Choose y=αλ; division by two gives the integral raw representative (0,λ,λ+z), with B-total zero and A-differences spanning <y,z>. Rank one can be moved to (0,0,z); by Nebe Lemma 3.1, every (isotropic) A class has a Leech representative of squared norm ≤8, so this phase has depth exactly 4. Rank two has depth exactly 6 by the good-plane test in the integral chart: no raw 4+4 or 4+6 representative (for the three possible pairs of nonzero A labels), and raw 6+6 always exists by solving the two independent anisotropic parity equations in B. The literal plane and its root sets can be replayed using the [pinned KissingNumbers checker](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/tree/902483c2bbf14f9ec8c6626428c9a7c75de29eb2/research/gamma72_half_phase_palette_and_qr48_chords_20261127); this proof only needs one such plane. Consequently, for this library the depth of a difference is 0, 4, or 6 exactly according to its matrix rank 0, 1, or 2. This also explains why the previously found eight mutually depth-six shifts are part of a mathematically larger usable alphabet rather than merely eight unrelated colors.

## A doubled-code obstruction for the 5D and 6D root graphs

Use the unscaled D_n={x∈Z^n:Σx_i even}, with minimum squared norm 2. Its parity group D_n/2D_n is a two-to-one cover of the binary even-weight code E_n, via coordinate reduction modulo 2. Both lifts over any binary weight-two word are root parities: e_i+e_j and e_i−e_j differ by the nontrivial kernel element 2e_j. Therefore the distance-2 root graph on D_n/2D_n is obtained by replacing each vertex of the Hamming-distance-2 graph of E_n by two nonadjacent twins and replacing each edge by a complete bipartite graph between the twin pairs.

For n=5 a binary independent set has size at most 2: translate one word to zero; any further word has weight 4, and two distinct weight-4 words in length 5 differ in precisely two positions. For n=6 the corresponding bound is 4: a code of size m with pair distances ≥4 satisfies 4·m(m−1)/2≤6·m²/4 by the coordinatewise count of disagreements. The doubled graph independent-set bounds are therefore 4 and 8 respectively. In either case, every set that attains the bound consists of complete twin pairs (its projection has maximum size and each fiber must be full).

Suppose there were an injective assignment of the 32 (respectively 64) parity labels of D5 (respectively D6) into the 64 library matrices such that all root edges have rank-two differences. Each of the eight lines a=constant has rank-one differences between distinct labels. Its preimage is independent, with size ≤4 (respectively 8). The total number assigned forces every line to attain the bound. The eight lines b=constant similarly have preimages that are unions of twin pairs. Take any selected (a,b): the two line preimages intersect in exactly its singleton, impossible since intersections of unions of twin pairs are even. No additive hypothesis was used.

These assignments would have to be injective for a full D5 or D6 period-two normal graph packing at squared separation 8, not just for lattice glues. Every nonzero normal parity has a representative of squared norm ≤4 in D5 and ≤6 in D6: the nonzero weight-ℓ classes have a vector with ℓ entries ±1, by altering one sign to choose the kernel lift, and the nonzero kernel class has representative 2e_i. Repeated horizontal labels would therefore violate separation. The det 4 integer carriers would have offered actual center-density ratios √(6/4) in 77D and √((27/4)/4) in 78D, but this entire fixed-plane period-two mechanism is ruled out, including arbitrary nonlinear colorings.

## Continuous 77D result: every D4 layer gap is at least √(3/2)

Precise setup: in R^72⊥R⁴⊥R, use any locally finite stack of complete D4 layers with distinct real heights H_i and arbitrary shifts S_i∈R⁴. For each point x+S_i of a layer (x∈D4), keep a full horizontal Γ translate. The choice of shift is an arbitrary map f_i:D4/2D4→U³, independent for each layer. Assume true squared separation ≥8. No common height/shift period, no common label map, and no homomorphism requirement.

Two elementary metric facts: `covrad(D4)^2=1`, and `covrad(D4*)^2≤1/2`. For the first, round to a nearest integer vector. If its parity is even, the squared error is ≤1. If odd, fix parity in a coordinate of largest absolute error m≤1/2, for bound 4m²+1−2m≤1. The point e₁ attains 1. For the second, D4*=Z⁴∪((1/2)^4+Z⁴); for coordinate errors t_i∈[0,1/2], summing the squared distances to the integer and half-integer grids gives Σ[t_i²+(1/2−t_i)²]≤1. Hence the closer grid is at squared distance ≤1/2. All z∈D4*\D4 have squared distance 1 to D4, either by odd integer parity or by four half-integer coordinates. Modulo D4 translations and isometries, all such deep holes are equivalent to e₁. For the half-integer case, each coordinate can be reduced to ±1/2 by an even integer shift, signs can be changed by D4 isometries, and the symmetric Sylvester Hadamard matrix divided by two takes e₁ to (1/2)^4 and preserves D4 (integral output with even sum; the matrix is its own inverse).

Take two occupied layers with squared height gap a<3/2 and horizontal-normal relative displacement s. Each D4 parity map is injective and the paired maps have disjoint images: any repeated horizontal phase would have a true pair with projected squared distance ≤covrad(2D4)²=4, giving distance²≤a+4<8 (within one layer take a=0). Because all phase differences have depth ≤6, every projected pair must have squared distance ≥2−a. Thus d²=dist(s,D4)²≥2−a>1/2. Choose z∈D4* with Δ=s−z and w=||Δ||²≤1/2. It is a deep hole since d²>1/2.

Snap s to z purely as a diagnostic, retaining the injections of both layers. Within each layer, q=2 edges have depth 6 by the original packing condition. The two parity sets, with q=1 cross edges at z, form exactly the D5 root parity graph: reduce z to e₁ by the above isometry, and write the two copies as (v,0) and (v+e₁,1) modulo 2D5 for v∈D4/2D4. In the same layer, the roots are the D4 ones. Between layers the roots at vertical +1 have horizontal ±e_j; the roots at vertical −1 give the same list modulo 2D4, since 2e₁ toggles the ±e_j lifts for every j. Thus the previous injection obstruction forces at least one q=1 cross edge with depth 4. Choose p=z+v with ||p||²=1 for that edge. The opposite −p is also a member of the deep-hole coset (because 2z∈D4), irrespective of its horizontal label. Hence
```
d² ≤ ||−p+Δ||² = 1+w−2<p,Δ>,
||p+Δ||² ≤ 2+2w−d² ≤ 3−d² ≤ 1+a.
```
The actual pair associated to p consequently has squared distance ≤4+a+1+a<8, a contradiction.

The gap bound implies vertical mean frequency ≤1/√(3/2), even in nonperiodic stacks. Each layer's eight-norm packing uses complete Γ fibers, and the D4 covolume is 2. Thus with radius √2 the 77D center density is at most 2^(77/2)/(2√(3/2))=2^(77/2)/√6, exactly the project's attributed comparator (ordinary density multiplies by the volume of the 77D unit ball). This does not establish equality achievable within our fixed-plane subclass, much less global 77D optimality.

## Independent positive 76D assembly, a tie only

D4/2D4 has an exactly two-dimensional root-free subspace R, the preimage of {0000,1111} in its coordinate reduction: its three nonzero members have norm 4, all other members norm 2. Identify the group linearly with F4² in coordinates (u,v), R={v=0}, and the good plane with F4 as an additive space. For a primitive element ξ∈F4, assign the 16 normal parities the Barnes triples (u,v,ξv). When v≠0, v and ξv are distinct nonzero and hence independent over F2; their phase has depth 6. For v=0, u≠0 has depth 4. The zero class uses 2D4, minimum squared norm 8, so all distances are certified. Density is 2^38/2=2^37 in center convention, the current 76D value, not a new bound.

The proof uses the fixed plane's exact depth alphabet {0, 4, 6}. It does not rule out a larger alphabet that includes depth 5 or a different carrier.
