# Sphere density research, round 20

**Scoped exact results. No change to the lattice or general density records.**
The verified opening comparison is the public `SpherePackings/main` at
`f1482e70b87937c8b50b54965d81818133442aa6`.

- **92D, non-Nebe:** [Complete index-47⁴ obstruction and almost all of index 47⁵](92-mw-cusp-index4-5).
  Among sublattices of Shioda's MW92 invariant under the fixed cusp
  translation, all 4,985,761 of index 47⁴ have squared minimum at most
  22, and a direct short-witness screen excludes 234,277,752 of 234,330,768
  at index 47⁵. The fifth-jet complement has 53,016 primitive lifts of
  two short-prefix orbits; it is not certified to improve.
- **92D and transferable:** [Gauss-sum reduction](92-mw-gauss-period).
  The smallest density-preserving similarity period along the invariant
  MW92 filtration is 23. The same proof gives period (p−1)/2 for an
  integral fixed-point-free prime-order isometry. Independent polynomial
  witnesses settle exact minima at depths 3 and 8 (22 and 32).
  The latter excludes all mixed types at index 47⁹.
- **12D:** [Weighted ternary packets](12-e6-weighted-packets).
  At most 189 packets can have at least four sites in the equal-scale
  E6×E6 family. A second exact degree-moment condition, together with
  the earlier heavy-packet cap, excludes all mixtures of occupancies
  4–6, with at most 920 sites against the 923 needed.
- **48D:** [Negative-slot determinant theorem](48-e8-determinant-negative).
  Cap 16,800 for arbitrary nonlinear half-period motifs in the
  seven-slot zero-sum E8 carrier for a<0, 0<b≤2 and c≥max(1,b);
  no upper restriction on c. This does not prove P48 optimal.
- **48/72D and conditional 96D:** [Paired fifth-torsion bounds and
  ternary stacking](48-96-third-fifth-tori).
  Modular covariance and shell moment integrality yield paired depth
  obstructions; a signed-shell Gram controls arbitrary ternary
  palettes for one added dimension in the stated radius ranges.
- **96D:** [Four-shell exact relaxation witness](96-golden-four-shell).
  The previous golden A=0 single-anchor-pair histogram survives the
  complete affine-unit constraints and marked Jacobi data through
  norm 16, with 556 positive weights. It is not a lattice.
