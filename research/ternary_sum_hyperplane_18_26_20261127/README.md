# Ternary sum-hyperplane periods in 18D and 26D
Date: 2026-11-27. Both constructed packings are verified; neither
improves the published unrestricted density in its dimension.

Let a copy of \(A_2\) have basis \(u,v\) with Gram
\(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{smallmatrix}\right)\).
For \(m\ge2\) put \(E=A_2^m\) and
\[
 X_m=\ker(f:E\longrightarrow\mathbb F_3),\qquad
 f((p_i u+q_i v)_{i=1}^m)=\sum_i(p_i+q_i)\bmod3.
\]
Then \(\mathrm{covol}(X_m)=3^{m/2+1}\) and its minimum squared
norm is 4: no root of \(E\) lies in \(X_m\), and a sum of suitable
roots in two distinct \(A_2\) factors does. In one factor put
\(g=(u-v)/3,\ w=(u+v)/3\).

## The exact torus metric and the ternary bank constraint

All of \(X_m^*/X_m\) is parameterizable as
\[
 (a,d,s) =
 a(w,\ldots,w)+(d_i g)_{i=1}^m+s(u,0,\ldots,0)\pmod{X_m},
 \qquad a,s\in\{0,1,2\},\ d\in\mathbb F_3^m .
\]
Indeed \(E^*/E\) is generated by \(g\) independently in each factor,
and \((w,\ldots,w)\) pairs with \(E\) by \(f/3\) modulo integers,
generating \(X_m^*/E^*\). The three choices of \(s\) cover \(E/X_m\).
Changing an integer representative \(d_i\) by 3 adds \(u-v\),
which is in \(X_m\).

For differences within one bank \(a\), if \(d\ne0\) the squared torus
separation is \(2\,\mathrm{wt}(d)/3\), independent of \(s\). If \(d=0\)
and the color difference is nonzero it is 2. For consecutive banks
\(a\to a+1\pmod3\), put \(\epsilon=1\) for the wrap \(2\to0\), else 0.
The exact squared torus separation for \((a,d,s)\) and
\((a',d',s')\) is
\[
 \frac{2m}{9}+\frac23\,[s'-s+\mathrm{wt}(d'-d)-2m\epsilon]_3,
\]
where the bracket is the representative in \(\{0,1,2\}\).
These formulas minimize over all period translates. To see this,
in one factor fix the color \(j=f\) of its integer correction.
The exact local minima, in units \(1/9\), are
\((0,18,18)\) for \(a=d=0\), \((6,6,6)\) for \(a=0,d\ne0\),
and \(2+6[j+\mathbf 1_{d\ne0}]_3\) for \(a=1\).
They are confirmed by the short exact replay; testing corrections
in \([-3,3]^2\) is complete because each listed minimum is at most
18 in numerator units and
\(2(x^2+y^2-xy)\ge x^2+y^2\).
Across factors, independent corrections must have the assigned
total residue. In the same-bank formula any nonzero \(d_i\) can
realize all three residues at its optimal cost. In a cross bank
the least total nonnegative extra residual is the required total
residue itself (take the correction in one factor). At a wrap
\(-2w=w-3w\), requiring the color carry \(-2m\).

For 18D, \(m=9\). When the desired minimum squared separation lies
in \((8/3,10/3]\), each bank is one color function \(s_a(d)\) over
a ternary \((9,M_a,5)\) code. Every pair chosen from cyclically
consecutive banks must satisfy
\[
 s_{a+1}(e)-s_a(d)+Q(d)+Q(e)+d\cdot e=2
 \quad\text{in }\mathbb F_3,\qquad Q(d)=d\cdot d .
\]
There is no wrap carry since \(2m=18\). Subtract the four equations
for a rectangle of two words in each bank. This shows that their
difference spans \(U_a,U_{a+1}\) are orthogonal. When all three
banks are nonempty, fix one word in each of the adjacent two
banks and add the incoming and outgoing equations at a third
bank's word: its color cancels, and each bank lies in a single
level of \(2Q(d)+\ell_a(d)\) for a linear form \(\ell_a\).

## Exact optimum over the full 18D phase alphabet

**Theorem.** Allow any subset of all \(3^{11}=177147\) phases in
\(X_9^*/X_9\), with any common sphere radius, and period \(X_9\).
The exact maximum center density is
\[
 \frac{162}{3^{11/2}}\left(\frac56\right)^9
 =\frac{5^9}{2^8 3^{21/2}}\approx0.0745962334.
\]
It matches the value in the [18D remark of Chen et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02394v1#S3)
without claiming their unpublished explicit carrier, and it is
strictly below the [Bierbrauer–Edel 18D packing](https://www.yvesedel.de/Papers/Sphere.pdf)
of center density \((3/4)^9\approx0.0750846863\).
This period is an index-27 refinement of the period in the
rank-six fixed-Leech alphabet described in the preceding round;
the construction and upper bound here do not require that embedding.

**Explicit link to the Leech period.** The companion leech_frame.js
realizes the rank-six section in ordinary Golay coordinates divided
by \(\sqrt8\). The six normal vectors have Gram congruent by an
integral unimodular matrix to \(3A_{E_6}^{-1}\). The shell has Leech
vectors of all six unit pairing profiles, proving saturation of the
section: the coefficients of a Leech vector in the normal span
are its integer pairings with these dual-basis witnesses. For
normal profile \((0,-2,2,2,1,0)\), the projected squared norm
in the normal space is 2 and its 27 norm-four Leech lifts
project horizontally to nine orthogonal equilateral triples
of squared norms 2. Each triple gives one \(A_2\) factor of \(E\),
with the three vectors \(u,v,-u-v\). All 27 vectors are in one
horizontal \(L\)-coset, so their differences lie in \(L\).
They generate exactly \(X_9\): modulo their differences every
root in these triples has the same class, of order three because
the three roots sum to zero. The homomorphism \(f\) shows its
order is not smaller. Finally \(\det(X_9)=3^{11}\) and
\(\det(L)=3^5\), so \([L:X_9]=27\).

**Upper at the critical gate.** The independent coding input
\(A_3(9,5)=81\) is the 9,5 entry of
[Brouwer–Hämäläinen–Östergård–Sloane (1998), Table I](https://neilsloane.com/doc/Me226.pdf).
The bound \(A_3(9,6)\le27\) is also given by the exact two-term Hamming
dual in the verifier. Two banks therefore give at most 162 words.
If there are three banks, write \(d_a=\dim U_a\). In a bank of affine
span dimension at most three the bound is 27. In span dimension
four, if \(Q|_{U_a}=0\) then all distances in this bank are multiples
of 3, so its distance-five code is really distance six and has
at most 27 words. If \(Q|_{U_a}\ne0\), take parallel lines in a
direction where the quadratic coefficient is nonzero: at most
two words per line can lie on the required quadric. This gives
a bound of 54 words. Hence all three banks of dimensions at most
four have total at most 162. If one dimension is at least six,
the others are at most three by orthogonality, giving \(81+27+27\).
If the maximum is five, the other two are at most four. If at most
one has dimension four, the total is at most \(81+54+27\).
If both have dimension four, their two spans equal the
four-dimensional orthogonal complement of the dimension-five
span. They are perpendicular to each other, so this common span
is isotropic, and the bound is \(81+27+27\). This covers every
nonlinear motif without enumerating the 177147 labels.

Compare \(M\mu^9\) in each row to \(162(10/3)^9\).

## Separate constructive transfer to 26D

For \(m=13\) use a ternary extended Golay \([12,6,6]_3\) code
\(G\) on the first twelve coarse coordinates. The verifier gives
a literal six-row basis from cyclic generator
\(2+2x+x^2+2x^3+x^5\) in length 11, extended by the negative coordinate
sum, and checks all nonzero weights and orthogonality.
In bank \(a\) take all \(729\) words of \(G\), append the spare
coordinate \(\beta_a\), and choose colors \(s_a\), with
\[
 (\beta_0,\beta_1,\beta_2)=(0,0,1),\qquad(s_0,s_1,s_2)=(0,2,0).
\]
Every same-bank nonzero coarse difference has weight at least
six. Across consecutive banks the bracket in the metric
formula equals 2: differences in \(G\) have weights divisible
by 3, and the spare coordinate pays the wrap carry \(-26\).
Therefore the cross-bank squared separation is \(38/9\) and
the within-bank squared minimum is 4. The 2187 phases have
unit packing radius and center density
\(2187/3^{15/2}=1/\sqrt3\), equaling the attributed
[\(\Lambda_{26},T_{26}\) benchmark](https://cohn.mit.edu/sphere-packing/#data),
not improving it.

The cross-bank slack \(2/9\) leaves some freedom, but merely
moving identical whole Golay fibers in the last \(A_2\) plane
cannot increase the density at radius one. More explicitly,
fix horizontal period \(X_{12}\), and for each of the three
first-coordinate bank labels and each color \(s\) allow only
the full 729-word horizontal fiber. In the last plane place
these fibers at arbitrary positions, even without periodicity.
For two fibers in the same bank in the plane, take equal
coarse words from the two fibers. Their horizontal squared
torus separation is 0 if the color is the same, and 2 if
it differs. Their normal points must thus have pair separation
at least \(\sqrt2\), for each bank separately. Planar triangular
packing bounds the intensity of each bank by \(1/\sqrt3\).
Each fiber has horizontal intensity \(729/3^7=1/3\), so the
26D upper is \(1/\sqrt3\) for this full-fiber model at radius
one. The live constructive lever would have to change coarse
horizontal stocks at adjacent plane positions (or change the
horizontal metric), not just shift whole fibers.
