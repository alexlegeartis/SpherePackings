# Strict local optimality of the 35D nonradical lift among lattices

This is a local shape theorem about the lattice from [the 35D certificate](../), **not** a new density lower bound, a global optimality result, or a statement about nonlattice perturbations.

The coordinate matrix \(G\) in the replay is four times the Euclidean metric for the integer-coordinate representation: first 34 entries are the twice-horizontal coordinates and the last is the layer; its squared contribution is \(3/2\) times the layer squared. The 630 listed lattice vectors have squared norm 8 in the actual metric. Their contact forms span all \(35(35+1)/2=630\) symmetric matrix coordinates; the proof also gives a strictly positive linear combination representing \(6G^{-1}\).

Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python verify_extremality.py` from this directory (NumPy and SciPy). It reads the pinned [34D parent certificate](../../e7_d6_lattice34_20261125/cert.json) and the contact rows. Floating operations only generate integer approximations \(B_0,w_0\); the membership, norms, residuals and the certification inequalities are integer, with fixed-width overflow bounds checked. For \(A\) the symmetric contact-form matrix, \(b=\operatorname{vech}(6G^{-1})\), define \(e=\|TI-B_0A\|_\infty\), \(k=\|B_0\|_\infty\), \(r=\|Wb-Aw_0\|_\infty\), and \(m=\min w_0\). Then \(e<T\) proves invertibility and \(\|A^{-1}\|_\infty\le k/(T-e)\); \((T-e)m>kr\) proves every exact coordinate of \(A^{-1}b\) is positive. No assertion about the complete contact shell enumeration is required.

To see strict local optimality directly, fix the squared minimum by rescaling. Every sufficiently small admissible symmetric perturbation \(H\) of the metric satisfies \(v^\top Hv\ge0\) for all listed contacts. Positive eutaxy and their span give \(\operatorname{tr}(G^{-1}H)\ge c\|H\|\) on that cone for some \(c>0\) (compactness on its unit section). Hence \(\log\det(G+H)-\log\det G>0\) for every sufficiently small nonzero feasible \(H\). This proves strict local lattice density maximality up to similarity, in all 630 metric parameters.

By [Schürmann, Theorem 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.5534), the lattice is also a (weak) local density maximum at every fixed finite periodic realization. This rules out sufficiently small density-increasing joint metric-and-motif changes in each such realization. The neighborhood can depend on the representation; strict periodic isolation (non-floating) is not established.
