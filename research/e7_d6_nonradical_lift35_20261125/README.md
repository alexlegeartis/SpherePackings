# 35-dimensional nonradical D6/E7 lift

Take the [verified 34D D6/E7 lattice L](../e7_d6_lattice34_20261125/) at squared minimum 8 and covolume 32768. This distinct 35D lattice has center density **8/√3**, ordinary density **0.00000154512247769657** (rounded down), squared minimum **8**, and covolume **32768√(3/2)**. It improves the preceding [five-E7 35D lattice of center density 4](../e7_hermitian35_20261123/) by **15.470054%**, and the older B35 of center density 2√2 from the [Nebe–Sloane lattice catalogue](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/homes/Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/density.html) by **63.299316%**. This is a constructive lower bound in both the unrestricted and lattice classes, not an optimality result.

## Shift and lattice

The coordinates and binary syndromes for the four E7 factors of L are those of the [parent](../e7_d6_lattice34_20261125/); the pinned data at commit 8266a13e0c13bc8d6d9f0ae3d3d6e1f577981de2 are copied into cert.json. In the six Euclidean coordinates choose q=(3/2,0,0,0,0,1/2). In the four E7 simple-root coefficient systems, let b be the four 0/1 coefficient vectors of the bit masks (21,60,41,40) with coordinate 0 the least significant bit. All four are nonradical. Choose any integral four-block vector v of syndrome 896, and put s=(q,b/2+v). For a fully specified v with no search, use field symbols t0,t1 in the first two blocks, satisfy t0+t1=896 mod 64=0 and 1·t0+14·t1=floor(896/64)=14 in F64, take both radical bits zero and the last two blocks zero. Map each field symbol to the 0/1 coefficient vector given by the phase_isometry table. Different choices of v differ by a vector in L.

The new lattice is generated by L×{0} and (s,√(3/2)). Its covolume is the parent's covolume times √(3/2) by the height decomposition; no finite-order assumption on s is necessary. In this example 4s belongs to L.

## Exact separation

| New layer relative to 0 | Minimum squared horizontal distance against the two banks of L | Vertical squared contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ±1 | 13/2, 13/2 | 3/2 |
| ±2 | 2, 6 | 6 |
| any absolute layer at least 3 | nonnegative | at least 27/2 |

The horizontal squared distance for the zero layer is at least 8 (attained). The two parent banks are inverse-stable individually since the parent is an index-two overlattice. In the first new layer the exterior offset in bank m is b minus m times the parent half-radical tuple. Coefficientwise b-r=(b XOR r)-2(r AND NOT b), so the subtracted integral carry is transferred to the syndrome (its sign is immaterial in characteristic two). For the second layer, the doubled local point belongs to D6 and has parent tag 88, while the four integral b blocks have syndrome 919. Removing this projected local fiber leaves just integral exterior syndrome 975. The table thus checks every possible 35D lattice difference.

Run `python verify.py` (NumPy) or `node verify_dp.js` (no dependencies) in this directory. Both use an exact integer LDL ellipsoid factorization to enumerate E7 norms in all 16384 coefficient classes modulo 4, verifying a representative of squared norm at most 24 for every residue. The Python verifier independently solves the two F64 equations and one binary equation directly for all 32768 phase assignments per syndrome; Node uses min-plus convolution. They also compute the local D6 minima by different methods (integer boxes versus parity dynamic programming). The parent proof supplies the zero-layer minimum. The occupied density is (8/√3) π^(35/2)/Γ(37/2).
