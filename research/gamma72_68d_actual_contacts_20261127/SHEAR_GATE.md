# What the complementary 80D route would actually need

This is a constructive criterion, not a new 82D/83D bound. The current [Bachoc–Nebe 80D parent with parity extensions](../packing83_85_deep_verified_20261117/README_DEEP.md) has minimum squared 8. The 82D restriction uses an `A2` normal lattice, while the 83D extension uses `A3`. Their three or six short normal parities use certified horizontal half-cosets of depth 6. More distant normal parities contribute at least 4, and all-even normal vectors contribute at least 8. The project's attributed exact 82D value is `delta^2=2^82/3`, current 83D `delta^2=2^81`; those are centre-density squares. Simply finding a slightly deeper real translate in each root class would not suffice to lower the normal determinant: the all-even normal contacts with horizontal phase zero are already tight at 8.

There is a literal mechanism that would bypass this. Write the packing with lattice centres `(x+b(z),z)` for `x∈L`, `z∈Z^k`, where the squared normal length is `z^TVz` and the homomorphism `b mod L` consists of half phases. For each nonzero `z` with normal square at most 8 whose full phase reaches exactly 8, let `S_z` be the nearest horizontal displacements in its coset. If a nonzero linear map `C: R^k→R^80` satisfies

```
 C z is orthogonal to every vector of S_z,   for every such tight z,
```

then replace the horizontal lift by `b(z)+epsilon C z` and the normal Gram by `V−epsilon² C^T C`. For a nearest contact the change in the horizontal square is exactly `epsilon²||Cz||²` and cancels the decrease in the normal square. At a zero-phase tight contact the nearest horizontal vector is zero, so its equation is vacuous but the same cancellation works. In-phase distances with `z=0` stay fixed. Every finite remaining short pair has a strict separation gap; for sufficiently small epsilon no new pairs can emerge from infinity because the perturbed positive definite metric remains uniformly comparable to the old one on the fixed lattice coordinates. Thus this would give a genuine periodic lattice of minimum 8 with strictly smaller determinant; the Gram decrease is strict since `C≠0`. Since each half-coset contact shell is centrally symmetric, this orthogonality is also necessary for this particular compensated shear: any nonzero contact linear term violates one of the two opposite signs.

For the known 80D stocks there is no certified nonzero map `C`. Replaying the old case101 decoder at a larger cutoff to prove the entire contact null-space is not cheap: its original smaller cutoff already traversed 5,877,535,660 nodes. One should instead use the 10-block split and a stabilizer of a given half-class to generate a contact spanning set, or find an exact orthogonal complement without listing its entire shell. For an even depth-4 half class of an extremal even unimodular 48D lattice, the analogue is already obstructed: the even index-two neighbour has the same depth-4 shell in that class, no roots, and its degree-two associated harmonic theta must vanish (`S_26=Delta E4^2 E6`, whose first coefficient is nonzero). Thus those contacts span the whole horizontal space. The rank-80 norm-6 case does not have this same immediate vanishing (`Delta^3 E6` is a possible degree-two harmonic theta).
