## Construction

Use [Nebe’s Γ72](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2862) and the
[Leech-coordinate description](../gamma72_depth6_20261122/).
Start with a four-dimensional vertical D4 and a map from its integral
coefficients into Γ72 half cosets. The D4 Cartan Gram is unchanged;
the four horizontal phase representatives in this calculation are specified
explicitly rather than taken from an implicit choice of basis modulo two.
For rank $k=1,2,3$, embed the leading A1, A2, or A3 chain of D4 roots
into the old D4. For a tail vector $z$ in that chain, use projection $z/2$
onto the old D4 and a new orthogonal component of squared length
$3\lVert z\rVert^2/4$. Thus the vertical squared norm for old vector $x$ is
$\lVert x+z/2\rVert^2+3\lVert z\rVert^2/4$.
The Gram determinant is $4(3/4)^k(k+1)$.

Each generator is also assigned an explicit integral Leech-block phase.
The full lattice is generated by the horizontal Γ72 together with
those vertically lifted generators. The full Gram is verified as
`[G, GW^t/2; WG/2, WGW^t/4+V]`, for binary-phase numerators $W$
in the Γ72 basis and vertical Gram $V$; its determinant is `det$V$`.
The literal half-block coordinates are in [phases.json](phases.json).

For a proposed short vector, the lower bound on the horizontal part is
verified using the mod-two syndromes of its three Leech blocks. A
nonzero syndrome excludes norm zero; a dependent A pair is checked for
single-block norm 4 and 6 by the exact Leech shell and the polarized
quadratic form. An independent A pair forces at least two occupied
blocks, so only 4+4 and 4+6 need exclusion for required horizontal
depths 5 and 6. The complete short coefficient box in each dimension
comes from $z_i^2\leq 8(V^{-1})_{ii}$, not an empirical truncation.
The 77D, 78D, and 79D checks cover 67, 147, and 295 short parity/norm
cases, respectively. Γ72 itself attains squared norm 8.

## Replay

The integer numerator of each Gram is divided by 4:
[77D](gram77.json), [78D](gram78.json), [79D](gram79.json).
Run with Python, NumPy and SymPy:

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python verify.py
```

This checks the pinned parent files, reconstructs all Grams and their
determinants, proves the minimum, and checks [results.json](results.json),
including the rationally rounded density floors and gain percentages.
