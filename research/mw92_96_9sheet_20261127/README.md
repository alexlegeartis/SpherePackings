# Nine colours over an optimal D4 period: conditional 96D gain and unconditional tie

**Status: no new unrestricted density is proved.** There is a new exact periodic architecture with nine sites per vertical D4 cell, instead of the twelve colours of the former construction. It unconditionally matches the *unrefined* twelve-colour density, falling about 0.00030269% below the maintained density. It **would improve the maintained bound by 1.51336%** if one specified order-three torus depth can be certified. This note proves the reduction to that one condition; the condition is not assumed in the unconditional comparison.

## Proven horizontal inputs and comparators

Use the even [Shioda MW92 lattice](https://doi.org/10.2307/2374791) \(L\) of squared minimum 16 and covolume \(47^7\), and its orthogonal integral automorphism \(J\) satisfying \(J^2+J+I=0\). The [MW92 parent certificate](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/tree/main/research/mw92_extensions_20261122) audits these particular inputs. Write \(d(x)\) for torus distance to zero modulo \(L\).

The [published three-ball CFR certificate](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/tree/main/research/mw92_cfr_taylor13_20261123) supplies a point outside any three equal closed torus balls of squared radius \(10.74136270\). The [certified half-phase construction](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/tree/main/research/mw92_96_periodic12_20261124) supplies \(H=\{0,h,Jh,J^2h\}\) modulo \(L\), with all nonzero elements at squared depth at least \(21/2\), and a \(b\) such that every element of \(b+H\) has squared depth greater than \(106743/10000\). In the same source the unrefined twelve-colour tail has per-centre covolume \(231/16\). The current [refined twelve-colour tail](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/tree/a987f72ae7467f3db8a69390c604f92d099765b3/research/mw92_96_refined_tail_20261125) has squared per-centre covolume
\[
D_{\rm live}=\frac{300153807922100058481}{1440000000000000000}.
\]
The separately named historical lattice comparator is [Bos–Conway–Sloane's 96D lattice](https://neilsloane.com/doc/Me103.pdf), with centre density \(3^{48}/2^{24}\). No exhaustive worldwide-priority claim is made.

## Vertical mechanism

Write \(D_4=\{x\in\mathbb Z^4:\sum_i x_i\text{ even}\}\), with basis
\[
(1,-1,0,0),\quad(0,1,-1,0),\quad(0,0,1,-1),\quad(0,0,1,1).
\]
A residue in \(D_4/3D_4\) is denoted by its four basis coordinates modulo 3, packed as little-endian base 3. The plane \(W\) generated by tags 5 and 46 has all eight nonzero tags of exact minimum ordinary squared norm 6. One can obtain these two tags from the literal vectors \((2,-1,-1,0)\) and \((1,-1,0,2)\). Different integer representatives of a tag give the same vertical coset. The 81-class residue table has minimum-norm counts \(1,24,24,32\) at \(0,2,4,6\); the verifier checks it by enumerating all \(D_4\) vectors with squared norm at most 6. It also finds exactly eight such planes among the 130 two-planes; no coverage assumption on the other classes is needed.

Take the vertical period \(P=\sqrt8\,D_4\), of squared minimum 16 and covolume 128, and use its nine cosets \(\sqrt8\,w/3+P\), \(w\in W\). At every nonzero tag difference the vertical squared minimum is exactly \(8\cdot6/9=16/3\), so a horizontal alphabet with all pairwise squared depths at least \(32/3\) is sufficient. The pure period plus horizontal \(L\) makes the construction finite-periodic and checks all lattice translates, not just representative pairs.

## Conditional +1.51336% construction

Suppose there is a torus class \(u\) with \(Ju=u\) and \(d(u)^2\ge32/3\). It is nonzero and has exact order three, since \((I-J^2)(I-J)=3I\). Choose \(b\) outside the three closed equal balls around \(0,u,-u\) of squared radius \(10.74136270\), and choose it not to be 3-torsion (the complement is open and nonempty). The nine horizontal labels are the Minkowski sum
\[
\{0,u,-u\}+\{0,b,-Jb\}.
\]
Differences in one group have depth \(d(u)\); cross-group differences are, up to a sign and power of \(J\), one of \(b,b-u,b+u\). Thus the constructed packing has squared minimum exactly 16, attained within \(L\). It is not a lattice: tripling a point labelled by \(b\) returns the normal coordinate to \(P\), but horizontal offset \(3b\) is not in \(L\).

Its per-centre vertical covolume is \(128/9\), and its occupied density is
\[
\frac{\pi^{48}9\,2^{89}}{48!\,47^7}
=6.46416181138194\ldots\times10^{-22}.
\]
The exact gain ratio over the maintained construction is \(9\sqrt{D_{\rm live}}/128=1.015133646\ldots\). This comparison is **conditional**. In particular this cannot be entered in a lower-bound table without the depth hypothesis.

## Unconditional exact tie with the old twelve-colour value

Take the same nine-coordinate geometry and use \(P\) stretched along the two-plane \(U=\{(x_1,x_2,x_3,0):x_1+x_2+x_3=0\}\) by squared factor \(a=33/32\), and in \(U^\perp\) by squared factor \(c=63/64\). Distances in ordinary coordinates are now measured by \(8(aP_U+cP_{U^\perp})\), using representatives \(w/3\). The 24 ordinary D4 roots remain at squared length at least 16; longer pure period vectors remain longer. Every shortest representative of the two nonzero tags on line 5 lies in \(U\), so their vertical squared minimum is \(11/2\). The shortest representatives of the other six nonzero tags have original squared projections 2 and 4 in \(U\) and \(U^\perp\), and vertical squared minimum \(16/3\). Any representative of ordinary squared norm at least 8 has vertical squared length at least 7 by the smallest scale, so no further case is missing.

Assign the coordinate along line 5 the horizontal labels \(0,h,Jh\), and the other coordinate the labels \(0,b,-Jb\) from the certified half-phase/four-ball construction. Within a ternary group horizontal squared depth is at least \(21/2\), and across groups greater than \(106743/10000>32/3\). Thus this is an unconditional periodic packing of exact squared minimum 16. It is not a lattice: tripling an element labelled by \(h\) gives a normal point in the period, but horizontal shift \(3h=h\notin L\). Its vertical covolume per point is \(128ac/9=231/16\); this is the old published value, slightly worse than the refined comparator, not a record.

## Precisely what must still be checked

Let \(T=I-J\). Then \(T^TT=3I\) and \(T^{-1}L/L\) has \(3^{46}\) elements, precisely the fixed classes for \(J\). A class represented by \(T^{-1}w\) has depth at least \(32/3\) exactly if its class \(w+TL\) has **no vector of squared norm at most 30** (the squared norms in \(L\) are even). A quantitative alternative sufficient check is that the number of vectors of all norms 16 through 30 is less than \(3(3^{46}-1)\): a represented nonzero class contributes at least a full three-element \(J\)-orbit, since \(J\equiv I\pmod{TL}\), and \(J\) has no nonzero fixed real vector. Neither check is completed here.

The [94D fixed branch](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/tree/main/research/mw92_paired_cover_9495_20261126) would give an even deeper class, \(34/3\), if it occurs, but the branch is not known to occur. A tempting candidate from the two displayed orthogonal Eisenstein lines actually fails: [the cubic descent calculation](CUBIC_DESCENT.md) proves that all eight nonzero classes in their plane have squared depth \(16/3\), not the required \(32/3\). No other class is excluded.
