# Cubic descent: the two evident Eisenstein directions are shallow

The tempting explicit coset \((P+Q)/(1-J)\) does **not** satisfy the depth required by the main conditional construction. In fact the entire order-nine subgroup generated by these two directions has squared depth \(16/3\) on all eight nonzero elements, although the literal representatives \(P+Q\) and \(P-Q\) both have squared norm 32 before reduction. The existence of a different deep order-three class is open in this note.

Use the [same exact MW92 model](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/tree/main/research/mw92_cusp_d4_20261122) \(E:y^2=x^3+F,\ F=t^{48}+1\), over the geometric function field in characteristic 47. Let \(s^2=F\), \(J(x,y)=(\omega x,y)\), and \(T=I-J\). The cubic class map \(\kappa(x,y)=[y+s]\) is a homomorphism into nonzero elements of the quadratic extension modulo cubes. Indeed, intersecting the line \(y=mx+b\) with \(E\), the three factors \(y_i+s\) have product \((b+s)^3\), by evaluating the monic cubic in \(x\) at \(-(b+s)/m\). This also holds for repeated points and horizontal lines; vertical lines are handled by \((y+s)(-y+s)=-x^3\) and the identity at infinity. Since \(J\) fixes \(y\), \(\kappa\) vanishes on \(TE\).

For completeness the induced map on \(E/TE\) is injective. If \(y+s=(A+Bs)^3\), adjust the cube root by a cube root of unity so that \(x=A^2-FB^2\), using the product of the conjugates. The coefficient of \(s\) gives \(B(3A^2+FB^2)=1\). The rational point
\[
V=\left(-1/B,\ (1+2\omega)A/B\right)
\]
therefore lies on \(E\) (use \((1+2\omega)^2=-3\)). The addition formula gives \(x(TV)=-\omega^2(x(V)^3+4F)/(3x(V)^2)=\omega^2 x\). Thus \(TV=\pm J^2(x,y)\); by applying the inverse isometry, \((x,y)\in TE\). The identity is immediate for the point at infinity. These arguments apply to the Mordell–Weil lattice since the norm computation and parent identification are taken over the same geometric function field.

Let \(P=(-1,t^{24})\), \(Q=(-t^{16},1)\), \(f=t^{24}\), and \(g=s+f\). Then \(g^{-1}=s-f\), \(s=(g+g^{-1})/2\), and \(s+1=(g+1)^2/(2g)\). In the cubic-class group:
\[
\kappa(P+Q)=[g(s+1)]=[(g+1)^{-1}],\qquad
\kappa(P-Q)=[g/(s+1)]=[(g+1)/g]=[s-f+1].
\]
The first equality on each line drops a multiplicative cube in the algebraically closed constant field. The class of \(P+Q\) is therefore represented by
\[
R_+=(21t^8,\ -t^{24}-1),
\]
since \((y(R_+)+s)(g+1)=-2t^{24}\) is a cube. The class of \(P-Q\) is represented by
\[
R_-=(-21t^8,\ 1-t^{24}),
\]
whose \(y+s=s-f+1\) literally. Since \(21^3=2\) in \(\mathbb F_{47}\), the displayed points satisfy the curve equation directly. Both points have squared norm 16 in the MW92 Gram: they are integral at finite \(t\) and, under \(x\mapsto t^{-16}x,\ y\mapsto t^{-24}y\) at infinity, extend to finite affine points, hence do not meet the zero section. The [height formula for this model](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/tree/main/research/mw92_cusp_d4_20261122) is \(16+2(P\cdot O)\).

The two classes \(P,Q\bmod TL\) are independent: the four possible nonzero directions \(P,Q,P+Q,P-Q\) have the displayed norm-16 representatives, none in \(TL\) because its squared minimum is \(3\cdot16=48\). With negations these are the eight nonzero classes in their plane. Consequently their least representative norms are exactly 16, and their squared \(T^{-1}\)-torus depths are exactly \(16/3\). This does not count or bound the remaining order-three classes.
