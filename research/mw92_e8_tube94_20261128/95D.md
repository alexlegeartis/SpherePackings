# Companion result: same tube cover improves the 95D lattice again

This is a distinct corollary of the 94D tube proof. The [cross-phase 95D predecessor](../mw92_cross_phase_cover95_20261128/) used a dimer in exactly one demanding weak phase, and its fixed-order first two shears still used the old 94D paired-cover value. Apply the new eight-dimensional tube cover in **both** places. The resulting 95D lattice has occupied fraction at least **2.13767740764968e−21**, more than **0.09975%** above that exact predecessor, **2.13554696184854e−21**. The shears remain existential; both the lattice-only and unrestricted lower bounds improve. No other dimensions or optimality claims are implied.

## Fixed-order branch with a tighter 94D base and fewer balls

Use all horizontal notation, source results and generic branch from the cross-phase 95D predecessor. The [tube cover on a literal scaled \(E_8\) sublattice](README.md) supplies weak-phase replacement rate \(\beta<1703/4000\) whenever the larger minus smaller **squared** radii differ by \(2p=28/3\). In its improved 94D fixed-order branch it gives the first two shears \(u,b\) with
\[
 p=14/3,\quad B=10.79109,\quad q=16-B,\qquad
 d(b)^2,d(b-u)^2>B,\quad d(b+u)^2>B-2p.
\]
As before, choose \(b\) within its open feasible component near the first boundary met on a path to 0. Some one of the three distances is within \(10^{-5}\) **in squared units** above its threshold; all three are still strict. The exact three cross-phase pairings therefore remain available.

Use the same third-generator construction with fixed-order vertical Gram
\[
 G_f=\begin{pmatrix}p&p/2&p/2\\p/2&q&q/2\\p/2&q/2&16-D_f\end{pmatrix},\qquad D_f=10.55715.
\]
The forbidden third-level demand list has unchanged groups and labels. The verifier rechecks the whole list, all longer-vector bounds, both unlike-radius merging conditions with the new \(B,q,D_f\), and the radius fit for the two remaining tiny phases handled by nine-site simplices. The only former dimer phase is at label \((1,0)\): it needs squared radius \(D_f-2p\). Thus instead of rate \(1/2\), replace the entire weak coset at this phase by the 8D tubes with rate below \(1703/4000\). This is the identical periodic construction from the 94D proof, independent of the absolute value of \(D_f\) so long as the difference in **squared radii** is \(2p\). Intersect the finite-index periods of all replacements. The covering rate is at most
\[
 N_f=6+1703/4000+2/9,\qquad N_f D_f^{46}<11.00098526^{46}.
\]
Consequently the ordinary equal-ball Coxeter–Few–Rogers bound guarantees an acceptable third shear.
