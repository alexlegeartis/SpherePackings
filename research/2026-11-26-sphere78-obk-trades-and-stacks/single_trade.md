# Sphere78: no real one-to-two augmentation of the 96 classified 11-dimensional binary optima
Primary public source: P. R. J. Östergård, T. Baicheva, E. Kolev, Optimal binary one-error-correcting codes of length 10 have 72 codewords, IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 45 (1999), 1229–1231. Authors' supplementary page https://users.aalto.fi/~pat/72.html and file https://users.aalto.fi/~pat/opt/72_4.txt list 96 (11,72,4) codes. Completeness up to binary coordinate isometries is the classification input, not reproved. The supplied coordinate checks cover all 96 listed words sets without needing classification to be complete. SHA-256 of the authors' file after whitespace normalization to one decimal integer and LF per line: 0f3fe74e631f11d3d3263c1c476d86ad142298ed6e2184f2b06370c771519d23. For 96 rows of 72 words separated by ASCII spaces and each row terminated by LF: d43da5806c9185fc2cbbbb82bb5965929aeb8abc9ab0b00569141a724a07acbc.
Take period 2Z^11, minimum point distance 2. OBK codes give center density 72/2048=9/256; target is 73/2048 (+1/72). For any such code C and c∈C, when C without c is frozen, at most one arbitrary real site can be added per 2Z^11 cell. In 6396/6912 cases this same intensity upper bound applies to arbitrary nonperiodic refilling while preserving the translates of the 71-site core. No global optimality or arbitrary-period extension in the other cases. No assertion that removing different sites in different cells is excluded.

Proof. Represent y modulo 2 by its nearest binary v and magnitudes x_i/2 ∈[0,1/2] of residuals; ties may be resolved arbitrarily. To binary u, four times the torus squared distance is Σ_i(x_i−2·1_{v_i≠u_i})², independent of residual signs. On every dyadic box each term is bounded above by its endpoint secant (a rational affine expression). For the 72 or 71 frozen-site lower constraints, a nonnegative integer combination whose maximum on the box falls strictly below 16 times its total weight proves emptiness. Coordinate extrema of an affine function are attained exactly by taking positive slopes at the upper endpoint and negative at the lower. Floating-point simplex only proposes weights; every exclusion and every accepted witness uses integer arithmetic. If a strict combination excludes a rounding cell for the full code, dropping a center outside its support cannot change the exclusion. Otherwise test the punctured stock, and if needed refine boxes. Exhaustion: 1,860,332 initial LP proposals, 8,861 relaxed survivors including 22 full-code survivors, 953 final exclusions and 7,908 actually feasible corners. Every feasible corner is itself a legal binary site. Numbers of admissible corners after a deletion: 1 (6396 cases), 2 (324), 3 (160), 12 (32). No whole-code extra site survives.

To exclude TWO unrestricted replacements, let v and w be two admitted binary corners, possibly equal; k=wt(v xor w) and J their agreeing coordinates. Per differing coordinate the maximum torus distance is 1. Across the agreeing coordinates the distance is at most (||x_J||+||x'_J||)/2 by Minkowski. Exhaustively prove, for every relevant directed (v,w) and every feasible y rounding to v, that Σ_{i∈J}x_i² < 4−k. To do this, append the reverse inequality to the other constraints; its LHS also has a rational affine upper secant. The preceding box/weighted-mixture test strictly excludes every case. 13,740 directed conditions (after deduplication), 19,180 node visits, zero unresolved. Thus squared separation <k+(4−k)=4. In the singleton-corner cases this proves the actual Euclidean radius of each hole component about the physical rounded corner is strictly below 1. There is only one such physical corner every 2^11 units of volume, so nonperiodic refilling also gives at most one site per component by the triangle inequality. For several inequivalent rounding corners the torus distance bound is deliberately asserted only in the common 2Z^11 cell.

The single-file Python/C++17/OpenMP replay is [verify_single_trade.py](verify_single_trade.py). It validates the canonical original dataset hash, distance and corner counts, and complete coverage of all real-pair checks. This result is a scoped tool for prioritizing actual 2-to-3 and larger trades, not a density gain.
