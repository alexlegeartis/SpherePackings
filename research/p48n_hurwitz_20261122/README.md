| Dimension | Occupied density at least | Increase over previous repository bound | Gram determinant |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 51 | **9.34489307985541e-10** | **17.2603%** | 4 |

| 53 | **1.66176494990127e-10** | **11.0281%** | 4 |

These lattices have squared minimum 6. Their center-density squares are exactly \((3/2)^n/4\). The comparisons use the exact [preceding P48q extensions](../adaptive_layers_20261115/P48_PROOF.md) in 51–52D and the [Leterrier54 section](../adaptive_layers_20261115/SECTION53_PROOF.md) in 53D. ## Construction and proof

Take the even unimodular 48D lattice \(P_{48n}\) of squared minimum 6 from Nebe's [catalogue entry CQ48a](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/CQ48a.html). The construction uses the published minimum. The [original catalogue snapshot](../density_breakthroughs_20261113/seed_deformations/sources/CQ48a.html) supplies its exact Gram \(G\).

The certificate gives integral quaternion operators \(I,U,X,Y\), where \(X^2=Y^2=-I\), \(XY=-YX\) and \(2U=I+X+Y+XY\). It also gives a binary seed \(w\) in a specified reduced basis. A complete enumeration excludes all vectors of squared norm at most 12 in \(w+2P_{48n}\). Their norms are multiples of four, so the corresponding horizontal half-coset has squared depth at least 4.

Equip the 53D construction’s vertical space with Gram
\[
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
2&1&0&0&-1\\
1&2&1&1&0\\
0&1&2&0&0\\
0&1&0&2&0\\
-1&0&0&0&2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Use the leading rank-three and rank-four blocks for 51D and 52D. The first four horizontal numerator rows are the images of \(w\) under \(I,U,X,Y\). The fifth row comes from the extra binary residue in the certificate. The verifier enumerates all 40 roots of \(M\). For the 24 roots supported in the first four coordinates, it checks that the associated quaternion operators are integral isometries of \(G\). These root parities therefore inherit depth 4 from the seed.

The other 16 roots give eight parities. Their horizontal numerator classes lie in five orbits under the signed quaternion group generated by \(X,Y\). The verifier checks those orbit identifications modulo \(2P_{48n}\) and repeats the complete norm-12 exclusion for a representative of each orbit. Every root parity therefore has depth at least 4.

All 31 nonzero binary combinations of the numerator rows give distinct nonzero classes with norms divisible by four. A nonroot parity has vertical squared norm at least 4, and the parent minimum gives horizontal depth at least 2. All-even nonzero vertical coefficients give squared norm at least 8. These cases give squared minimum 6, which occurs in the embedded parent.

The exact numerator rows \(B\) are in [results.json](results.json). In a lattice basis with the 48 parent coordinates first, the Gram is
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
G & GB^T/2 \\
BG/2 & BGB^T/4+M
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its determinant is 4 in all three dimensions. The verifier checks the unimodular basis change, the exact determinants and the outward density rounding.

## Replay

Install python-flint and SymPy. Run `python research/p48n_hurwitz_20261122/verify.py` to verify the exact setup, archived enumeration reports and comparisons. Add `--replay` to compile and rerun all six coset exclusions and the short-vector control. The full replay uses GNU C++ with IEEE-754 binary64 and the existing outward-rounded [interval enumerator](../covering_and_deep_cosets_20261115/explicit_p48/interval_enum.cpp). Every leaf norm is checked with integers.
