# Sphere71: continuous independent motion of both QR18 banks at squared minimum 24

For the same horizontally oriented even and odd banks E,O of Bierbrauer–Edel as in the [full-layer proof](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/commit/88f60041aa233dce62faedc639125bc43f80eed4), allow *each of them* an independent real horizontal translation at each height. With squared minimum 24, the upper 19D center density is at most 0.085863579, strictly below Chen–Hu–Li–Wang–Wu's exact 0.088958102129… . No restriction on period, choices of directions or successive gaps. This is an architecture bound at inherited minimum, not a record and not an all-radius or all-orientation statement.

1. Directional mixed cloud. In the notation of the pinned proof, E−O itself (not merely its union with the opposite sign) contains every σ+4z with σ having even minus parity and z odd sum: every allowed residue is attained by C+C' and the wrong sheet would produce norm 18; 4D is the period. This cloud equals its negative. Also 2·1 is a period of E and O. For arbitrary s write s=2y+4b+w, y binary, |w_i|≤1. Its squared distance to the mixed cloud is at most 8+Σ(1−|w_i|)² by the pinned sign-and-sheet lemma. Thus if this mixed distance is ≥√24, 2||w||₁−||w||²≤2, in particular ||w||²≤2.

2. Rigidity of the possible relative displacement. The 2^18 integer vertices neighboring 2y+4b in the odd cube are 2y+4b+ε, ε_i=±1. Writing ε_i=(-1)^{e_i}, the residue sign σ has minus support e+y; the sheet parity is Σ b_i+Σ y_i(1−e_i) modulo two. Hence the cloud constraints fix independently the parities of the selected signs in supp(y) and its complement if both are nonempty. Condition a uniform ε on those two parities. All signs are balanced except potentially the one in a singleton block. Mean squared cost ≤18+||w||²+2||w||∞≤22, so 0<wt(y)<18 is impossible for a displacement of mixed depth √24. For y=0 or 1, reduce by the shared period 2·1 if needed. With odd Σb the odd cube contains all even-minus signs, mean squared cost ≤20. Therefore s=w modulo a genuine common period (Σb even).

For every j, the mixed cloud contains all vectors with one coordinate of magnitude 3 at j, others of magnitude 1, and odd total minus parity (flip the sign at j to obtain the original even-minus σ). Put L=||w||₁, M=||w||∞, d=||w||₂. Select j of maximum size and align signs, changing one other minimum coordinate if necessary. The scalar product is at least L+2M−2 min |w_i| ≥ (8/9)L+2M ≥ (8/3)√(LM) ≥ (8/3)d. The nearest such norm-26 vector then gives 24≤26+d²−(16/3)d. Since d≤√2, d≤(8−√46)/3 = .405890005625.

3. Strengthened covering dichotomy. For every real s one has dist(s,E−E)≤√18 or dist(s,E−O)≤4. The single-code four-cover certificate in the pinned verifier has 344 bad words for Q; these are exactly weights 0,1,2,16,17,18 (for all 153 weight-two words, running four(sorted([c^y for c in Q if c not in (0,M)], key=(weight,mask))) returns None; weights 0,1 follow from minimum weight; use complementation). Outside those weights the single-code averaging itself bounds dist²(s,E−E)≤18. For the endpoints of weight 0,1 the published endpoint algebra uses only 4D and gives mixed squared distance ≤16 whenever the even bound exceeds 18. For weight two reduce as there by the common complement and write sorted high residues 1+b,1+c with 0≤c≤b≤1; the other sixteen residues a_i≤1 and T=(1+b)²+(1+c)²+Σa_i². If E−E distance exceeds √18 the 4D repair implies T+8−8b>18, hence Σa_i²>8+6b−2c−b²−c². Mixed cost is ≤24+b²+c²−Σ(2a_i−a_i²) <16−6b+2c+2b²+2c²≤16.

4. For two arbitrary layers align notation by their even banks, with displacement s between them. If even-even distance exceeds √18 use 3 and triangle inequality on the directed cross pair between the even bank of the first layer and odd bank of the second: its distance is at most 4+d. Point 2 bounds the latter bank displacement, so all gaps are at least H=√(24−[4+(8−√46)/3]²)=2.14199282406…. Counting layers at this spacing gives δ₁₉≤(3/4)^9 √6/H=0.08586357845…. Hexagonal stock rotations, altered codeword banks, and reducing in-layer minimum are not addressed.
