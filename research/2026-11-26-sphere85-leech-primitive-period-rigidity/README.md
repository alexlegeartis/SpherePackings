# Strict primitive-period local density maxima in two explicit Leech antipode packings

**Scope.** These are explicit Golay-coordinate realizations of the 20D and 21D Gram-matrix constructions of [Chen, Hu, Li, Wang and Wu, §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02394v1#S3), at their published center densities. We prove a local obstruction to simultaneous changes of the period metric and of every motif center, with the number of sites per period fixed. This is not a global bound, a result about larger supercells, or a new density record; no uniqueness of the embedding of the normal lattice is assumed.

**Construction.** Use the exhibited integer coordinates for the 196,560 minimal Leech vectors, divided by sqrt(8), in `verify.py`. The indicated rank k=4 or 3 Leech roots W have Gram G=W W^t/8, determinant D=125 or 36; let J=adj(G). Let E be their orthogonal complement of dimension d=24-k and L the full Leech section in E. The code verifies that each coordinate unit vector is a pairing profile in the Leech lattice. Thus the map to Z^k given by pairing with the normal roots is onto, the normal span is primitive, and covol(L)=sqrt(D) by unimodularity. Place k+1 translates of L at the projections of the fibers with profiles 0,e_1,...,e_k. For any difference of marked profiles q, its normal lift has squared length q J q^t/D. The maximum beta over these differences is 2/5 (20D) or 5/12 (21D), so every projected difference has squared length at least mu=4-beta. The density is (k+1)(mu/4)^(d/2)/sqrt(D).

**Literal contacts.** Equality occurs on every pair attaining beta, because norm-four Leech roots with exactly that pairing profile exist. At 20D all ten pairs attain beta, and every pair has 4,750 such directed contacts for its chosen orientation. At 21D the four active pairs 01,02,13,23 have 9,072 each. There are no other contacts at this radius: a difference in the horizontal section has squared length at least four, and a vector of parent squared norm at least six is too long for any chosen profile. These counts are per oriented pair, not the kissing number.

For each such contact shell V_ij of true horizontal displacements in E, the exact integer identities in the verifier give sum(v)=0 and sum(v v^t)=|V_ij| mu I_E/d. Moreover on the first active pair the evaluation rows of (v_a v_b, v_a) with a≤b (and all linear coordinates) span dimension d(d+3)/2, exactly 230 in 20D and 252 in 21D. The verifier gives a full rank minor modulo 65521 via Gaussian elimination on deterministic rows. Any d independent ambient coordinates restricted to E suffice, since changing linear coordinates induces invertible transformations on linear and symmetric quadratic forms.

**Strict local maximum.** Normalize a small perturbation to the old minimum mu by uniform scaling; mod out overall rotations, translations, and similarities. In fixed material coordinates its variables are a positive definite metric I+H on E and shifts u_i of each motif site in that period, with u_0=0. If a sequence of genuinely different perturbations with density at least the original approached it, then det(I+H)≤1. Divide (H,u_i) by their joint norm, pass to a convergent subsequence, and denote the unit limit by (h,t_i). The determinant gives tr h≤0. The original contact displacements remain admissible, requiring
  v^t h v + 2 v·(t_i-t_j) ≥ 0
for every v in each active V_ij. Summing over any such shell eliminates the translation term and gives a positive multiple of tr h, so tr h=0 and all inequalities in every active shell are equalities. On the full-rank first shell this forces h=0 and t_i=t_j there. On every remaining active pair the second moment is positive definite, so the shell spans E and its equality forces t_i=t_j. Both active graphs are connected. Thus all t_i=0, contradicting the unit normalization. Consequently the packings are strict local density maxima in this fixed-cell model. A better construction from these carriers must leave this neighborhood or change the number of marks per primitive cell.

**Replay.** Run `python verify.py` with NumPy and SymPy. All verification uses integers, including the four/equivalently ten moment identities and the derivative ranks. The modulus is prime.
