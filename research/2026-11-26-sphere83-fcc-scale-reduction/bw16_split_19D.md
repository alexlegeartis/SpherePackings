# 19D independent constructive reserve: an index-two split of the Barnes–Wall binary carrier
At squared minimum eight and period H_U⊕4Z³, choose U a four-dimensional subspace of RM(1,4) and H_U=⋃_{u∈U}(u+2D16) (binary u written as 0/1 integer coordinates). All nonzero U words have weight at least eight. All intersections of two affine functions on F2^4 have even size, so H_U is a lattice. Its covolume is 2^17/16=8192. A 19D motif with 65 sites at squared minimum 8 would give 65√2/1024=0.089771…, beating Chen–Hu–Li–Wang–Wu, https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02394v1#S3 . A 64-site motif gives √2/16=0.0883883476483, not an improvement. The 3D exterior and 16D horizontal phases in this construction are co-designed, rather than layering another full 18D QR or 12D Coxeter–Todd stock.

For integral horizontal phases modulo H_U, label a phase by its binary mask v modulo U (choose smallest XOR representative) and a sheet bit. For distinct binary mask cosets the squared distance is exactly d_H(v⊕w,U): if the nearest representative contains an odd coordinate then choosing its two nearest signs incurs the same cost and can repair even-translation parity for free. For the two sheets of the same binary coset the squared distance is four (shift 2e₀); for equal phases zero. Adding masks x,y adds carry parity weight(x&y) mod 2, then reducing x XOR y by XOR with u∈U adds carry parity weight(u\(x XOR y)) mod 2. The two types of U used: (0) all affine functions of three bits repeated on a fourth bit, (1) all homogeneous linear functions of four bits. For each exterior p=(p₁,p₂,p₃)∈(Z4)³, the pure exterior distances are sums of squares of the three ordinary coordinatewise wrapped differences. Two distinct sites must have the sum of the horizontal and exterior squared distances at least eight. Different horizontal phases at one outer p are explicitly permitted.

Why linear integral labels can never win even with an arbitrary transverse basis/metric: take one H_U coset above each point of a rank-three Bravais lattice L and a homomorphism L→Z¹⁶/H_U. Twice ANY integral phase belongs to the common 2-element subgroup {0,[2e0]} (parity of the sum of its representative entries). The image has size ≤16, since the mod-doubling part has at most 8 elements on three generators. Thus its kernel is a 3D lattice of index at most 16 in L with squared minimum at least 8. By the 3D lattice-packing optimum it has covolume ≥16 (2D3 attains it). Consequently covol(L)≥1 and the 19D center density is ≤√2/16. This does not cover nonlinear assignments, repeated sites at one projection, arbitrary nonintegral phases, or a different fiber.
 
A full linear equality fixture exists for type (1): choose three odd integral binary phases g_i of min H-distance from U at least seven, each of their pairwise XORs of distance at least six, the triple XOR of distance at least five. One may use the image ∑p_i g_i above each of the 64 exterior p; the C++ replay verifies all 2016 site pairs and writes the marks. Generic construction was applied to type (1) plus nonlinear trades via whole-bank isometric intersections, simultaneous removal of blockers and joint independent insertions. The actual sampled totals were 4,422 successful independently built templates, 13,417 rounds, 2,260,189,088 candidate comparisons to a template (probes, not distinct exhaustive configurations), 66,773 bipartite combinations and 1,201,400 trade proposals. Best sampled 64, threshold 65, so no gain. Type (0) yielded 128 deep odd classes but zero template triples in the sampler; DO NOT claim that this random miss proves their impossibility. First 3-generator trial only searched the stated sufficient inequalities. No global nonlinear impossibility claim.
