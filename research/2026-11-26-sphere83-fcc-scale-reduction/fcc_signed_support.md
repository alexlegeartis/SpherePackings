# A sharp product-plane reference for FCC–triality packings in 11 dimensions
Set F=D3/√2, period 2F⊕2D4⊕2D4, with centers in F⊕D4*⊕D4* and squared minimum 4. The outer group F/2F is indexed by three bits, with 6 unit squared separations, and one nonzero class 111 (7) of squared separation 2. Inner labels are q,r in D4*/2D4; wrapped squared distance is the sum of the two D4 costs. Coordinates and distance computation are in the independent verifier. The comparison is 9/256; the present full-cell volume 8192/√2 needs at least 204 sites. No new density asserted.
 
## Exact projection and structural threshold
Every full packing projects injectively to its inner 8-dimensional support U (two sites with the same inner point cannot be separated by 4 even with the most distant prefixes). Distinct inner points at squared distance <2 cannot occur. Form a graph by joining pairs in U at distance 2. Every edge requires outer labels to differ by 7, so each connected component must be bipartite, with labels given by an ordered complementary pair. There are four unordered complementary pairs of labels. Assign to each component one of those four IDs and choose an orientation. Each inner squared-distance-3 pair must receive different labels. In one component a distance-3 pair in the same bipartite half is therefore immediately impossible. Between different components such a pair only restricts relative orientation if IDs coincide. There are no conditions on pairs of inner distance at least 4. Conversely, these conditions construct actual prefixes and are sufficient. In particular fixed site-by-site outer-label failures need not forbid an inner configuration after global relabeling.

A product affine Lagrangian plane of the two D4 discriminant colors is a pair of independently selected integral charts (one of three triality-rotated Z4s in each, up to translation), by the D4 gluing description from the double-triality paper. Partition the eight prefixes using any transversal of the four complementary pairs and its complement. In either half, any two inner sites have squared distance at least 3. All points restricted to one product plane lie in a translate of rotated Z8. Tile Z8 by disjoint elementary binary cubes (two integer values per coordinate). Each cube contains a binary length-eight distance-three code, hence at most 20 sites by A(8,3)=20. In the quotient one half has at most 4·20 points: D4² of periods 2D4⊕2D4 has volume 1024, or four 256-site cubes. Equivalently average over large boxes, avoiding any boundary choice. Sum the two halves: **at most 160 full-period marks** can lie in the product plane.

On a diagonal affine Lagrangian the inner 8D cosets glue to E8; since its norms are even, each half now has squared minimum at least 4. Viazovska's optimal 8D density implies at most 1024/16=64 points in its cell, hence at most 128 points across both halves. Thus a winning 204 must have **at least 44 marks outside every product Lagrangian and at least 76 outside every diagonal Lagrangian**, regardless of incomplete central fibers. The additional central-fiber constraint from round 82 still applies independently.

## Constructive sharpness of the 160
Take the 40 cyclic shifts of the four 10-bit words 0100000011, 0011111101, 1100101100, 0001010111, a representation of Best's binary (10,40,4) code. The verifier independently checks cardinality and minimum distance. Puncture the high bit, writing the remaining nine bits as x in F2^8 and c in F2. Assign prefix p=(weight(x) mod 2) XOR (7c), interpreting weight parity as the integer 0 or 1. For two words the inner Hamming distance is at least 2. If it is 2, the c bits differ (punctured code minimum 3) and the parities of x agree, so the two prefixes are complementary. If it is 3 the parities of x differ, so the prefixes are distinct regardless of c. This yields 40 sites in a product chart of the small period. Lift the two central bits independently to the full 2D4² period, obtaining 160 verified marks, attaining the exact product-plane cap. This beats the preceding sampled FCC motif cardinality 148, not the sphere-packing record or even the 204-site threshold.
