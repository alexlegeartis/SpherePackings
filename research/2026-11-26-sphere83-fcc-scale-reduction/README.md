# A single exact 204-site target for the three-scale FCC × D4 × D4 family

Outer period 2F, F=D3/√2; inner periods 2D4 twice and candidate inner positions D4* twice. Scale the three squared metrics independently by α,a,b, with α≥1 (2F periods), a,b≥1/2 (2D4 periods). These thresholds are necessary and sufficient for pure periods to respect advertised squared minimum 4. Code labels form an abelian group of order 32768. An outer difference has squared cost k in {0,1,2}; the two D4 differences have costs i,j in {0,...,4}, per the companion geometry. The full distance test on distinct sites is kα+ia+jb≥4 for all their actual difference types. At min²4 the center density is N√2/(8192 α^(3/2)a²b²). To beat 9/256 one needs 2N²>288² α³a⁴b⁴.

**Exact reduction.** If any packing on this three-parameter carrier beats 9/256, then a 204-site packing exists at α=a=b=1. Conversely any such 204-site packing improves the record in this carrier. This does not exclude that such a packing exists, and concerns no other carrier.

For a given motif clamp all three scales at 4, because any inequality affected by a clamped coordinate has its coefficient an integer ≥1 and that coordinate alone suffices. Its objective cannot get worse. The bounded feasible polytope is defined by the lower and upper bounds and the occupied-type inequalities only. Minimizing (3/2)log α+2 log a+2 log b over this polytope is attained at a vertex by concavity (express any point as a convex combination). Thus it suffices to list intersections of three linearly independent support planes from the six box planes and all 74 nonzero difference-type planes. Swapping a,b is a symmetry and is performed **after** enumeration, without adding a fake a=b plane. Exact integer determinants give 592 rational triples in the box up to block symmetry. Some are deliberate superset vertices irrelevant to a given motif. The standalone script rederives the entire set with fractions and checks the set equality to the certificates.

**Exact Fourier bound.** Let P_{r,u} be the mean of characters in dual block r evaluated at any primal difference of type u; independence of representative verified directly in both groups. Tail characters are exp(2πi X(k)·X(q)/8), where X denotes the doubled 4D coordinates. The dual of D4*/2D4 is identified with (D4*/2)/D4, giving all 64 such characters, of order dividing four. The three dual and primal outer classes are separately enumerated as in the script from binary characters. Products give 75 types including the trivial character r=0 and the origin u=0. The script checks by integer arithmetic that ∑_{r≠0} m_r P_{r,u}=−1 away from the origin, with m_r block sizes. Equivalently this is character orthogonality. With symmetric mean ordered per-site difference counts A_u, ∑_{u≠0}A_u=N−1, positivity gives 1+∑P_{r,u} A_u ≥0. If y_r≥0 and ∑y_r P_{r,u}≤−1 for every nonzero allowed difference, then N≤1+∑y_r. A floating LP only produced trial y, rounded to integers with a fixed denominator. The maximum remaining excess is computed EXACTLY for each allowed difference and this excess times m_r is added to every coefficient; orthogonality proves exact feasibility simultaneously. All inequalities are checked by the replay without SciPy or floating point. Of 592 triples 590 immediately have integer upper below their target.

**Odd-size refinement and the last two vertices.** Every character takes Gaussian unit values in this exponent-four group. For odd N, a sum of N such units has odd real-plus-imaginary parity, hence squared norm at least one, not merely nonnegative. Consequently the normalized RHS improves from −1 to −(1−1/N) for every r. For the same dual this gives N−1≤(1−1/N)∑y_r, hence N≤∑y_r=U−1 for the previous ordinary upper U. At (α,a,b)=(1,1/2,1) a win would require 51; its ordinary dual upper 11520032767/225000000<51.201 and odd upper <50.201 exclude it. At unscaled (1,1,1) one needs 204 and ordinary upper is 1024/5=204.8. Thus precisely this one count and one parameter choice remain necessary (for a win elsewhere a winning motif may be moved to the unscaled vertex). Not all possible successful metrics themselves are claimed to be exactly unscaled.
