# Sphere 93 extension: quarter-grid A2 triangle obstruction (26 Nov 2026)

For fixed base O, suppose 2Q=B+e with e an integral syndrome, where B is the old nonintegral order-two generator (using C instead gives the same family because B+C=911). The old cheap 2-torsion plane still forces e in 1496+I. The two distinct e values to check are 1496 and 1623 since adding A to Q toggles 4096. All three nonzero doubled parities are ordinary. Corresponding D4 raw root pairs form A2, so some old X in A+G[2] and two new points Y, Y±X have H>7.25 if t²<1.5. Every old candidate with H>7.25 has horizontal fractional mask 82 in each of the five blocks by the exact old/e table (6.5 vs 7.5). Thus Y and Y±X have paired shape vectors R, R xor (82,...,82) in coordinates 4Y=2B+2R+4k. The exact local squared minimum for a shape is in units of 16H. Evenness means >116 requires ≥118.

Let m_i denote a block's cheapest cost. Any two GF64 columns i,j uniquely correct all field components; radical parity remains shared. Sort each field label's two possible costs (l,h) by size. Thus the maximum over all syndromes of exact two-block repair cost is bounded by max_{(l,h),(p,q)} min(l+q,h+p) (either aligned or opposed low bits, with aligned sum l+p never worse). Leave remaining three blocks at their local minima, so an upper bound U for any charge is M+min_{i<j}(W_ij-m_i-m_j), M=sum m. Direct orbit type enumeration produces only (M,repair,complement M,repair)=(56,64,56,64) when both U values are at least 118. The first two local m_i + m'_i ≤24, the third and fourth sums are exactly 20 and fifth exactly 24; hence achieving both 56 forces both first-two sums 24. For each e a complete recurrence over 4,194,304 shapes with these checks leaves exactly 195,392. Now last-three baseline is 32. The first-two exactly achievable cost P at the residual syndrome must therefore be ≥86 (P actually takes multiples of 8 and reaches 88); all P<88 are safely rejected. Vary admissible last-three lifts among nearest local labels; any explicit representative giving 16H≤116 rules out the charge. For these survivors the fixed schedule below sufficed for every single charge; no heuristic question or exact fallback remains. Thus there can be no required triangle at t²<1.5 for this particular fractional-double extension. This is a family restriction, not a 39D density bound. A general 2Q having other fractional masks, or a different vertical shape, is not covered.
