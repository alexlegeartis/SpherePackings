# Exact obstruction to the integral-double 39D 64-colour family

No new packing record. Fix the [35D carrier and order-16 horizontal group](../2026-11-26-39d-color-budget/), extend by an order-four Q with integral double outside the old group, and embed the 64 colours in isotropic D4/4D4 with vertical period 2tD4. If this construction has squared minimum 8, then t²≥2, so its centre density 32/t⁴ is at most 8. This does not apply to quarter-integral lifts with nonintegral double, to other vertical metrics or to arbitrary packings.

Horizontal carrier L35 and old order-16 subgroup O as in dcfd2673770bac2101393904d31328f2977c7f7b. On the proposed 39D 64-colour construction with vertical D4/4D4 scale t and 2Q in the integral syndrome group, every valid ordinary order-four parity must contain a colour X appearing above a D4 root ±u; otherwise its two raw-norm-2 slots are missing. This follows because G[2] maps onto 2D4/4D4. Then 2X equals the ordinary doubled syndrome d. Multiplication by 2 on G identifies the two possible ordinary representatives through the established cheap plane: for e∈1496+I the norm-4 elements are 1623,5592; the remaining 1496 and 5719 have norm 6. Actual old horizontal generators give 2A=4096, B+C=911, w=5007. Switching Q by A toggles 4096 only. Thus both ordinary reps (not one modulo I) must be checked. For any t²<2, such an X must satisfy H(X)>7. For half-integral X, 4H is even, hence H≥7.5.

Method. Every solution 2X=d mod L35 admits a unique representative as a tuple of 5 binary fractional masks R, plus an integral syndrome s. Each shape must solve the two GF64 checks plus the radical parity. For each of 128² first-two masks, choose third label in 128 ways, fourth radical bit in 2 ways, and solve the invertible two-column system for remaining projected labels and fifth radical bit. This gives 2^22 shapes, each with 8192 charges s (2^35 possible X per d). For each fixed first-two masks, p2 is exact least cost for each syndrome from those two blocks. Fix test representatives for other three blocks, first their cheapest choices, then 99 more using short labels. If any gives total cost ≤28, this is a concrete upper-bound witness to rejection. For any uneliminated charge use exact five-block min-plus over 8192 charges and calibrated E7 mod4 costs. Because each E7 root lattice is even, if every charge is eliminated, the root slot cannot exist. All thresholds in solver are in 4H. Only scope is 64-colour extension by a half-integral order-4 new colour and homomorphic isotropic D4/4D4 lift over this fixed base O; not a 39D density upper bound. Quarter lifts with nonintegral doubled colour and nonisotropic vertical metrics remain distinct.

Constructive alternative: 2Q=B+e where B is the fixed old nonintegral order-two generator, e∈1496+I. The same cheap exceptional plane E consists of integral elements. Thus doubles U=⟨2A,2Q⟩ are a complement to E, and all three nonzero parities of U are ordinary. The associated ±root pairs in D4 form a type-A2 triangle: two independent ordinary classes whose sum is ordinary have odd mutual inner product, hence ±1. This forces horizontal lifts A′,Q′ with H(A′)≥7.5, H(Q′) and H(Q′±A′)≥7.375 at t²=1.3, simultaneously. Search for this triangle rather than separately maximizing one colour.
