# Exact covering radius of the shortened Golay–D18 carrier

Reproducible Python 3, standard library, no optimizers or network required. The 24 middle orbits are checked with integer arithmetic. For any real t choose t=2v+4b+w, with binary v, integral b, |w_i|≤1. For any h∈H and nonempty S=supp(v xor h), wrapping t against 2h+4D18 permits selecting ±2 coefficients on the support S subject to a single sign parity (choose the nearest wrapped coordinates off S). Align these signs optimally; if their sheet is wrong, flip at j∈S. Writing a_i=|w_i|, Q=Σ a_i², this yields D(t)^2≤4|S|+Q−4Σ_{i∈S}a_i+8a_j simultaneously for ALL j∈S. For every binary rounding-coset orbit v of Hamming depth 3 through 6, the included explicit positive probability distribution on (S,j) has E4|S|=26 and E(4·1_{i∈S}−8·1_{i=j})=1 for each coordinate i. Hence D(t)^2≤26+Q−Σa≤26. If Hamming depth 1, use the sole support point and Q≤18 to get 4+18+4=26; if depth 2, flip the lesser support amplitude to obtain 8+Q≤26. At depth 0, the opposite sheet is repaired on a maximum-amplitude coordinate; letting M=max a_i, cost ≤16+Q−8M≤16+18M²−8M≤26 (convexity, 0≤M≤1). At Hamming depth 7 the verifier finds one orbit and 33 nearest supports. Every coordinate belongs to at least 12 of them. On each seven-support choose best aligned signs and, if it has wrong sheet parity, flip a minimum-amplitude coordinate. At most 2/7 of the aligned scalar contribution is lost. Averaging over the 33 supports gives at most 28 + Q − 4(5/7)(12/33)||w||₁ ≤ 28 − 3||w||₁/77. The bound is reached at w=0. Explicit attaining vector twice the zero/one mask 1595; direct wrapping gives exactly 28. Therefore the Euclidean covering radius is sqrt(28), not merely an upper estimate. All binary Hamming possibilities are covered. Consequently every adjacent pair of translated complete horizontal layers needs vertical gap h≥2 at in-layer minimum μ=32. Arbitrarily varying phases, nonuniform gaps, long or aperiodic stacks cannot beat center density sqrt(2)/16 at this inherited radius. No archived priority claim about the covering radius; not the 18-dimensional optimal laminated lattice. Σh gap suffices for density upper bound; attaining alternative uses alternating 1595 and 1993 from Sphere71.

Independent rotations of complete horizontal layers do not help at this same squared minimum: for any site in one layer, an entire other rotated layer still meets its horizontal radius-√28 ball. With fixed squared minimum 32 the vertical separation is therefore at least 2. The layer covolume is 2^31, yielding the stated center-density bound. This does not cover partial layers or another horizontal lattice.
