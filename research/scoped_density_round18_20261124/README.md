# Sphere-packing research round 18: exact improvements in restricted families

There are **no catalog density changes** in this round.

* [Real covering bounds](real-cover/README.md): a two-shell harmonic-theta argument gives squared radius at most 4.5154374386… and 6.9828948250… for every extremal even unimodular lattice of ranks 48 and 72. In the conditional 96D case the exact cap is 256/27. The bounds apply to all real shifts. At the parent minimum they also tighten the upper bounds on the corresponding arbitrary-shift full-layer stacks, not unrestricted packings.
* [12D ternary motifs](12-e6-ternary/README.md): no additive motif in the orthogonal E6 × E6 ternary carrier beats Coxeter–Todd at any independent positive block scales. A 36-site patch with a rational joint-occupancy dual closes the last anisotropic nonlinear vertex (cap 1931; threshold 1935). Only one equal-scale mask is still open for nonlinear motifs.
* [48D diagonal zero-sum E8](48-tensor-diagonal/README.md): arbitrary half-period motifs with two exceptional scalar weights have center density at most 38,246,144/2401, below the P48 level, even with a negative ambient weight in the positive-definite range. The high-minimum exclusion transfers to arbitrary diagonal weights.
* [83–87D uniform-loss antipodes](83-87-antipode/README.md): exact normal-cell cap for integral self-dual min-8 rank-88 parents. A separate [shell argument](83-87-antipode/SHELL.md) keeps over a million min-8 vectors in the 85D orthogonal period for every low-determinant triple covered.
* [Two 85D Sakamoto charts](85-sakamoto-shell-charts/README.md): verified literal shell blockers and exact finite cliques exclude density gains for all actual projected motifs in one chart of each parent. Other normal spaces remain open.
* [92D cusp sublattices](92-mw-cusp-index2/README.md): six short witnesses settle the whole index-47² family through the second jet, including all translation-invariant cases. The largest squared minimum is exactly 18, not enough to improve the parent. Twelve leading directions also rule out 26,556 index-47³ profiles.
* [96D golden anchored constraints](96-golden-anchored/README.md): exact genus-two shell budgets exclude the previous formal witness, but a new all-degree two-point solution fits those budgets, and the genuinely two-anchor shortest-shell model still has a rational solution. This does not decide the existence of the lattice.
