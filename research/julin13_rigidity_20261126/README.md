# Local rigidity of a specified 13D Julin packing
This is not another density improvement. The construction of record size in 14D is independent. This companion rules out infinitesimal escapes from one standard 13D realization of the classical P13a, even with arbitrary point motions in any **fixed** finite supercell.

## Motif and finite combinatorics
Let J be the 144-word Julin code on 12 bits formed from the 132 hexads of the small Witt design and six disjoint weight-two words together with their complements, as implemented in [the checker](../../constructions/dim14/verify.py). Put the bank J+2Z^12 at even integer heights and the horizontally shifted bank (.5,...,.5)+J+2Z^12 at odd integer heights in R^13. The primitive period used here is L0=2Z^13. Every source-bank/target-bank pair is distance 2 at a suitable horizontal translate between consecutive heights.
Three small exact combinatorial checks, rerun natively:
(i) for every coordinate i, the graph on 144 words with an edge at xor weight 4 containing i is connected; (ii) at each word, every pair i,j occurs in some incident weight-4 xor support; (iii) every one of the 495 four-subsets occurs as a difference support in J. Every coordinate is balanced. The checks for this explicit version are replayable in [verify_structural.py](../../constructions/dim14/verify_structural.py).

## Tangent cone
At any fixed finite-index period L⊂L0, let H be a symmetric infinitesimal strain, with normalized affine variables, and let u_x be the periodic velocity corrections at the individual sites. The derivative half-length for an oriented contact d from a to b is d'Hd+d·(u_b−u_a). Assume all nonnegative and tr H≤0. Averaging the length-2 axial bars ±2e_i over the period forces H_ii≥0, hence all vanish, and every individual axial bar is exact at first order. Within each bank, every weight-4 difference support gives 16 contacts with independent coordinate signs. Averaging over signs and translates gives zero, hence every such bar is exact. For consecutive distinct banks, the horizontal part is s_i=(-1)^(a_i+b_i)/2 and the last component ±1. Sum all such bars over both signs, all bank pairs, and all translates. The transverse velocity terms cancel by the sign ±. Horizontal affine strain terms from s'Hs vanish **because the intra-bank Walsh argument below gives H_ij=0 first**. Horizontal velocity terms cancel by balance, even when u depends on motif: for fixed a,b average over period shifts at each of the two vertices, then sum over the other bank using its sign balance. Normal cross affine terms cancel by ±. Thus these bars are individually equalities. There are no missing unilateral contacts in these deductions.

## Zero character on L0/L
Take overcell means. On a weight-4 bank pair, the equality is a polynomial in the four independent signs: its quadratic Walsh coefficients force H_ij=0 on every supported pair, hence all horizontal mixed terms vanish. Linear coefficients say the horizontal velocity component i at the two words is equal whenever i is in the support, so all bank velocities are horizontally constant separately by (i). Opposite normal signs in cross-bank bars give separately s·(u_B−u_A)=0 and v_b−v_a+2 s·H_hz=0. The sign-product vectors span R^12: if ∑t_i(-1)^(a_i+b_i)=0, subtract a rectangular 4-term combination. For any i choose a,a' and b,b' with their two weight-4 support sets intersecting exactly in i, by (iii); only coordinate i survives. Thus the horizontal constants agree. The same rectangle on the second equation kills the H_hz entry at that coordinate. All normal velocities equal.

## Nontrivial characters
Write ξ_i=χ(2e_i). Affine H is now absent. Axial bars force each component u_i=0 when ξ_i≠1. If ξ_j≠1 for some horizontal j, take a weight-four pair a,b in a bank whose difference support contains j and any prescribed horizontal coordinate i other than j; such incident pair exists by (ii). Compare equations on a sign vector and the same vector with sign j reversed. The horizontal dot product ignores j (both u_j=0), but the translate phase of the target changes by ξ_j^±1≠1. Thus the signed linear combinations of remaining velocity components at both endpoints separately vanish for all their signs. Isolate i; all horizontal velocities vanish at every site. Cross-bank equations now imply v_a=χ(2p(a,b))v_b for the upper neighboring bank with p_i(a,b)=−(1−a_i)b_i the integer indices of the necessary horizontal period shifts. If any v is nonzero then all are, and this phase matrix has multiplicative rank one. Its rectangular identities yield ∏ξ_i^((a_i−a'_i)(b_i−b'_i))=1. The single-coordinate rectangles from (iii) now force every horizontal ξ_i=1, contradiction. If only ξ_13≠1, normal velocities vanish and (i) leaves constant horizontal velocity per bank. Compare bars with normal shifts +1 and −1: target phases differ by ξ_13 and horizontal dot product is the same, so both horizontal bank velocities are zero by the sign-vector span. Nontrivial character killed.

## Conclusion and limits
For any specified finite supercell the tangent cone of nondecreasing-distance, nonincreasing-volume deformations is just translations/rotations. To deduce strict local optimality for that finite cell modulo trivial motions, a convergent hypothetical sequence of other at-least-as-dense feasible representatives gives normalized tangent vectors in a finite-dimensional unit sphere after gauging translations/rotations. A subsequential limit would contradict the result above. No uniform radius over periods, no global upper bound, and no prohibition on remote alternative packings are asserted.
