# A 37D hyperplane section of the coupled E7 lattice

This explicit lattice has squared minimum 8 and Gram determinant
\(3\cdot2^{29}\). Its center density is \(16/\sqrt3\), a **15.47%**
gain over the preceding 37D lattice and unrestricted lower bound of 8.
It is **63.30%** higher than Cohn's pinned 37D value \(2^{5/2}\).
This is a lower bound, not a claim of global optimality.

The [coupled 38D parent](../e7_lattice38_20261123/) is generated by its
horizontal lattice \(L_{35}\) and three lifts with vertical coordinates
\((1/2,1/2,0)\), \((1,0,1)\), and \((0,1,1)\), respectively. Take
the hyperplane normal \((1/2,-1/2,1/2)\) in the vertical space, with
zero horizontal component. Its pairings with the three lifted generators
are 0, 1 and 0. It is primitive in the dual, and deleting the second
lift gives a basis for the full section, not merely a sublattice.
Its squared length is \(3/4\), so the section Gram determinant is
\(2^{31}\cdot3/4\). The minimum is at least 8 by inheritance and
equals 8 because the horizontal period includes every doubled E7 root.

Run `python3 -B verify37.py` without optimization. It replays the
parent minimum and basis proofs, then builds the section basis and
Gram matrix from those same defining data. In section37.json both
the basis and Gram matrix are scaled to integers, by factors 2 and 4.
