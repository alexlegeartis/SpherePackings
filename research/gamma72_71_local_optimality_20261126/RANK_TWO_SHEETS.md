# A sharp 71D constructive limit on independently arranged canonical Γ72/(2A2) sheets

The known [70D section data](../novel_routes_20261113/higher_parent/gamma72_verified.json) give a primitive Γ72 dual normal of Gram K=[[8,−4],[−4,8]]. The full 70D section P has covolume √48. Retain the 48 canonical phases, indexed by Z²/KZ², but place an arbitrary full translate of P at each independently chosen real height. Multiple distinct phases at the same height and non-periodic sequences are allowed. The theorem concerns the **standard ambient-minimum/normal-distance certificate only**. It does not exclude a better actual distance for a particular phase pair.

The scaled dual triangular norm is q(i,j)=(i²+ij+j²)/6. Write q_T for its minimum over KZ² changes of representative. For distinct labels the squared horizontal separation is certified ≥8−q_T. For a repeated label the horizontal component can vanish at identical sites; its height difference must be ≥√μ to certify μ. Necessarily μ≤8 within this certificate. Put e=8−μ. For any two distinct labels at vertical gap g the check being imposed is q_T≤e+g². We bound the upper number f of sheets per unit height, so the certified center density is f(μ/4)^(71/2)/√48.

* For 0≤e≤1/8, consecutive sites are at least √(1/6−e) apart because the least nonzero triangular torus distance squared is 1/6.
* For 1/8≤e≤5/12, four consecutive sites have total height span ≥√(1/2−e). Indeed, distinct normal classes with all pairwise q_T<1/2 form at most a triangle: the only nonzero norm below 1/2 is 1/6, the six nearest directions form a hexagon, and two distinct neighbors of 0 are adjacent only when consecutive on the hexagon. A triangular loop here lifts coherently (three edges of length √(1/6) total less than the shortest period √8). If a label repeats the stronger √μ gap applies. Summing the ordered four-site windows gives f≤3/√(1/2−e), even without a repeat cell.
* For e≥5/12, each color separately has f_color≤1/√μ; hence f≤48/√μ.

The three upper densities attain their common global maximum in this envelope at e=1/8, f=√24. For either of the first two intervals the log-density is const+(71/2)log(8−e)−(1/2)log(q−e); its derivative changes from negative to positive at most once, so only endpoints need be compared. The last bound is decreasing. The exact rational comparisons are in verify.py, including the comparisons at e=0 and 5/12.

The sharp witness alternates normal steps (1,0) and (−1,1) on that triangular dual, with all consecutive heights 1/√24. An even separation j=2r has lifted normal norm r²/6=j²/24; either odd separation j=2r+1 has normal norm (r²+r+1)/6=j²/24+1/8. Distinct labels therefore pass the 63/8 separation test. An even separation repeats a label only after at least 24 steps (the order of (0,1) is 12), and an odd one cannot repeat since one numerator in K^-1 applied to (1,r) or (−1,r+1) is odd over 12. Thus repeat gaps exceed √(63/8). The exact certified center density coincides with the old norm-8 two-layer construction: its square is (4/8)(63/32)^71. It reaches 99.9052320963% of the actual 71D incumbent (the separate norm-10 double, center-density square (4/10)(79/40)^71). There is no useful rerun with another height word on this canonical rank-two carrier; the surviving moves must change the physical phases or their number of sites per period, or prove better horizontal distances.

**Discovery scope.** An independent variable-gap scan on 420 positive binary normal forms (even diagonal 8…20 and up to 28 for the second; off-diagonal 0…a/2) with translated two-step words found no uniform gain, including instances recovering the norm-10 incumbent. Additional three- to six-step finite word screens were used to try actual staggered structures, not prove a global negative. The sharp result above is mathematical and does not use those scans. No higher-rank/wild-period result is inferred.
