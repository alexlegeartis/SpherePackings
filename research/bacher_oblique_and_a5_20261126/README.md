## 27D constructive outcome

Use exactly the established 22D horizontal lattice S, determinant 64, and its 64-element group H=S*/S=Z4²×Z2² with the id and W4 order in research/2026-11-26-27d-bacher-glue/. Taking the NEW period P=√2 A5, in the basis e_i−e_6 of A5, map the four generators to (320,31,2,8) in 4-torsion encoded in five base-four digits, low first, divide corresponding lattice vector by four. The union of 64 translates of S×P is a lattice because the matching is additive. All 64 squared distances meet old requirements (57 exact matches, 6 with slack 1, 1 with slack 2 counting zero); pure period has min4, so total min exactly 4. Determinant 64·192/64²=3; center density 1/√3, a lattice TIE only. Any 27D translated copy with horizontal displacement in S* first subtracts a glued point and becomes a pure-tail displacement, whose squared distance is at most 3 (covering radius squared of √2 A5); hence no doubled union from this entire horizontally aligned family can beat Vardy (requires offset distance squared > 4(√(3/2)/2)^(2/27)). Arbitrary off-palette translations remain open.

## All common-parent oblique sections, inherited minimum only

Start at existing G28 min8 and its h=1,2 slip extensions (29D and 30D, respectively). In original coordinates put f=e4*, g=e27* (zero-based). These have Gram 1/3[[2,−1],[−1,2]], and slip direction d=−6G28^{-1}e4* is entirely in their plane; both binary half labels vanish at coordinates 4,27. Remove rows/columns 4,27 to obtain rank-26 common lattice T (not the S above). Then det T=3·2^26, min T*=4/3; the exact shell up through exponent 8 of 6 T^{-1} is {0:1,8:1638}. Every parent dual projects to T* and has low-dimensional kernel M=(span T)^⊥∩parent*, with scaled Grams in the verifier; ranks (min Gram determinant by rank ascending starting at rank zero) are M2: (1,2/3,1/3); M3: (1,6/11,4/11,2/11); M4: (1,22/35,12/35,8/35,4/35). For any integral normal lattice N of rank k, let q=rank π_T(N), and intersect N with M, rank k−q. The vertical component orthogonal to the kernel can only increase the Gram determinant of the quotient vectors, so det N≥R_{k−q}(M)·(4/3)^q/γ_q^q, with γ_q^q=(1,1,4/3,2,4,8) for q=0..5. Section density at inherited radius obeys δ_parent/(2^(k/2)√det N) for a basis of the saturated dual normals. Across all three parents, resulting upper bounds for these inherited-radius certificates are: 25D 1/(2√2), 26D 1/√3, 27D 1/√3, 28D 2/3. For truly core-oblique normals (q>0), 27D bound ≤1/√6 and 28D ≤√(2/11). Does NOT rule out sections whose true minimum is independently larger, or other parents or multi-coset constructions.
