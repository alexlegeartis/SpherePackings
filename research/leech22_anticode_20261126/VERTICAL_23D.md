# 23D: exact ceiling for arbitrary heights over the 22D carrier

Let P=Λ22*=π_W Λ be the fixed 22D projected Leech grid of covolume 1/√12 used in the twelve-point anticode theorem. Let X be any subset of P×R with squared separation μ∈(0,4]. Fibers can be arbitrary real height sets, empty, finite or infinite, unrelated in different columns. Then its upper 23D center density is at most 1/2, exactly achieved by the laminated Λ23. This is below the [Chen–Hu–Li–Wang–Wu 23D packing](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02394v1#S3), with center density 2/√6·(23/24)^(23/2)≈0.50049. In particular adapting individual heights, allowing an arbitrary time-like stagger, and switching uncountably many vertical phases (across candidate packings) on these fixed 22D columns do not suffice under the stated normalization. Different 22D grids and μ>4 are not covered.

Use the anticode stencils S from [ANTICODE.md](ANTICODE.md), with parameters (s,β)=(1,0), (4,3), (12,11/3), where β is the maximum squared horizontal distance. For μ>β, all heights appearing in columns indexed by a single translated stencil t+S are pairwise separated by at least √(μ−β), even within a single column, and hence their count in an interval of length H is at most 1+H/√(μ−β). Summing over centers t∈P whose translates meet a large horizontal cube, and accounting for the s occurrences of each counted site, bounds upper point intensity by √12/(s√(μ−β)). This uniform cube-intensity bound also holds for upper density in Euclidean balls: tile large balls by cubes of side H, use the uniform cube bound, let the ball radius grow first and then H→∞. Hence the center-density upper bound is
F_s(μ)= (√12/s)(μ/4)^(23/2)/√(μ−β).

For μ≤16/5 apply F₁, increasing, at most F₁(16/5)<1/2. For 16/5≤μ≤15/4 apply F₄, increasing there because its only critical point is 69/22<16/5. Its largest value is F₄(15/4)=(15/16)^(23/2)<1/2 (exact comparison 4·15^23<16^23). For 15/4≤μ≤4 use F₁₂; its only critical point is a minimum at 23/6, and its endpoints are F₁₂(15/4)=F₄(15/4) and F₁₂(4)=1/2. The boundary value F₁(16/5)=(√15/2)(4/5)^(23/2)<1/2 by 15·4^23<5^23. Therefore equality requires μ=4.

For actual attainability take a primitive shortest norm-four Leech vector v in the normal √2A₂ lattice (e.g. raw 4(e₀−e₁)). Inside its perpendicular hyperplane the lattice Λ∩v^⊥ has covolume |v|=2 by primitive unimodular section duality and minimum squared 4 (other min vectors use disjoint coordinates). Write that hyperplane as W⊕(U∩v^⊥); any such lattice point projects in W onto P, and the remaining component is a real height. Thus it is an admissible packing of center 1/2. No equality classification in 23D is claimed.
