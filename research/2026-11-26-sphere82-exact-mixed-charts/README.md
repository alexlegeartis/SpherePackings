## A constructive four-chart equivalence

In the period 2Z³ × 2Z⁴ × 2Z⁴, allow integer or simultaneous half-integer coordinates in either four-block (2Z is a subperiod assumption for the packing in both blocks, not just an ambient grid convention). A mark is uniquely indexed by its outer binary word p, two inner binary words x₁,x₂ and two bits c₁,c₂: its representatives are p, x₁+c₁(1,1,1,1)/2, x₂+c₂(1,1,1,1)/2. For two words let h be the squared wrapped OUTER distance and d₁,d₂ the inner Hamming distances. If cᵢ differs across a pair, the shortest wrapped displacement has exactly four coordinates of size 1/2 in that block, so the entire contribution is exactly 1, independent of both binary patterns. If the chart agrees, it is dᵢ. Therefore each pair can be tested by h+(Δc₁?1:d₁)+(Δc₂?1:d₂)≥4. Two equal underlying 11-bit words never coexist since their distance is ≤2. Conversely any subset of binary words with such a four-label assignment is a physical packing with the stated periods. For outer cubic h=d_H(p,p'), period volume 2048; 73 would beat the current comparator.

**Exact successful/failed decision for two chart labels over incumbent words.** Choose two labels (without loss 0 and u≠0). Difference 0 demands full Hamming d≥4; difference u erases one or two chosen four-blocks and demands d_outside+|u|≥4. On a fixed binary support each pair forbids neither, one, or both possibilities for equality of the two labels. This is exactly a signed graph consistency test, solvable by parity components. Starting with any one of the complete (11,72,4) binary codes, retain all 72 words or delete just one. For the retained old words no pair forbids equal labels. Edges that forbid different labels form ordinary connected components on which the label must be constant. For each proposed new word w record component sets E(w),D(w) requiring its label equal to or different from that component. If these intersect it is impossible. For two proposed new words w,z additionally test either same relative labels (E(w)∩D(z) and D(w)∩E(z) empty, plus d(w,z)≥4) or opposite (E(w)∩E(z) and D(w)∩D(z) empty, plus altered distance ≥4). This condition is sufficient as well: pick the first label 0, the second as tested, fill required components, and assign free ones arbitrarily. Any deleted old word reinserted with a changed chart is already covered by testing all assignments in the all-72 branch. A candidate whose individual equal/different blockers hit two or more identical old vertices is impossible after ≤1 deletion and can be filtered safely.

## Independent noncubical construction: a sharp target obstruction

Take outer FCC lattice F=D₃/√2 (covolume 1/√2, minimum 1), modulo 2F. Its eight difference classes consist of 0, six with squared norm 1 (opposite root pairs of D₃), and one with squared norm 2 (e.g. 2e₁/√2); this labels them by F₂³, last class 111 after basis choice. The minimum is verified by integer coordinate parity: the six norm-two residue classes have representatives ±eᵢ±eⱼ, i<j, quotienting by 2D₃ identifies signs in opposite pairs, and the remaining class contains 2e₁, of squared norm four before normalization. The outer period's shortest length is 2. At inner compressed period 2Z⁴ × 2Z⁴ a strict gain requires N > 72/√2, i.e. at least 51. Actually at most 48 marks are possible in the entire four-chart architecture:

Partition the eight prefixes into two groups containing one from each of the four complementary pairs. Inside one group outer squared distance is ≤1. Two opposite chart labels cannot occur there: their two inner contributions are 1+1. Thus either there is a single chart, or only two adjacent labels (any 3 of the 4-chart square contain opposites). Projection to the inner two blocks is injective and has minimum squared distance ≥3. A single chart admits at most A(8,3)=20, by parity extension A(8,3)=A(9,4) and Best–Brouwer–MacWilliams–Odlyzko–Sloane, 1978 (https://neilsloane.com/doc/Me54.pdf). For two adjacent labels, project both to the unchanged binary four-block. Their nonempty projection sets S,T have mutual Hamming distance at least 2, because the erased block contributes exactly 1. Removing unused vertices disconnects Q₄, which is 4-vertex-connected, hence |S|+|T|≤12. Elementary justification: induct on n with two Q_{n-1} halves and their perfect matching. If ≤n−1 vertices are removed and both halves lose at most n−2, they remain connected by induction and a matching edge survives; otherwise one half is untouched and every remaining vertex of the other has its matching neighbor. For n≥2, 2^{n−1}>n−1. Over each used value all sites come from one chart, and their other binary 4-bit words have mutual distance ≥3, hence at most 2 such words (translate one to zero; two different vectors of weight ≥3 are at distance ≤2). Thus this outer group contains at most 24 sites. Sum both groups. This proves only the compressed FCC model has no strict gain. In the full 2D₄ periods a different-chart wrapped contribution can be three instead of one, and one must NOT quote the 48 bound there. With full inner periods the gain instead needs 204.
