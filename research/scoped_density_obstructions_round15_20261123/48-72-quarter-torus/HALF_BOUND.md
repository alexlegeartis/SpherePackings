# Half-period torus bounds for extremal Type II ranks 24, 48, 72 and 96

No new unrestricted numerical bound is asserted. The 96-dimensional statement is conditional on the existence of an extremal even unimodular lattice (minimum squared norm 10).

In rank 24k, k=1,2,3,4, let L be even unimodular of minimum squared norm 2k+2. Let a=v/2 and the twisted theta f as in [HALF48_BASE.md](HALF48_BASE.md). At infinity the coefficients of t^0,t²,...,t^(2k) are (1,0,...,0). The group and cusp proof in rank48 works with theta weight 12k in every case (Poisson phase is +1). For integral a² and squared depth at least 2k, f has a factor [Y(X+Y)]^(2k). The remaining T-invariant polynomial has degree 2k and k+1 basis monomials Z^(2k−2i) X^(2i). Exact solution gives leading shifted coefficient t^(2k) equal to 48, 2,400, 136,704, or 8,222,400, respectively. Thus an integral half-period has squared depth at most 2k, and a deepest integral half-period has that exact number of neighbors.

For half-integral a² assume depth ≥2k+1/2. Divide by θ₃²θ₄² as in rank48. The quotient weight 12k−2 again has the same factor, leaving degree 2k−1; the k available monomials cannot have leading coefficients (1,0,...,0) after multiplying back the theta factor. Thus anisotropic depth is at most 2k−1/2. For harmonic degree two and a deepest integral phase, weight 12k+2 leaves degree 2k+1 and k+1 monomials. Vanishing of the first k+1 even coefficients forces the harmonic theta to vanish. Antipodality and this identity prove the whole nearest shell is a spherical 2-design.

Replay with python half_bound.py (SymPy). The coefficient matrices use finite rational/integer recurrences. In rank72, this yields a fixed-family upper matching existing 73–75 periodic and 73–76 lattice-only lower bounds for full L layers using half-period phases at squared min 8, via one-, two-, three- and four-dimensional optimal vertical covolumes at squared min 2. Same caveats as rank48: arbitrary off-half phases, other horizontal periods, partial levels and smaller minima outside the quantified range are not covered.
