# A half-torus theorem for extremal 48-dimensional unimodular lattices

A scoped optimality result and not a general covering-radius upper bound.

Let L be any even unimodular lattice of rank 48 with minimum squared norm 6, and a=v/2 modulo L for v in L. If v²=0 mod4, then d(a,L)²≤4. If v²=2 mod4, then d(a,L)²≤7/2. Every half phase of squared depth exactly 4 has precisely 2,400 exact nearest vectors, and the relative contact vectors form a spherical 2-design. Consequently
d(a+h,L)² ≤ 4+||h||²−||h||/√3,
so no half phase of depth four has any deeper point in its Euclidean torus ball of radius 1/√3.

## Theta derivation

Use t=exp(πiτ), q=t², and
f(τ)=Σ_{x∈L} (−1)^{(x,v)} t^{x²}.
By Poisson summation for self-dual L, f|_24 S=Σ_{y∈L+a} t^{y²} (the phase factor is 1 in rank 48). f is T-invariant since L is even. The conjugate S T² S acts on f by +1 if a² is integral, −1 if a² is half-integral, since all numbers y² are in a²+Z. The two matrices T² and ST²S generate Γ(2) up to −I. Put X=θ₂⁴, Y=θ₄⁴, Z=X+2Y, with θ₃⁴=X+Y. Under T the variables transform X→−X, Y→X+Y, so the polynomial invariants are generated by Z and X². Under S the weight-two variables transform X→−Y and Y→−X.

For integral a², f is holomorphic on Γ(2), including all cusps: f and f|S have nonnegative powers, and f is T-invariant. Holomorphic weight 2d forms on Γ(2) are the homogeneous degree-d polynomials in X,Y. Indeed the d+1 monomials are linearly independent by distinct leading exponents at infinity, and the valence formula gives dimension at most d+1 (Γ(2) has index six and no elliptic fixed points). If d(a,L)²≥4, then f|S starts at t⁴ or later; as X starts at t and Y has constant 1, f is divisible by Y⁴. T-invariance also gives divisibility by (X+Y)⁴. The remaining invariant has degree four and basis Z⁴,Z²X²,X⁴. The t⁰,t²,t⁴ coefficients of f are (1,0,0); the exact three-by-three matrix in all_half48.py forces (1/16,−1/32,11/2048). After S its coefficient of t⁴ is 16⁴ times the sum, namely 2,400. In particular it cannot have depth >4.

For half-integral a², put g=f/(θ₃² θ₄²). The denominator has weight 2, is nowhere zero on the upper half-plane, is invariant under T, and changes sign under ST²S: its S-transform is −θ₃²θ₂², which changes sign under T². Its S-transform has leading exponent t^(1/2), so the quotient is holomorphic at the cusps. Thus g is a Γ(2) holomorphic form of weight 22 invariant under T. If the depth were ≥9/2, then g|S would vanish to order at least 4 in t. The same divisibility leaves the weight-six invariant basis Z³, ZX². Multiplying by the denominator, their t⁰,t²,t⁴ coefficient columns are (8,0,−6912) and (0,512,−20480); no linear combination has (1,0,0). Thus depth ≤7/2 by its discrete congruence.

For contact 2-design, apply the integral-case argument with f weighted by a homogeneous harmonic quadratic P. Poisson summation gives the coset weighted by P up to a constant phase, which does not change vanishing orders; the weight is now 26, the first three even coefficients are all zero, and the normalized theta is again Γ(2), T-invariant. The same cusp divisibility leaves degree five and basis Z⁵,Z³X²,ZX⁴. Its three-by-three coefficient matrix is nonsingular (all_half48.py), so the entire harmonic theta series vanishes. The nearest relative vectors are antipodal (2a∈L) with second moment 200 I. For any h one therefore has a nearest relative vector w with w·h ≤−||h||/√12; use it to bound the distance. This proves the displayed torus inequality.

## Consequence for full-layer packings

For 1≤k≤5, take full horizontal L layers, each shifted by a point of (1/2)L/L, stacked vertically in R^k. If the packing has squared min μ>4, at most one layer occurs at each vertical site and the sites have minimum squared distance at least μ−4. At μ=6 all these sites therefore have vertical min²≥2. For arbitrary periodic sites in k=1,2,3, sharp vertical covolumes per point are √2, √3, 2 from the 1D, hexagonal and Kepler results. For a lattice in the full dimension, the vertical projection is itself a discrete lattice (reduce horizontal representatives modulo the full L to bound the horizontal coordinates; discreteness excludes a sequence of distinct projected points tending to zero), and the sharp vertical lattice covolumes for k=4,5 are both 2 from D4 and D5. The existing lower bounds in 49–53D attain these respective ceilings with full depth-four half cosets (49–50 P48q, 51–53 P48n). This is an exact match for the half-shift stacking family at μ=6, unrestricted among such periodic stacks through 51D and lattice-only through 53D. It also includes μ∈[4+k/12,6] since μ^((48+k)/2)(μ−4)^(−k/2) is increasing there. Off-half translations, other horizontal geometry, thinner layers, and smaller radii outside this interval remain outside the proof.

## Both parity halves of the minimum shell are perfect and strongly eutactic

For a depth-four v/2, the t^6 coefficient of its ordinary twisted theta is −12,800 (quad48.py), whereas the unsigned extremal shell has 52,416,000 vectors. The degree-two harmonic vanishing above shows each character-sign half of the original norm-six shell is a 2-design in its own right. The following additionally proves perfection without a huge shell enumeration.

For a harmonic quartic P4 the twisted theta has weight 28, hence degree 14. Divisibility by Y⁴(X+Y)⁴ and the zero coefficients at t⁰,t²,t⁴ leave only a multiple of X⁶ for the invariant of degree six. Triangularity follows from the powers X^0,X²,X^4,X^6 starting at t^0,t²,t⁴,t⁶. Its t⁶ coefficient therefore equals, up to the weight normalization sign of modulus one, 16² times the shifted harmonic contact sum at t⁴.

If A is traceless symmetric and F(x)=(x^T A x)², its harmonic decomposition is P4+||x||²P2+c||x||⁴ with c=||A||_F²/1200 by the degree-four spherical moment. On the 2-design of 2,400 contacts w of norm²4,
Σ F(w) ≤ Σ 4 w^T A²w = 800||A||_F².
The harmonic-quadratic part sums to zero and the radial part is 32||A||_F², so the absolute shifted P4 sum is bounded by 832||A||_F². The signed quartic perturbation of the parent moment is bounded by (256×832+384)||A||_F²; the 384 accounts for −12,800 twisted scalar count. On the other hand the full 11-design unsigned moment is 1,572,480||A||_F². Both half-shell quadratic evaluation operators consequently have strictly positive fourth energy on traceless nonzero A. For any A that annihilates all evaluation rows of either half, its trace is zero by the respective 2-design, and this positivity then gives A=0. Thus both halves are perfect and strongly eutactic. The argument is independent of which extremal rank-48 parent or which depth-four half coset was used.
