# Sphere-packing density research: exact family results (23–24 November 2026)

These results concern named construction families. They do not update the density tables or establish a new unrestricted or lattice-wide optimum.

- [96D square-affine Craig lattices](96-craig/README.md): all 48 ideal classes over the full stretch continuum lie below 54.3% of the MW92–D4 lattice comparator. Arbitrary metric perturbations with relative condition ratio at most 11/8 still lie below 99.8%.
- [48D and 72D fourth-torsion layers](48-72-quarter-torus/README.md): distances squared to any extremal parent from fourth-period points are at most 4 and 6, respectively, sharp across the stated families. This extends the previously matched density ceilings for specific stacking families adjacent to these ranks.
- [88D and 96D golden trace twists](96-golden-trace/README.md): exact twist curves, a reverse 5-modular construction criterion, and obligatory short-shell counts. The large gains remain conditional on constructing a fully cancelling parent.
- [96D golden graph gluing](96-golden-graph/README.md): low-rank same-parent graph modifications cannot produce the cancelling branch. [Ranks 7–8 with a totally singular annihilator](96-golden-graph/SINGULAR.md) are also excluded.
- [12D ternary E6 phases](12-ternary-E6/README.md): two sheared masks have exact capacity 21 each. Piecewise-quadratic phases under one orthogonal mask have a separate obstruction.
- [Conditional 96D fourth-torsion bound](96-quarter-torus/README.md): from a hypothetical rank-96 minimum-10 even unimodular parent, squared depth at most 8. Extract [replay.tar.gz](96-quarter-torus/replay.tar.gz) for the standalone proof and replay source.
