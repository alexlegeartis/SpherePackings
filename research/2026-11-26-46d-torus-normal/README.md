# 46D: sharp scope for full discriminant-colour fibres, not a new packing record

Use an extremal positive definite even unimodular lattice P of rank 48 and minimum 6. For a primitive rank-two section K=P∩U, write L=P∩U⊥. A colour is one complete L-translate in its canonical discriminant glue K*/K; independently chosen arbitrary translates outside this glue, partial fibres, deformations of the carrier and nonprojected constructions are outside this proof. Discriminant gluing and unimodularity give covol(L)=sqrt(D) for D=det(K). A palette with s colours and squared separation m has center density s(m/4)^23/sqrt(D).

**Actual metric computation.** For every D≤68 and any actual K, even without assuming m≤6, every palette has density ≤(13/9)^23/sqrt(3) (CS96; occupied 2.86095747213606e−8 in the repository at 271316b59d099c4a0a3f517377c21ed9923aecca). Only the equilateral normal (6,6,3) can match. This is not a bound on arbitrary 46D packings. Reduction gives an even binary Gram [[A,g],[g,B]] with 6≤A≤B, 0≤2g≤A, D=AB−g²≥3A²/4; hence D≤68 gives exactly the 20 triples enumerated by audit.py. In all of them the origin-colour population at norm 6 has a strictly positive rational lower certificate, so indeed m≤6. As a separate corollary, no two-colour motif at m≤6 beats the known construction for *any* normal: at D≥69 even the trivial radius bound falls short since 12(27/26)^46<69. No assertion m≤6 for arbitrary huge D.

**Theta dissection.** C=K*/K. Write p=(<w,u>,<w,v>); normal squared length q(p)=pᵀG^{-1}p. In each discriminant class c let q_c be its minimum. For a nonzero horizontal vector in the glued colour c, squared norm is 6−q_c+2j, j≥0; subtracting a K period resets normal to its shortest representative. Unknown h0(c),h1(c) count horizontal vectors for j=0,1 (the zero horizontal vector is excluded, even for c=0). These counts are invariant under c↔−c. A norm-6 shell joint profile consists of h0(c) copies at each q=q_c, plus separately the pure K shell. Norm 8 has h1(c) at each q=q_c and h0(c) at q=q_c+2, and its pure K shell. In mixed.py the central-orbit loop sums all representatives on both c and −c but uses one population per individual signed coset. Distinct q values in each class differ by even integers. Enumeration |a|,|b|≤12, q≤12 is complete: nearest point by the centered fundamental parallelogram has q_c≤(A+B+2g)/4≤13/2 for these types; hence q≤q_c+2≤17/2, and Cauchy gives |a|≤sqrt(8·17/2)<9, |b|≤sqrt(12·17/2)<11. For pure shell m≤8 basis coefficients satisfy |j|≤sqrt(8A/D), |i|≤sqrt(8B/D), so the checked box [-3,3]^2 suffices.

**Exact harmonic constraints.** Ordinary theta series is E4^6−1440 E4^3 Δ+125280 Δ², giving shell populations N6=52,416,000 and N8=39,007,332,000. For a harmonic homogeneous polynomial H_d, positive even degree d, the weighted theta sum is a modular form of weight 24+d divisible by Δ³. Therefore d=2,4,6,8,10 vanish; d=12 gives a multiple of Δ³ (ratio shell8/shell6 of −72), d=14 vanishes since M₂=0, and d=16 is a multiple of E₄Δ³ (ratio +168). These equations hold when a shell6 sum vanishes as well. Project each monomial a^i b^j to harmonic degree d=i+j in R^48 by summing c_h ||w||^(2h) Δ_E^h(a^i b^j), c_0=1, c_h=−c_(h−1)/[2h(2d+48−2h−2)], where Δ_E=A∂aa+2g∂ab+B∂bb. This gives integer equations on nonnegative h0,h1 after clearing denominators. The script computes exact RREF and stores an affine parametrization, not an assertion of realizability for arbitrary free parameters.

**Elimination.** two.py uses floating LP only to choose support of potential nonnegative dual certificate; solves for multipliers over Q, checks exact identity and signs, then saves multipliers. audit.py independently rechecks these lower bounds using only integer/rational arithmetic. If h0(c)>0 then actual horizontal separation squared for colour difference c equals 6−q_c. Else, if h0+h1>0, it is at most 8−q_c. If neither is certified we impose no cap beyond 6. The pointwise conservative metric may combine incompatible classes; it can only overestimate a palette's performance. Form the finite Cayley graph at every resulting threshold beta: pair allowed when the **upper bound** for its horizontal separation is ≥6−beta. Translation invariance fixes zero. Independent pivoted Bron–Kerbosch recursion certifies max clique s and s²(6−beta)^46/(D·4^46) is compared to the squared reference exactly. The score table summary46.json is a family upper bound, not a new SOTA.

**Geometry with a merely guaranteed distance.** For *any* K, choose beta=max shortest squared torus distances of colour differences. The safe radius gives 6−beta, which might not be actual without shell witnesses. If beta<2/3, representatives chosen close to one anchor lift consistently: any triangle winding would sum to a nonzero K vector of length ≤3sqrt(beta)<sqrt6. For unwrapped marks in a common planar diameter sqrt(beta), fundamental area d=1/sqrt(D)≤1/sqrt27 and shortest spacing ≥sqrt6 d. For one mark, trivial. For collinear s≥2, sd≤2sqrt(beta/6). For three noncollinear marks d≤min(1/sqrt27, sqrt(3)beta/2) by the maximal triangle area; best score at beta=2/9 equals benchmark. For s≥6 a triangulation yields sd≤2s area/(s−2)≤3πbeta/4; for s=4 diagonals give area≤beta/2 and for s=5 polygonal isoperimetry and P≤πsqrt(beta) give area≤π²beta/(20tan(π/5)), also sufficient. Maximize beta(6−beta)^23 at 1/4. At beta≥2/3 (possibly wrapped), lift *only* to disk radius sqrt(beta) around one anchor. Pick with disk area and perimeter bounds gives sd≤πbeta+πsqrt(beta/6)+1/sqrt27; for collinear marks the same bound follows from counting a disk chord. Product with (6−beta)^23 decreases there and endpoint is already strictly below benchmark. Hence a genuine improvement in this model requires actual shell slack, not a more ingenious choice of colours from a guaranteed distance bound.
