# Sphere81: two interacting D4 triality blocks in 11D
No density increase is asserted. Squared minimum four. The period before scaling is Λ=2Z³ × 2D4 × 2D4, covolume 8192. Marks are taken from V/Λ, V=Z³ × D4* × D4*, size 32768. Independently squared-rescale the two D4 blocks by a,b≥1/2; covolume becomes 8192 a²b² and nonzero periods have length ≥2. At a=b=1 need 289 marks to beat 9/256. With doubled coordinates X(q)_k=2((q>>(k+1))&1)+(q>>5)+4(i=0)(q&1), one D4* /2D4 mark is q=0,...,63. A wrapped difference Δ of these doubled coordinates has r_i congruent to Δ_i mod4 in [-2,2), and squared norm [∑r_i²+( (∑(Δ_i−r_i)/4) mod2 )min_i(16−8|r_i|)]/4. Total cut test: Hamming(p,p') + a d(q,q')+ b d(s,s')≥4.

**Exact integral-carrier dichotomy, a=b=1.** Mod D4 each D4* block gives the four-element group F₂², with nondegenerate alternating pairing 2〈x,y〉 mod2 (for instance an odd unit vector paired with the half-ones vector gives one). In the two blocks this is a symplectic space E of dimension four. All Euclidean scalar products of the differences in the packing are integral iff the affine color support is contained in an affine Lagrangian plane in E: translate one mark to zero, take the span of differences and extend the isotropic span to a maximal isotropic 2-space. The ordinary period pairs integrally with all such marks. There are nine transverse product planes ℓ₁⊕ℓ₂ and six graphs of symplectic isomorphisms F₂²→F₂². Indeed if a Lagrangian meets one summand in a line, its projection there lies in the perpendicular of that line, the line itself, forcing the product form; otherwise it graphs an isomorphism. In both 2-space factors GL(2,2)=Sp(2,2) has six elements.

A product plane adjoins one of the three nonzero discriminant classes to each D4. The three resulting rank-four lattices are isometric to Z⁴ via D4 triality, so the entire packing lies in a translate of a rotated Z¹¹. Partition into disjoint integer cubes of side two; each supplies a Hamming (11,4) code of size at most 72. Thus at most 288 per Λ period (attained by any lifted OBK code).

A diagonal plane glues the two D4 blocks to E8: the norm mod2 in each nonzero discriminant class is one, hence their common graph gluing gives an even unimodular rank-eight lattice (a copy of E8); triality also explicitly identifies all six gluings. Partition the eight 3-bit prefixes into four adjacent pairs. Within such a pair, projected inner marks are distinct; their squared separations are at least 3, hence by evenness at least 4. They form a min-2 8D packing of period 2D4×2D4, volume 1024. By [Viazovska 2017](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2017/185-3/p07), each pair has ≤1024/16=64 marks. Thus the whole diagonal plane admits ≤256. This is sharp: write J for 90° rotation on each consecutive coordinate pair in both D4 blocks. It preserves both D4 and each discriminant class (it has two sign changes per 4-block), so J(E8)=E8. The similarity I+J generates a min-2 sublattice T⊂E8 of index 16, containing 2E8 since (I+J)(I−J)=2I. Four different T-cosets at the four even cube prefixes (one per prefix) are mutually separated: outer distance is two, inner nonzero E8 distance at least two squared. There are 64 marks per chosen prefix. It follows that in the FULL 32768-mark carrier, any strict record needs a demonstrable pair of differences with half-integral scalar product; neither of the integral carrier types will do. This is not a restriction on all 11D packings.

**Exact sharp endpoint and open scale neighborhood.** For a=b=1/2 each of the two blocks is maximally contracted; the record target is 19 marks. In fact exactly 16 are possible, also throughout all a,b≥1/2 with max(3a+4b,4a+3b)<4, 4max(a,b)<3, max(2a+3b,3a+2b)<3. H={0,1,30,31} consists of zero and the three norm-four residues in one 64-alphabet; direct evaluation of the four-coordinate formula shows it is a subgroup and that d(v,H), as a list with multiplicities, is either 0,4,4,4 (v∈H), 1,1,3,3, or 2,2,2,2. In one fixed prefix two marks must have d=4 in both blocks, so each nonempty occupied bank uses distinct marks in each of two fixed H-cosets, a partial permutation; size at most 4. For two adjacent prefixes suppose their sizes sum to at least 5. If their H-cosets match in either block, some pair has equal component there, and the other component contributes at most 4max(a,b)<3, impossible. If neither matches, all distances in each block are ≤3. Across adjacent prefixes both block distances must be 3, since a 2 would make their weighted sum <3. At least one of the two banks has three marks, but for a mark in the other, each list of distances has at most two 3s. Contradiction. A matching of the cube now bounds the size by 4×4=16. The four even prefixes attain it with their four marks (x,x+c_p), x∈H, where the four c_p are different. Internal marks have squared distance 4(a+b)≥4, distinct prefixes have Hamming 2 and at least one deep difference 4 adding at least 2. This rules out only the stated corner of the two-scale model.

**A two-pitch bank reduction, including arbitrary vertically nonperiodic donors.** Fix S old period-2 axial rows independent of each other. Require each donor point in its transverse 10-torus to have projected distance ≥2 from S; assume donor layers have vertical gaps ≥1, so nonconsecutive levels cannot interact. Each layer bank A has internal distance ≥2. Its successive gap to bank B is at least h(A,B)=max{1,sqrt(max(0,4−min d_T(A,B)²))}; the min is over actual wrapped transverse pair distances. The best normalized density over any sequence of allowed banks is the supremum of |S|+(|A|+|B|)/h(A,B). Proof: each numerator counts the sizes of the adjacent banks; half of their sum over consecutive edges counts each interior site exactly once. Pointwise edge bound, add and divide by total height; endpoint terms are bounded since every 10-torus bank has bounded size. Conversely alternate any chosen A,B at that minimum gap. Period 2h of donors and period 2 of S need not be commensurate: they cannot cause a cross collision by the projection assumption and their densities add. Baseline is 72 in these normalized units. This is a sharp reduction within this architecture, not all possible donors (e.g. mixed pitch with clearance depending on axial phase or gaps <1).
