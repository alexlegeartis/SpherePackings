# A primitive 288-site 11D double-triality witness

This constructively attains the prior center-density bound `288/8192=9/256`; it is NOT a new density record. The two-block carrier has period `2Z³×2D4×2D4`. The pinned motif has squared minimum 4 and exactly 16,020 wrapped sharp pairs. Its full translation group is exactly that period, of covolume 8192, because every allowable period shift is a difference of centers in `Z³×D4*×D4*` and only the identity class preserves the 288-site motif. Therefore this is not an enlarged cell for the previous 4096-covolume triality packing. The centers of this particular witness do lie in one rotated integer grid.

The only directions of length two with overlap above 272 motif sites are the six outer axis translations, of rank three. Thus any subpacking invariant under seven, or eleven, independent translations of length two has center density at most `272/8192`; this is an upper bound, not a claim of attainment. A direction outside the center group has zero overlap. Invariance of the subpacking under a period direction places it inside the intersection of the original packing and that translate, so the claim also holds without assuming the subpacking shares the 8192 period.

[Construction and scoped carrier theorems](double_triality.md) give the product-grid versus diagonal-E8 dichotomy, exact two-scale corner, and a two-bank reduction for the stated vertically separated architecture. A density gain would need specifically interacting nonintegral color differences or another architecture. To replay the pinned witness and finite chart assertions, run `python verify.py motif.json`.
