# Rotation refinement: 23/9 for arbitrary orthogonal axes, 5/2 on the entire half-integral family

This strengthens the short 21/8 proof in [the main note](README.md). The old 21/8 remains the exact threshold for its simpler **quarter-coordinate localization**, not the sharp bound asserted for every rotated interface. Here 5/2 is attained by an ordinary all-half translate and maximizes over the family of orthogonal R with 2R integral. It need not be attained for every particular R. For arbitrary orthogonal R our presently certified interval for the supremum of `dist(P,RP+t)^2` is **[5/2,23/9]**, not an exact value.

### Two small exact localization certificates

1. If squared clearance of a point from P exceeds 23/9 then its nearest integer is a B-corner and every coordinate differs from that integer by **strictly less than 1/3**. Cover all non-B boxes and the parts of B boxes with a coordinate ≥1/3 by 47 rational weighted bounds on 13 symmetry-reduced roots, exactly as in the main quarter bound. The equality 23/9 at w_0=w_1=1/3 around a=0 comes from the same pair of coordinates as the quarter example.
2. If squared clearance exceeds 5/2, the nearest integer is a B-corner and lies at **Euclidean distance strictly less than one**. In each non-B box certify the whole box. On each B box certify either maximum squared clearance ≤5/2 or Σu_i²<1 on that entire subbox. The replay over 11 symmetry-reduced roots uses 435 weighted bounds and 164 Euclidean-radius leaves. Ties in coordinate rounding cannot occur: choosing the other equally near corner would change total parity, and a non-B corner is excluded.

[verify_extra.py](verify_extra.py) independently checks both certificates using integers; no numerical optimum makes a leaf pass.

### For clearance greater than 23/9, 2R must be integral

Apply the first certificate to all points of RP+t. For any row r of R the subgroup H of R/Z generated by the entries of 2r has a full coset inside an open arc of length 2/3. Hence H is finite of order one or two. If it has order two, 4r is integral. The residues of r·c as c ranges over C, modulo the subgroup {0,1/2}, must all agree: otherwise their union of cosets gives four equally spaced points which require an arc of length ≥3/4. Thus 4r·(c−c') is even for all C-pairs. Because C affinely spans the odd-parity hyperplane, the ten entries of 4r are all odd or all even. The all-odd option contradicts (4r)·(4r)=16: ten odd squares sum to 2 modulo 8. The all-even option contradicts |H|=2. Thus |H|=1 for each row, so 2R is integral.

### All half-integral rotations, not just a preselected list of twists

Write T=2R integral, TTᵀ=TᵀT=4I, and suppose an interface has squared clearance >5/2. By the second certificate, rounding RP+t gives an injective map to B+2Z^10: two distinct points in the same open unit ball about a corner would be <2 apart. Therefore the rounded intensity is still 40/1024.

For each column v of T the rounded set is invariant under the integer period v. Thus its possible residues lie in B_H={b in B: b+H is contained in B}, with H the column span of T modulo two. In particular |B_H|≥40. Each non-complete-axis column has four entries ±1 and all others zero. Of the 210 binary supports of size four, exactly ten have |B∩(v+B)|≥40: they are precisely unions of two of the five Gray coordinate pairs. The other supports give intersections of size 16 or 32. By reversing the two fibers this applies to the **row** supports as well, because the inverse rotation is Rᵀ. Both tests concern the canonical source and target pentacode coordinates.

Consequently a coordinate in a nonsingleton row or column always activates its Gray partner. On the active coordinate pairs the matrix consists of 2-by-2 all-sign blocks or zeros; each block row and each block column has degree exactly two. Every inactive coordinate has a single entry ±2; these isolated source and target coordinates may be matched in **any** permutation, not necessarily respecting Gray pairs. This form follows from the two support conditions: if one entry of an active block is nonzero, both entries in its column pair and both in its row pair are nonzero. There are k=2,3,4 or 5 active Gray pairs or none (the signed-permutation case, already checked).

At a fixed such T, it suffices to inspect binary corner shift β and one bit ε_j per active row. For the point with numerator Tc, its rounded integer corner modulo 2 equals
`β+ floor((Tc+ε)/2) mod 2`
componentwise: depending on the common fractional part of t_j, a half-integer is rounded down or up. Integral coordinates involve no choice, and all ties are excluded by localization. Varying a source period only adds a column-span translation from H. Therefore, for the matrix to work, one β,ε must place **all 40** residues above inside B_H.

* [triality.cpp](triality.cpp) proves the support count, checks the dihedral symmetries of the five pairs, and exhausts k=2. The active 4×4 matrix is a Hadamard matrix; independent input/output coordinate signs preserve P exactly, so we may make its first row and column positive. The other three rows must be the three signed rows of a Sylvester matrix in any order. For both source and target there are two inequivalent active pair locations under the dihedral symmetries. All 6! inactive matchings, six row orders and 16 rounding signs are tested: 276,480 cases, zero surviving corner translates.
* [half_indices.cpp](half_indices.cpp) covers k=3,4,5. Active triples of five pairs are represented by masks 7 and 11 (complements of the two inequivalent pairs); four are represented by 15 and all five by 31. Enumerate every labelled two-regular bipartite incidence between source and target pairs; the program computes H and B_H before doing any signed search. All 2,040 five-pair frameworks have |B_H|<40. For k≤4 assign signed entries one row at a time, checking all previous row dot products. Coordinate sign symmetries allow previously unseen columns to be made positive in the present row and the first previously encountered column (if any) to be positive after choosing the row sign. All other previously encountered columns are free. This pruning can keep duplicate representatives but loses none. All inactive matchings, active rounding signs, and β with the first word landing in B_H are exhausted. There are 888 normalized higher-block matrices and 503,808 tests; zero surviving translates. For example, if an active column graph has two disjoint four-coordinate Hadamard blocks it is included, not discarded as a compound case.

This rules out >5/2 for the entire T-integral family. The (ordinary, permutation included) all-half translate achieves 5/2. Combined with the circle subgroup reduction above, arbitrary independent orientations have squared horizontal clearance at most **23/9**.

### Numerical consequence and scope

For k=1,2,3 unrestricted-dimensional **normal sites** supporting full independent Best fibers require mutual separation at least √(4−23/9)=√13/3. Thus the center-density ceiling is (40/1024)(6/√13)^k δ*_k.

For ordinary coordinates permuted or twisted by any half-integral isometry **relative between every pair of fibers**, replace √13/3 by √(3/2). The lower 5/2 is a two-fiber example, not a claim that all planar or spatial neighboring pairs can achieve it simultaneously. This is not a global density upper bound, and it does not limit partial shelves by using full-fiber rigidity.

**Irrational rotations close exactly at the ordinary benchmark as an upper bound.** In fact, any orientation with at least one irrational matrix entry has squared interface clearance ≤5/2 as well. For clearance strictly larger, fix a value D strictly between 5/2 and the infimal squared clearance. The D-superlevel set in the compact horizontal 2Z-torus is closed and avoids every coordinate half-integer hyperplane: at a half-integer, either nearest-corner choice is valid, but only one of these opposite-parity choices can belong to B. Thus the superlevel set is uniformly separated from every such hyperplane. An irrational entry of 2R makes the period orbit of that coordinate dense modulo one, a contradiction. The only conceivable improved pair is therefore a genuinely different **rational, non-half-integral rotation**. This is stronger than a numerical failure to find an irrational improvement; it does not assert that any irrational orientation attains 5/2.

The three final squared ratios to the comparators are exactly 100/117, 492075/692224 and 1800/2197.
