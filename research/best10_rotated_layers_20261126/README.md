# 11–13D: independently rotated Best fibers, and the sharp 32-point partial interface

November 26, 2026. No new overall or lattice lower bound. The [rotation refinement](ROTATIONS.md) sharpens the unrestricted clearance upper to 23/9 and proves the optimum 5/2 for half-integral relative rotations; [mixed families](MIXED.md) treat replacement layers. Opening commit `8fb73c3e1ea32885d547695cad3812b8b1512133`: [pinned table](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/blob/8fb73c3e1ea32885d547695cad3812b8b1512133/LOWER_BOUNDS_1_96.md), [exact center-density factors](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/blob/8fb73c3e1ea32885d547695cad3812b8b1512133/research/lower_bounds_1_96_20261122/cohn_inputs.json). Compare 11D and 13D 9/256, and 12D 1/27. All packings below use Euclidean minimum 2 (unit balls), so center density equals number intensity; multiply by unit-ball volume for the occupied fraction. The historical Best code and extension are not claimed as new. The new constraint concerns arbitrary relative orientations, banks of individually free points, and sites in 1–3 extra dimensions, not a repeat of the [fixed translate obstruction](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/tree/8fb73c3e1ea32885d547695cad3812b8b1512133/research/pentacode_fixed_stack_20261125).

**Setup.** Let C be the binary Gray images of all cyclic permutations and signs modulo four of 01112,03110,21310,21132, with Gray symbols 0,1,3,2 in two-bit integer order. The 10D Best packing is P=C+2Z^10. See [Conway–Sloane, 1994](https://neilsloane.com/doc/Me185.pdf) for this 40-word code and its extensions. The 40 words have minimum Hamming distance 4 and the period supplies Euclidean squared minimum 4; covolume is 1024 and intensity is 40/1024. The affine span of C over F2 is the odd-parity affine hyperplane. Write B for the 112 binary corners at Hamming distance 3 from C, all even. The other corners have distance 0,1,2 with counts 40,400,472. [Östergård–Baicheva–Kolev](https://users.aalto.fi/~pat/72.html) gives A(11,4)=A(10,3)=72 and the unique (10,40,4) code up to equivalence.

**Continuous deep-point localization.** If d(x,P)^2>21/8, then x is within 1/4 **strictly in each coordinate** of an integer b whose mod-2 residue belongs to B. To prove it, round to an integer corner a, and let w_i∈[0,1/2] be the coordinate distances. The squared wrapped distance to c+2Z^10 is Σ_i(w_i−s_i)^2 with s_i=(a xor c)_i. To cover the unwanted region, take a full box for each a not in B, and for each a in B the ten boxes with one w_i≥1/4. Thus there are 2,032 initial boxes; 320 directly verified affine code symmetries reduce this to just 13 representatives. On a box [l,u], the attached certificate gives positive integer weights p_c of total H. Then the minimum of the 40 distances is bounded by their indicated weighted mean. The latter is a sum of univariate convex quadratics, each maximized at an endpoint. Writing h_i=Σ_{s_i=1}p_c, the resulting upper is H^−1 Σ_i max_{z=l_i,u_i} ((H−h_i)z²+h_i(1−z)²). All 33 leaves of the 13 binary splitting trees have upper ≤21/8 by exact integer comparison. The short [verifier](verify.py) does not call an optimizer or trust floating point. Its 2.4 KB certificate is [here](certificate.json). This cutoff for localization is itself sharp: at a=0 the missing pair of coordinates 0,1 belongs to no common nearest triple; taking w_0=w_1=1/4 and other w_i=0 gives distance²=3−1/2+1/8=21/8. This sharpness does NOT establish a pair of full fibers at that clearance.

**Full fibers, arbitrary relative orthogonal orientations.** For every orthogonal R and translate t, the infimum of squared distances between P and RP+t is at most 21/8. If it were larger, every point of RP+t would have all coordinates within the open quarter interval about an integer. For row j, vary a pure translation from 2R Z^10. The subgroup of R/Z generated by that row of 2R would have a whole coset inside an open arc of length 1/2. A nontrivial finite subgroup coset cannot fit, by equal spacing (even order two cannot); an infinite subgroup is dense. Thus 2R is integral. Fractional parts of row j of Rc+t_j vary by multiples of 1/2, so also cannot differ: 2R_j·(c−c') is even for all c,c' in C. The mod-2 row of 2R is therefore 0 or all ones by the affine span. All ones are impossible: a row of 2R has squared length 4, less than 10. Thus R is integral orthogonal, hence a signed permutation. Rounding RP+t now commutes with translating its integer sites. Its rounded 40 binary residues form an isometric copy of C inside B. Concatenating C with last bit 0 and this copy with last bit 1 gives a binary (11,80,4) code, impossible. No common period assumption was used.

If R is restricted to signed coordinate permutations, the exact best squared clearance is 5/2. Signs act trivially on binary residue codes. For each coordinate permutation σ form D=C xor σC. At a∈D use the matched corner (cost ≤10/4). Otherwise [the replay](permutations.cpp) verifies four words d∈D with pairwise disjoint mismatch supports a xor d. Averaging the four costs is at most Σ_i(w_i²+(1−2w_i)/4)≤10/4. The replay verifies a 320-element affine code symmetry group and covers all 10! coordinate permutations by 90 double-coset representatives (50,268 nontrivial corner checks); translations of D just relabel corners. The all-half horizontal shift realizes 5/2. For unrestricted R we claim the upper 21/8 and lower 5/2, not equality.

**Consequence in 11, 12 and 13 dimensions.** Let Y be an arbitrary set in R^k, k=1,2,3, and at each y take a full 10-dimensional fiber R_y P+t_y in the common horizontal 10-plane, with unrelated orthogonal orientations and offsets allowed. If the union has separation 2, the vertical sites have mutual distance at least h=√(4−21/8)=√(11/8), since the horizontal infimum is at most 21/8 for each pair. The center density is bounded by (40/1024)(2/h)^k δ*_k, where δ*_k is the optimal k-dimensional **center density**, namely 1/2, 1/(2√3), 1/(4√2). This uses the exact circle- and three-dimensional sphere-packing theorems, not a lattice-only tail restriction ([Cohn's primary-source index](https://cohn.mit.edu/sphere-packing/), [Hales et al.](https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2017.1)). Rotated periodic copies of P have the same point count per horizontal cube volume up to a uniform boundary error O(L^9) by tiling with their fixed-diameter rotated cells. Tail bounds likewise apply uniformly in large boxes by repeating a finite pattern with a separating buffer. Counting on products and tiling Euclidean balls by large cubes gives the same asymptotic upper density; no common horizontal orientation, period or tail lattice is needed.

**Sharp capacity if you discard the second complete fiber.** For every squared horizontal clearance D in [11/4,3], any 2-separated set Q in R^10 satisfying d(q,P)^2≥D for all q∈Q has upper number intensity at most **32/1024**, and this is attained. Q can be aperiodic, unaligned and otherwise arbitrary. Above 3 no single point is possible.

Proof: The localization already puts w_i<1/4. For an a∈B let S_a be supports of its nearest codewords (triples), define σ=max_{s∈S_a}Σ_{i∈s}w_i and r=‖w‖. Then d(x,P)^2≤3−2σ+r². The graph joining coordinate pairs occurring together in a triple has minimum degree ≥6 and independence number ≤2, as exactly checked at all 112 corners. The fractional stable-set polytope z_i≥0, z_i+z_j≤1 on graph edges has only half-integral vertices: the subgraph of tight pairs among strictly fractional coordinates has an odd cycle in every connected component at a vertex (otherwise alternating signs permit a small perturbation); hence all these coordinates equal 1/2. Vertices with no ones have squared norm ≤10/4, those with one have at most three nonzero halves, and with two ones all other coordinates vanish. Maximize the convex squared norm on vertices. Since z=w/σ is feasible, σ≥r√(2/5); the zero case is immediate. For r∈[1/4,√10/4] the convex function 3−2√(2/5)r+r² is strictly below 11/4: check each endpoint using √(2/5)>5/8; the right endpoint is 21/8. Thus any such x is within Euclidean distance <1/4 of its B-corner. For 0<r<1/4 that bound is strictly below 3 as well; B itself attains 3. This establishes the exact covering radius and deepest-hole set of this particular P as a byproduct, without priority claim.

Rounding each q therefore injects Q into B+2Z^10, and the rounded points have squared separation ≥4. Indeed equal or squared-distance-2 rounded points would put their actual sites at distance <√2+1/2<2; odd squared integer distances are impossible by parity. Partition integer lattice points into axis-aligned blocks 2z+{0,1}^10. Each such block contains ≤32 rounded sites: append last bit 1 and append bit 0 on the block's 40 reference words in C; the combined binary code has d≥4, hence at most 72. Bounded rounding displacements preserve the upper number density bound. The 32-word witness in verify.py consists of B-corners at pairwise Hamming distance ≥4, periodically repeated modulo 2Z^10, and attains the bound across the entire interval. Its union at odd heights with P at even heights is a periodic **nonlattice** 11D packing: horizontal fibers have different intensities. Horizontal self-periods supply min²=4; adjacent heights add 1 to a horizontal cost ≥3, and heights at least two apart already supply 4. A 2Z^11 cell has 40+32=72 marks and volume 2048, center density 9/256 and occupied (9/256)π^(11/2)/Γ(13/2)≈0.0662380270098011. This tie is consistent with the historical optimal binary code classification.
