# Mixed Best-frame families

Companion to the [independent-rotation and sharp interface theorem](README.md). These constructions allow discarding and relocating sites in every other sheet in 11D and in two thirds of the sheets in a 12D trial. The accompanying bounds do not require a replacement sheet to sit on any discrete grid.

Use normalized units with minimum distance 2. At the wider spacings explicitly indicated below we use the assumption that the replacement sheets have number intensity ≤40/1024, **uniformly** in large horizontal cubes and across the chosen sheets. This includes arbitrary subsets of independently isometric Best fibers and nonbinary competitors at or below that intensity with a uniform boundary control. It is not asserted that every 10D packing obeys that bound. If there is no 10D packing denser than Best, the uniform bound with o(1) boundary error follows for every 2-separated replacement by repeating a finite cube with a buffer of width 2. A violation of that uniform bound along a sequence of large cubes would itself furnish a packing denser than Best in 10D.

**11D, freely spaced alternating sheets.** Put independently isometric full Best sheets at alternating positions on an arbitrary locally finite ordered family of parallel hyperplanes. Between them allow any nonempty 2-separated replacement sheet under the uniform bound just described. Consecutive planes have gap h≥1, since the first sheet's covering radius is √3. If 1≤h≤√5/2, the required squared horizontal clearance is ≥11/4, hence the pair supplies at most 40+32=72 sites per 1024 horizontal volume. This gives at most 72/(2048 h)≤9/256 per pair counted with half weight at each endpoint. If h≥√5/2, 40+40 suffices because (40/1024)/(√5/2)<9/256 (squared ratios give 320/324). Adding these inequalities over the intervals counts every interior plane once; boundary planes cause vanishing error. The proof permits two full sheets next to each other, too, if every adjacent pair contains a full one. Extremal density is **exactly 9/256**, attained by the 40+32 witness at unit gaps. Thus varying all rotations, spacings and all sites of these replacement planes cannot outperform that 11D benchmark unless the replacements admit a separate 10D density improvement.

**12D triangular normal grid with two freely reconstructed sheets per full sheet.** Take a planar triangular grid with edge h; select one color of its standard three-coloring for complete P10c sheets. These sheets have unrestricted, independent orientations and translations parallel to the common horizontal ten-plane. Allow independent nonempty replacements (as above) at the two other colors. Average over translates of one unit equilateral triangle so each grid color is represented once. The area per normal site is h²√3/2. Write N for the maximum average mark count per 1024 horizontal volume, averaged over the three triangle vertices. Necessarily h≥1.

* If 1≤h²≤17/16, all points of both replacements have squared clearance ≥47/16 from the same full vertex. By the proof in the README they have r=distance to a deep corner <1/4, and their clearance is bounded by 3−2√(2/5)r+r². On [1/16,1/4] this function is decreasing and its value at 1/16 is strictly below 47/16 (use √(2/5)>5/8). Hence all round within <1/16. Across the two receivers, horizontal distance is at least √(47/16), greater than √2+1/8; within each receiver it is at least 2. Thus the two sets **jointly** round injectively to even deep corners whose mutual squared distance is at least 4. In each binary cube their combined population is ≤32 by adjoining the 40 reference words. So N≤(40+32)/3=24; center density ≤48/(1024√3)=0.027063294….
* If 17/16≤h²≤5/4, each replacement separately has clearance ≥11/4, hence N≤104/3. Center density ≤(104/3)·32/(17·1024√3)=0.036791929….
* If h²≥5/4, simply N≤40, giving center density ≤320/(5·1024√3)=0.036084392….

All three upper bounds are strictly below the 12D Coxeter–Todd center density 1/27. This gives the constructive decision: if retaining even one full Best bank per such triangle, rechoosing its two neighbors independently, shortening their motifs, twisting their axes or uniformly changing the triangle size cannot yield a 12D record without beating Best in a horizontal 10D packing first. The 12D maximum in this family has not been determined; this analysis does not rule out nontriangular or nonuniform normal meshes or a partial reconstruction of all three banks.
