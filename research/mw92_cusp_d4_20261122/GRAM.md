# Explicit integral Gram basis and exact replay

The algebraic description in README.md defines the lattice without choosing
a Mordell–Weil basis. The supplementary file `gram96.json.gz` also supplies
an explicit integer Gram and exact source-to-basis arithmetic. It contains
210 pairs of polynomial section coordinates, encoded over
`F47[ω]/(ω²+ω+1)` as coefficient a+47b for a+bω, with coefficients
in increasing degree order. It also records the 92 independent source
indices, the full rational horizontal basis in those coordinates, the
integral coordinates of all 210 generators in that basis, the corresponding
two-column cusp map modulo 47, and the basis of the index-47² gluing kernel.

Each recorded polynomial pair satisfies the defining equation identically,
and degrees ≤16 and ≤24 show it is disjoint from O in every minimal chart.
Thus each has height 16. For two distinct such sections P,Q, the finite
intersection divisor has degree deg gcd(x_P−x_Q,y_P−y_Q): at each finite
place their intersection ideal on the graph of a section is generated by
the two coordinate differences. Even over the algebraic closure a section
cannot meet the singular point of a simple cusp. Using the charts at
infinity, the infinity contribution is the smaller of
16−deg(x_P−x_Q) and 24−deg(y_P−y_Q), taking an identically zero difference
to have infinite vanishing order. The sum gives P·Q. With all fibres
irreducible, Shioda's height formula is then ⟨P,Q⟩=8−P·Q. Coincident
sections have the known diagonal 16; opposite sections are explicitly
recognized and assigned −16. No infinity degree is read from a zero
polynomial.

The exact replay reconstructs this complete Gram of 210 sections,
checks the rational basis changes, and verifies with row HNF that these
integral rows span all 92 horizontal basis vectors. Its determinant
equals the full Mordell–Weil determinant 47¹⁴, so the source subgroup is
the full lattice. It verifies all cusp values on those generating rows
directly over the finite field. The glue basis has determinant 47² in
the orthogonal product coordinates and every row satisfies the displayed
colour equality. The final Gram determinant is 4·47¹⁸.

Replay (the optional coordinate replay requires python-flint 0.9.0):

    python research/mw92_cusp_d4_20261122/verify.py
    python research/mw92_cusp_d4_20261122/verify_gram.py

`build_basis.py` is historical provenance and is not needed to reproduce
the certificate. It uses seeded NumPy generation, exact HNF, and FLINT LLL;
rebuilding with different versions may choose a different, isometric basis.
The frozen replay performs no randomized enumeration or reduction.
