# Scoped sphere-packing results, 23 November 2026

These are exact statements about specified construction families. They do not change any of the record tables and are not global upper bounds.

- [10–11D binary exceptional-root periods](10-11-binary-exceptional/README.md): exact binary-family maxima and a strict local nonlattice 11D packing, even when the finite period and sites can move.
- [10–12D continuous Dₙ periods](10-12-continuous-dn/README.md): high-minimum obstructions for arbitrary real motifs, with exact capacity at inherited minimum in dimensions 2–16. [Shortened Hamming motifs](10-12-continuous-dn/HAMMING_LOCAL.md) also give a separate fixed-period local rigidity theorem.
- [12D coupled E₆ periods](12-e6-coupled/README.md): matches the Coxeter–Todd density over arbitrary positive two-slot metrics for binary motifs and ternary additive motifs; four nonlinear ternary masks are not excluded.
- [48D and 72D real-quadratic trace twists](48-72-trace-twists/README.md): full-family matched bounds, stronger than a small-strain result, with an exact conditional criterion for a 96D improvement. No qualifying 96D parent is known from this search.
- [96D Craig-type lattices and motifs](96-craig/README.md): classification under translations and square dilations; exclusion of 43 of the 48 resulting Legendre metric classes, together with separate phase and small-index obstructions. Five Legendre classes are not covered.

The replay sources are beside their respective notes. The benchmark tables are unchanged.
