# 81-dimensional Mordell–Weil half-phase lattice

The lattice is generated by the rows of an 80-dimensional Mordell–Weil lattice
of Gram G, embedded at last coordinate zero, and by (r/2,2), with r given
in small_rep.txt. Rows of the Gram multiplied by 4, and all exact constants,
are in candidate81.json. The minimum squared is 14, the Gram determinant is
4*41^12, and the center density is (7/2)^(81/2)/(2*41^6). This is 3.7105252163%
over the previous center density 2^40. The occupied density is at least `2.98455470421258e-17`, improving both the earlier unrestricted and lattice-only bounds in this repository. The named derived MW80 × A1 comparison is retained separately. This construction is one lattice, not merely periodic.

## Parent and minimum
The elliptic curve over F_(41^2)(t) is y²=x³+t^42+1. The elliptic-surface
Euler characteristic is 7, and t^42+1 has 42 simple zeros and no bad fibre
at infinity. The finite bad fibres are irreducible type II. Thus the height
formula gives 14+2(P.O) for a nonzero section P, at least 14. The literal
sections are integral and attain height 14. verify_parent.py checks the
field, polynomial sections, heights and all pairwise Gram entries directly;
it verifies saturation relative to the literal generating set by HNF and the
Gram determinant 41^12. No assertion that these sections generate the full
Mordell–Weil group is required for this packing. For the height pairing,
see Schütt and Shioda, https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.0298.

## Exact deep-point certificate
The nonzero phase r mod 2 is the row numbered 5 in newlabels.txt, packed
little-endian in two limbs (low 64 bits first). A directly checked
representative of the parity r has norm 40, and all vectors of that parity have squared
norm congruent to zero modulo 4. We exclude squared norm <=36.

The checked Gram isometry T has complementary orthogonal integral
rank-40 sublattices S_+, S_- in its +1 and -1 eigenspaces. Both contain the
corresponding (I+T)L, (I-T)L, respectively, and their mod-2 images share
a rank-38 intersection containing r. If y=a0+2z is of parity r, choose
a0 in S_+ of parity r. Write y=a+b with a=a0+(I+T)z, b=(I−T)z.
The half summands always have parity in the common intersection. This
shared-parity sublattice of each half is its Gram radical mod 2:
containment follows by orthogonality, and the ranks coincide. Each
radical has all squared norms multiples of 4, checked on its Gram diagonal
and off-diagonal. Each nonzero half summand thus has squared norm at least
16 by the parent minimum. If both are nonzero and their sum is at most
36, one of them must be 16 and the other at most 20. The pure cases are
also checked. An independently checkable parent isometry swaps halves;
the complete exact set of norm-16 vectors in one half, up to sign, is
transported to the other. Sign does not alter parity.

The enumerator takes rational LDL data and coordinate bounds from ksetup.py
and uses outward fixed-point bounds (all pruning comparisons integer).
For each coordinate, using scale S=2^20, a certified lower bound on the LDL
term is floor(S*d)*max(0, abs(S*z-c)-error)^2/S^3, where error bounds the
sum of contributions from the rounded-off LDL coefficients. Full leaves
are evaluated using the integral Gram. The two mixed searches test the
opposite-half parity y−a for every norm-16 a, using one parity trie.
Prefixes get the maximum cutoff of their descendant labels. A radical
parity has norm invariant modulo 8 throughout its half parity coset;
for common parity vectors in opposing halves the sum of norms is 0 mod 8,
since their half-sum belongs to the even parent lattice. These congruences
permit shrinking cutoffs; assertions and direct checks are included.

The squared torus depth is consequently 10. At last coordinate 0 the
parent minimum is 14; at last coordinate ±2 the squared distance is at
least 10+4=14; at all other nonzero last coordinates it is at least 16.
Taking the Gram determinant by a Schur complement gives the value above.

## Independent input audit

The supplementary `validate81.py` checks the rounded LDL inputs and their coordinate bounds with exact rational arithmetic, the determinant and a literal attaining witness, and the index-four shared-parity eigensublattices. Run it after extracting the archive and its `ksetup.py` has produced the four `.in` files: `python3 validate81.py /path/to/extracted/archive`. The main `replay.sh` does the all-translate enumeration; this input check is separate.
