# Sphere-packing research round 19: exact scope closures

No density catalogue values changed. The proofs are for the
stated constructions and relaxations, not unrestricted packing optima.

* [92D translation-invariant cusp sublattices](92-mw-cusp-index3/README.md):
  all **106,080** index-\(47^3\) sublattices contain a literal
  nonzero squared norm at most 20, too short to improve the parent.
  The [fourth-jet continuation](92-mw-cusp-index3/FOURTH.md)
  witnesses **3,665,047 of 4,985,761** index-\(47^4\) cases, with
  squared norms at most 22. This does not certify any survivor.
* [48D/96D prime-square Mordell–Weil twists](48-96-prime-power-mw/README.md):
  exact integral Frobenius line orbits give the regulator cost
  \(v_5(\det)=48\) and \(v_7(\det)=96\); short sections make
  the five standard CM twists at \((p,q)=(5,25),(7,49)\)
  uncompetitive. See the [three other twists](48-96-prime-power-mw/OTHER_TWISTS.md).
* [12D heavy radical packets](12-e6-heavy-packets/README.md):
  exact joint triangle moments reduce the equal-scale \(E_6\times E_6\)
  cap for wholly heavy-packet motifs from 1215 to 972. The
  923-site target is still open for arbitrary motifs; actual single-coordinate
  fibres have size at most three.
* [48D three exceptional weights](48-tensor-three-weights/README.md):
  arbitrary half-period motifs in the zero-sum \(E_8\) carrier
  have center density at most 16,800 in the stated nonnegative and
  all-light positive-definite sectors. Further analytic
  [negative-heavy tails](48-tensor-three-weight-tails/README.md)
  leave a bounded mixed-weight range open.
* [85D Sakamoto charts](85-sakamoto-all-normal-types/README.md):
  every motif in 52 explicit low-determinant charts has at most
  80% of BN85 density. There is one embedding of each admissible
  Gram type in each parent, not a classification of all embeddings.
  The [universal \(2A_3\) check](85-universal-2a3/README.md) separately
  excludes six of seven index-two additive motifs and the full phase
  group in every chart of that type in any relevant even unimodular parent.
* [96D golden three-shell tests](96-golden-three-shell/README.md):
  the earlier rational histogram fails exact cross-shell
  positivity, but a replacement survives 1,953 joint marked shell
  rows and 221 additional affine-unit rows. These are formal
  histograms, not lattices or a disproof of existence.
* [Additive deformations](48-96-additive-deformations/README.md):
  a separate uniform Cartier-index argument excludes four of the five
  CM families for every separable linearized degree-25 or degree-49
  base polynomial. The cubic 48D deformation remains open.
