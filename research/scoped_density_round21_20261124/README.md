# Sphere density research, round 21

**Exact scoped results, not new unrestricted or lattice records.** The
opening record comparator is public main at
`a297bb7cdeae548b9cd14e6a33ead7514f2707bf`.

- [Complete MW92 index-47^5 closure](92-mw-complete-index5).
  Two independently verified norm-24 rows from affine closest-vector
  searches close both residual orbits. All 234,330,768
  translation-invariant sublattices at this index have at most 55.0%
  of the parent density. An incidence count also excludes many
  non-invariant sublattices of indices 47^5, 47^6 and 47^7 containing
  the common depth-five lattice.
- [Universal MW92 duality](92-mw-universal-dual).
  A single order-four isometry maps every invariant profile to its
  canonical dual partner, via an alternating balanced linking
  form. At index 47^16, all such sublattices are 47-modular,
  not merely the stem.
- [Partial MW92 sixth-jet closure](92-mw-cusp-index6).
  All mixed types and enough primitive types to give 2,897,035,069
  total exclusions, out of 11,013,546,097 invariant profiles.
- [48D E8 tensors](48-e8-three-exceptions-complete).
  The remaining two-heavy negative-slot case closes the entire
  ordered three-exception family at center density 16,800,
  including nonlinear half-period motifs. The distinct
  [four-exception package](48-e8-four-exceptions-scopes) covers
  specific determinant regions and a heavy-weight cube, not the
  full four-exception family.
- [12D equal-scale ternary E6×E6](12-e6-marked-packet-obstructions).
  All motifs mixing only packet occupancies 4–8 have at most 922
  sites, under the 923 needed to improve Coxeter–Todd. If only
  sizes 4–9 occur, any improvement requires 103–117 occupied
  packets, including at least 23 full ones. Other masks remain open.
- [Cyclic torus depths](48-96-cyclic-torsion-modular).
  The residue theorem rules out an isotropic cyclic palette whose
  nonzero phases are all as deep as n/12 at every positive-genus
  modular level. Additional gonality and low-level arithmetic
  refinements, and a finite constructive P48n scout, are included.
