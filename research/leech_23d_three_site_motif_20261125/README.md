## Two constructive searches made finite
Let K have Gram [[a,c],[c,b]] in a primitive Leech two-plane, determinant D, and section L=Leech∩K⊥. The D discriminant group glues are labeled by K*/K. With ν(s)=D·dist(s,K)^2, their exact guaranteed horizontal squared separation is at least max(0,4−ν(s)/D) for s≠0; equal section translates can coincide. Give each helix period a vertical spacing h and a horizontal phase advance g in the finite group. To replace the discarded two directions with one, choose M motif phases r_i with freely chosen vertical times T_i=h_i sqrt(D). At target center density δ the available scaled period is H=M(μ/4)^(23/2)/δ. Write W(0)=sqrt(Dμ), W(s)=min(W(0),sqrt(max(0,ν(s)−D(4−μ)))). All same-helix differences must obey W(jg)≤jH. For each ordered pair i<j, d=r_j−r_i, the feasible time gap interval is [max_{k≥0}(W(d+kg)−kH), min_{k≥1}(kH−W(d−kg))] intersected with [0,H]. All k beyond 1+sqrt(Dμ)/H are automatic. These are ordinary difference inequalities and can be checked exactly at a candidate, without a generic packing optimizer. A second construction fixes the per-layer spacing but permits the packet of up to three cosets to change at every layer without a prescribed period.
