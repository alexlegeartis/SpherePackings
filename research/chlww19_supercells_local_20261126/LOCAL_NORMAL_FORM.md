# Exact local normal form of the 19D CHLWW packing

Companion to the sharp 300-phase and arbitrary doubled-period theorem. This is not an improved best density.

**Theorem (original six-fiber cell).** Fix the minimum-distance normalization. Allow arbitrary nonsingular strain of the original horizontal period and six independently displaced real motif positions in its deformed cell. In a neighborhood of the published CHLWW 19D packing, no such perturbation improves density. Up to a global isometry, every packing in this neighborhood having density at least the original is exactly a uniform relative translation of the triangles {0,4,5} and {1,2,3}. In particular its only nontrivial local degrees of freedom at that density are the 19 just constructed. The inradius of the fully certified translation ball about the published placement is sqrt(211/60)-sqrt(208/60). This local statement is NOT claimed for arbitrary enlarged cells.

**Contact data.** Use the basis/scale from the main round-108 note. For shortest vectors of the Leech lattice with one exact normal z (one of the six actual differences of the active pairs), there are precisely 2055 minimum horizontal differences h of length squared 208/60 each. At each of the six choices, their unweighted sum is zero, and they span the 19-dimensional horizontal space. These identities concern each oriented pair individually, not an unjustified average over all six fibers.

More strongly, for the single pair with normal z=(0,0,0,1,0), there are 9 strictly positive integer weights (repeated by the supplied word of 2055 group labels), for which the weighted sum of h is zero and the weighted sum of hhᵀ is a positive multiple of the identity on the horizontal space. The linear and quadratic functions h ↦ (h,h hᵀ) on this one cloud have full evaluation rank 19+190=209 over the rationals. The rank certificate is exact Gaussian elimination modulo the prime 1009, so full modular rank is sufficient. The other five clouds need only their ordinary zero first moment and full linear rank. The second moment is checked in the 19 coordinate positions listed in the script; their restriction to the horizontal space is an isomorphism by full linear rank. In the program physical h = H/(60 sqrt(8)) and |H|²=99840. The numerator of 120 times the orthogonal projector is 120 I - Bᵀ M B. The positive weights divided by common denominator 27360000 reproduce the target uniform average 2055*99840*P/19 in the H units, not merely a floating near-fit.

**Proof of local normal form.** Remove whole-space rotations by polar decomposition and describe a small deformation by its symmetric linear strain A (or Gram perturbation) and six independent translations of motif representatives. To first order each old active directed contact obeys hᵀAh+h·(v_j−v_i) ≥ 0 if minimum separation is not reduced; an immaterial common positive factor is suppressed. Nonincreasing covolume gives tr(A) ≤ 0. Apply the strictly positive weights solely to the first pair: its linear term vanishes while its quadratic term gives a positive constant times tr(A). Hence tr(A)=0 and every summand for this pair is zero. Full quadratic-linear evaluation rank forces A=0 and v_j−v_i=0 for that pair. For any of the other five active pairs this leaves h·(v_j−v_i)≥0 with zero unweighted sum; full linear span forces v_j−v_i=0. The six paired edges form exactly the two triangles. Thus infinitesimal density-preserving motions consist only of a translation on each triangle.

This infinitesimal conclusion gives the actual *local* statement, not just a first-order obstruction. Suppose otherwise and take a sequence of normalized nonworse packings tending to the original but not given by those translations and rotations. For the analysis of the active constraints, subtract one fiber's displacement in each of the two triangles (two independent common shifts), which leaves every active contact exactly unchanged. Cross-triangle distances have positive slack in the original, hence those subtractions are also locally admissible. Divide the remaining strain and four relative translations by their joint norm, and pass to a convergent subsequence. The limiting derivatives satisfy all the constraints above and have norm one, contradicting their vanishing. Pure relative translations are indeed exact finite motions in the displayed 19-ball, with density unchanged. This proves the result modulo global scale and motions; with radius fixed no scaling freedom remains.

**Finite-supercell first-order corollary only.** In any fixed finite enlargement of the period, average every active-contact inequality over its source-cell translations. For each pair and offset h the endpoint averages give the same two average fiber velocities, independent of h. The preceding stress and rank argument then forces zero macroscopic first-order strain and equal fiber-averaged velocities within each triangle. This does not assert nonlinear rigidity, or fixation of every individually translated point, in such an enlarged cell.
