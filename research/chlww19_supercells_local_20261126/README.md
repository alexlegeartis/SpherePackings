# Exact 19D CHLWW supercell ceiling and off-grid slides

The constructive target was to beat Chen–Hu–Li–Wang–Wu (2025), https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02394v1#S3.SS4, not a number attributed to Alex or to an updated table. Their 19-dimensional center density is 6/sqrt(300) * (208/240)^(19/2) = 0.088958102129..., occupied 0.00414736914586272... Their six fibers have two independent contact triangles, which motivates exact supercell and real-shift tests.

**Theorem.** Let U be the five-plane of the 19D construction in the article, K the displayed rank-five Leech section, V its orthogonal space, L the section of the Leech lattice in V, N its projection to V. For every index-one or index-two sublattice L' of L and every union P of arbitrary cosets of L' in N, the center density is at most the CHLWW value; it is attained by their six cosets of L. No restriction on the packing radius, motif size, or shape of that index-two subgroup. Does not cover higher-index periods, general subsets of N, other planes, or arbitrary real point motion.

**Proof and replay outline.** Represent the Leech lattice at scale 1/sqrt(8) by its standard Golay construction: even numerator shell containing 4D24 and doubled Golay octads, odd coset of (-3,1,...,1). The script uses cyclic extended Golay generator 0xae3 and permutes two four-intersecting octads to the first twelve positions so both octad normals in the article are present. The 5 by 24 numeric rows B here are the article’s numerators at scale 1/sqrt(2). Its five-dimensional Gram K has inverse M/60 with integral M as in the article. The group N/L is isomorphic to Z^5/K Z^5 and has 300 elements: take z_i = <w,B_i/sqrt(2)>; norm-four shell enumeration attains z=e_i, making the map onto and proving primitivity. Label by M z mod 60. The least normal squared norm is z'Mz/60 for a minimal representative. All candidate representatives of norm <=3 have |z_i|<=sqrt(12) since normal basis squared norms are 4; the script enumerates [-4,4]^5 and finds every minimum <=3. The script verifies all 196560 norm-four Leech vectors by their three classical types. If any vector of norm four has a given nonzero class, its normal is minimal, by subtracting K. Horizontal squared minimum times 60 is then 240 - z'Mz. Exactly 18 classes are absent; their minimum is 360 - z'Mz by evenness and explicit norm-six witnesses (sums of norm-four vectors with dot product -1). In each of those classes, at the same least normal, differences of the norm-six witnesses span the full 19-dimensional binary space L/2L. Consequently neither sheet of an index-two refinement can increase their minimum. When the class equals zero, index-two periods still have a norm-four horizontal vector: among 4(e_i-e_j), 4(e_j-e_k), 4(e_i-e_k) on three of the last twelve coordinates, one is in the parity kernel. The radius is therefore bounded independently of the motif.

For the remaining nonzero classes take all norm-four vectors with one fixed minimal normal z. The affine F2-span of their integral Leech-basis coordinates, projected to L/2L by subtracting the first vector, determines whether an index-two character on L sees shortest neighbors in both sheets. L is primitive, so all such characters extend to the ambient free abelian lattice. A systematic binary Golay basis plus elementary generators of 4D24 and its odd coset provide exact coordinates in a 24-element integral basis as generated in the script. Select the 19 pivot columns of one full-rank difference span in L; masks on these coordinates are representatives of all 524287 nonzero characters without duplication. Rank-deficient nearest sets give 19 distinct annihilator subspaces. Their joint membership vectors over the 524287 masks assume only 31 patterns.

For each of these 31, make a conservative weighted compatibility graph: a phase may be single (weight 1) or double (weight 2). For two phases known already separated both modes are compatible. If their original separation falls below the target, but the nearest vectors attain an odd and an even charge, both modes are incompatible. In the one-charge case allow the single-single edge without checking signs, never a double because one occupied copy would necessarily clash. This upper relaxation only changes at the 20 relevant distinct values h among the unrefined squared distances times 60. Use the largest h on each interval of constant graph, since density at fixed size increases with radius. Winning target is the least integer g such that g^2 h^19 > 144*208^19, equivalently g > 12 (208/h)^(19/2). Ignore h for which the target exceeds 600. Exact branch/greedy-independent-color upper bounds exclude 612 of the 620 graph thresholds directly. For the 8 remaining (signature, threshold) pairs, restore the binary sheet signs. If minimal normals of the two phases are z_i,z_j and that of their difference k is z_k, correct the first witness by the K-vector with integral coefficients M(z_i-z_j-z_k)/60. Represent each of the 300 fibers by a lift of extended character zero, by adding a suitable element of L; its other sheet then has charge one. The character of the corrected witness is therefore the unique short parity when the nearest set is monochromatic. The other parity is allowed arbitrarily (a safe upper, no unproved estimate about the next shell). Translation lets one fix the zero vertex both for the weighted graph (try single and double) and for the signed graph. At h=240 the 316 exceptional characters all have signed graph maximum 2 (four needed); at h=208 the unique exceptional character has maximum 12 (13 needed). At every other case the relaxed graph already proves the target impossible. The independently known stock meets the bound.

**Practical result:** no new density record, but the original construction cannot be improved by arbitrary rearrangement within any doubled period, including the two disconnected contact triangles. A successful change needs a different projected lattice, at least an index-three period, or non-grid motions. A parity splitting heuristic restricted to points from the first ambient shell would have missed 18 whole classes.

## Positive escape with no density loss: uncountably many noncongruent 19D record-density packings

Let the six horizontal fibers of the published CHLWW packing be numbered 0 through 5, for normal phases 0 and the five dual basis elements. The minimum horizontal squared separation is 208/60. The pairs with this minimum are exactly the edges of triangles {0,4,5} and {1,2,3}. The cross-triangle pair minima are either 211/60 or 219/60; at least one actually equals 211/60. These are exact, because all the normal differences of squared lengths 21/60, 29/60 and 32/60 occur above norm-four Leech vectors. Thus one whole triangle (its three infinite lattice fibers together) can be translated relative to the other by ANY vector of length at most sqrt(211/60)-sqrt(208/60) = 0.01337908470216842, in all 19 horizontal dimensions. The triangle inequality preserves every cross contact; an intratriangle contact remains unchanged. The guaranteed ball is sharp about the published position: move directly toward an actually closest intertriangle pair. The period L is unchanged and the center density stays exactly CHLWW's. Generic shifts leave the projected Leech grid, rather than simply enlarging its period: one stationary point can be fixed at the origin, so a single generic moving point cannot belong to the countable group N.

There are uncountably many isometry classes, not merely different coordinates of the same packing. For a fixed member P and any congruent member QP+t of this family, the same L remains a period, so Q^{-1}L is contained in the discrete, countable period group of P. Images of a basis of L thus give at most countably many Q, and 0 is a center of every member, hence at most countably many t for each Q. A fixed packing also has only countably many parameters realizing it, since a specified moving point determines the parameter and must belong to that countable packing. Consequently a countable set of isometry classes could not cover a 19-dimensional ball of parameters. This is a record tie, not a record break; no priority claim for this degeneracy. The distinction is useful: refinements internal to the discrete grid fail, but off-grid there is a genuine 19-dimensional exact starting space.
