# Exact obstruction to mixing two periodic QR17 sources

No new 17D packing record. Only the following two-source family is covered at the specified minimum distance.

Set D17={z∈Z^17: Σz_i even} and period 4D17. A sheet t is the choice of 4t e1 modulo this period, t∈{0,1}. Let Q={1,2,4,8,9,13,15,16} in Z/17, N its complement among nonzero residues. A and B are the F2-spans of cyclic shifts of the indicator masks of Q,N, respectively. Each space has weight enumerator 1+68 z^6+85 z^8+68 z^10+34 z^12. Directly A⊥B; both have dimension 8, so the dual of A is B+⟨1⟩, and vice versa, of minimum 5. These facts can be regenerated by starting with {0} and adjoining each cyclic mask g via S←S∪(g+S), checking the 256×256 dot products. A QR17 source has centers 2c (c∈A), and 1+2d+4e1 (d∈B). Equal-phase distance squared for distinct binary words in a bank is 4d_H (a coordinate ±2 can be rounded either way, correcting the sheet freely). Opposite phase has squared distance 17 or 25 depending on sheet parity; here it is always 25. Same-site period minimum is 32. Thus 512 centers, minimum squared 24. Generalize by arbitrary coordinate permutations/signed coordinate isometries, integral offsets, and affinely shifted paired binary banks and sheet labels provided each source is still feasible. For density comparison covol(4D17)=2·4^17, center density is M·6^(17/2)/(2·4^17); threshold M>521.9684309361, incumbent M=512 reaches 98.0902234033% of 1/16.

Theorem. The union of any two such QR17 sources permits at most 512 mutually separated centers at squared distance 24, even after an arbitrary further relative perturbation of Euclidean norm <√24−√20≈0.42684. This excludes all pairwise switches in this family; it does not obstruct arbitrary 17D periodic constructions, 3+ sources, or arbitrary real shifts outside that neighborhood.

Metric fact for cross points whose phase masks disagree in m coordinates: if the binary labels at the other r=17−m coordinates differ in h>0 coordinates, then exact toric squared distance is m+4h (one ±2 ambiguity repairs sheet parity). For h=0 and m>0 the metric is m or m+8: the mismatch between ±1 with correct parity can be repaired by changing one odd coordinate to ±3. If m=h=0 the possible distances are 0 and 16. These are squared distances in raw unscaled coordinates.

Integer mixed phases: pair the two 256-point banks of each source with banks of the other so the two matched pairs both have m=max(|T|,17−|T|) odd differences. If m=9…12, 5≤r≤8. Their binary projections admit an index subset I of size at least r−2 on which BOTH are surjective. Proof: extend a common independent set until that length. At any step |I|≤5, a single bank blocks at most one new index (i.e. produces a dependent restriction): if two x,y were blocked there would be two dual checks in I∪{x}, I∪{y}, containing x,y and ≥4 entries in I each. Their sum has weight≤4, impossible at dual minimum 5. Before size r−2 there are ≥3 unused indices, so the two banks cannot collectively block all. Surjectivity gives an equal-size fiber over every pattern (affine shifts permitted). Pair the 256+256 points bijectively on matching I-bits. Then h≤2 and squared distance≤m+8≤20, including the h=0 parity issue. For m=13…16, r≤4 and r≥1. Full projections are surjective, so pair fibers with opposite bit at one index and the same other bits. Then h=1 and squared distance=m+4≤20. Doing this for both banks gives 512 disjoint conflict pairs.

Aligned integer phases, |T|=0 or 17: an arbitrary independent selection consists of even 2c+4s_ce1 and odd 1+2d+4t_de1. At most one sheet per word (two sheets are only squared distance 16 apart). Both word sets have minimum Hamming distance 6. The cross constraint is s_c+t_d+|d|+c·d=1 mod 2, since the preliminary difference has a +3 exactly when d_i=1,c_i=0, and the all-±1 lift must be excluded. Taking rectangle differences implies (c+c′)·(d+d′)=0. Thus dimensions u,v of their affine spans sum to ≤17. If either ≤7, total ≤128+341=469, using unrestricted A(17,6)≤341, the conservative disclosed LP bound at https://aeb.win.tue.nl/codes/lpdetail.html. Empty-bank case also immediate. Otherwise pair (8,8) or (8,9) up to swapping. Dimension 8 holds ≤256. In a 9-space some nonzero difference has weight≤5, since otherwise size512 contradicts A(17,6)≤341. Translating by that word gives 256 disjoint forbidden pairs inside the 9-space, bounding its selected bank by 256. This proves ≤512 even for arbitrary nonlinear selections on both aligned masks. In the 512+512 labelled bipartite graph of two sources include a conflict edge for coincident physical points, so independence and real packing cardinality agree. König's theorem and 512 feasible centers give a perfect conflict matching. Every aligned-phase conflict has squared distance ≤20 (same phase possible distance 4h or 0/16, opposite phase 17/25). For any integer phase the complete matching therefore has squared lengths ≤20. The triangle inequality on the flat torus preserves every conflict when the second source moves by norm <√24−√20. Likewise the sum of two per-source displacement bounds may be < this radius.
