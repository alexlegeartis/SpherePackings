# Sphere 74, 26 November 2026: the four-packet continuation has a rigorous global obstruction
No new density record in this theorem. Fix the enlarged Coxeter–Todd fiber and E7 exterior of round 73 (scaled CT squared factor 3/2; physical E7 squared scaling factor 1/2). At each E7 site allow ANY subset, independent of all other sites, of ternary discriminant colors meeting advertised squared minimum four. The upper center density is at most 0.08769155573743466, below Chen–Hu–Li–Wang–Wu's 0.08895810212922774 (https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02394v1#S3). In particular the 32-label regular simplex cannot resemble a global four-color-per-site packing.

Write m(x) for occupancy above x. All pair differences at one site have q=2. Translating one label to zero leaves a Gram I+J on k−1 vectors (diagonal 2, off-diagonal 1). For k=8 that has rank 7, impossible in six dimensions. For k=2,4 the Gram is nonsingular, hence, by Witt extension for quadratic spaces in odd characteristic, each packet is determined up to ambient isometry. For k=3 exactly two isometry types are possible: translate one to 0 and its neighbors either span their degenerate two-space or are collinear with the forced third element. Witt extension also applies to the former embedding. Therefore we need only two triples, one tetrahedron, and all actual extensions of that tetrahedron. Enumerating the 729 colors gives maximum numbers a_k of colors distinct from the packet and avoiding q=1 against every packet color: 476,313,240,155,122,100,78 for k=1,…,7. Full exact arithmetic in sphere74_e7_variable_occupation_20261126.js.

Now center a copy of the 126-root first E7 shell at x. All colors of this shell must be among the a_m(x) allowed colors. A single color cannot occur at two nonopposite roots: their squared spacing in physical units is ≤3, hence identical horizontal color violates minimum 4. This gives (Am)(x)≤2 a_m(x) whenever m(x)>0 for the root adjacency A. This is substantially stronger than the surviving 32-label nearest simplex test alone.

Use every translation and Weyl image of an eight-point E7 nearest simplex (take the eight origin-shifted e_i in the A7 chart); the Weyl group is transitive on all 126 signed roots. Summing squares of sums of a real function over these copies proves A+18I is positive semidefinite: the relative diagonal-to-edge coefficient equals 126/7=18 by incidence. Apply it to m−μ under the periodic mean to give E[m Am]≥144μ²−18 E[m²]. For arbitrary configurations take a subsequence of Følner balls attaining the upper mean and convergent local averages, extending by zero and losing only a bounded boundary layer; the same inequality holds. Since 2k a_k+18k²≤1323+93k for k=1,…,7, also for k=0 by multiplying away the zero-site term, 144μ²≤1323+93μ. So μ≤(93+sqrt(770697))/288=3.3711579913670437. Multiplying by the exact unit-site density 512/19683 proves the claimed cap. No restrictions of periodicity, linearity or uniform occupancy are used. Deforming either shape, changing the squared scales, or using off-discriminant phases is outside this statement.
