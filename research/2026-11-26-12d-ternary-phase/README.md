# SpherePackings occurrence 92: 12D E7/D5 ternary glue, 26 November 2026

Scope: direct product period (sqrt(2) E7) + (sqrt(a) D5); both horizontal and vertical phases on their third-period grids. Impose the inherited squared minimum 4 (horizontal shortest vectors attain exactly 4). Arbitrary subsets of pairs of phases are allowed, with no linearity assumption. This entire normalized family fails to beat Coxeter–Todd center density 1/27. This is not an upper bound on 12D and not a statement about rescaling a geometry whose minimum has already fallen below 4, unrestricted phases, or different metrics. The 12D comparator is Cohn's table https://cohn.mit.edu/sphere-packing/ and Nebe/Sloane https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/density.html.

The period has covolume 32 a^(5/2); improvement requires M > 32 a^(5/2)/27. Pure vertical minimum forces a >= 2. Every E7/3E7 class has a representative of norm at most 8 (rather than the required 10 for a full ternary graph at a=8). The candidate graph on 1,093 nonzero antipodal phase pairs in which u,v are compatible when both u+-v have unscaled minimum >=10 has ZERO edges. The candidate originally targeted 243/(32*8^(5/2)) at minimum 4, but it does not exist.

Short proof of the diameter bound independent of lattice enumeration: use standard E7 simple roots with edges (1,3),(2,4),(3,4),(4,5),(5,6),(6,7), lengths squared 2. The affine Weyl group W(E7) ⋉ 3E7 sends each x in E7 into the closed dominant alcove at level 3. Write its Dynkin coordinates m_i>=0. The highest-root coefficients (2,2,3,4,3,2,1) have dot product <=3 with m. Integrality of G^-1 m (root lattice, not weight lattice) leaves precisely 0, 2ω7, ω6, ω3, ω2+ω7, ω1 of squared norms 0,6,4,6,8,2. Weyl isometries preserve coset minima; this proves the maximum is at most 8 exactly as needed.

A D5/3D5 class with Hamming weight w in F3^5 has shortest squared length t(w)=w+3(w mod 2): the centered residues are in {0,+1,-1}; if w is odd, correcting parity costs exactly 3 by changing one nonzero entry to its opposite-sign lift of magnitude 2. Hence the physical additive budget is (16+a*t(w))/9. No two sites can share a vertical phase. For 2<=a<10/3 only distance 5 is permitted and M<=3 by projection to one coordinate. For 10/3<=a<5 distances 1 and 2 are forbidden, so disjoint ternary Hamming balls give M<=floor(243/11)=22. For 5<=a<10 distance 2 is forbidden; uniform translates of the five-element set {e1,...,e5} hit at most one site, so M<=48. For a>=10, M<=243. At the left endpoints 2,10/3,5,10 the required motif sizes exceed each respective cap. Covers all nonlinear partial assignments.

The proof uses the exact alcove bound, not a search assumption. A better construction must change the phase grid, carrier, or separation normalization.
