# The 18D quadratic-residue stock cannot be promoted by merely translating its complete layers

Sphere 70, 26 November 2026, scheduled 00:22–00:52 UTC. Fixed historical sources: Bierbrauer–Edel, “Dense sphere packings from new codes”, https://www.yvesedel.de/Papers/Sphere.pdf (Theorem 1 and extended length-18 QR pair); Chen, Hu, Li, Wang and Wu, https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02394v1#S3 (19D center density 13^(19/2)/(3^9 5^(21/2))=0.088958102129...). Project main on opening: 13f96e8d54c1a2fd23e3c0b6268699cf4a9f0b55. The item below concerns an obstruction to the specific proposed record, not a new packing record.

**Theorem.** If P is the full extended-QR Bierbrauer–Edel 18D packing in integral coordinates (squared minimum 24), for every real s in R^18 there are p,p' in P with |s−(p−p')|²≤18. Thus even arbitrary, nonperiodic, unequally spaced, complete, parallel copies of P at arbitrary horizontal translations, with 19D squared minimum 24, have center density at most (3/4)^9=0.0750846862793: any two heights must differ by at least √6. This precludes the proposed +3.37% construction against 0.08895810, not just the originally suggested order-three subgroup. Copies independently rotated in their horizontal space, partial cosets, bank replacement and general non-layered packings are not covered.

**Difference cloud.** D is the even-coordinate-sum sublattice of Z^18. Let C and C' be the dual even [18,9,6] codes with C∩C'={0,1}; hence C+C' is the full even-weight binary space. The two stock banks modulo 4D are −2(1+c) with c∈C (binary addition inside parentheses) and 1−2d+4e₁ with d∈C'. Within one stock, for every c∈C except 0,1, the whole coset 2c+4Z^18 belongs to P−P: write the binary pair as a,a+c and vary a∈C; the carry-sheet parity toggles with a·c, since c is not in C⊥. The same holds in C'. Also 4D⊂P−P and adding 2·1 preserves P (complementation in the two even codes).

Across stocks, P−P contains every vector σ+4z where σ∈{±1}^18 has an even number of minus signs, and Σz is odd. Indeed modulo four the minus support is the XOR of one word from each code, and every even word occurs. In its residue class, the even-sheet alternative would include σ itself, of norm²18, contrary to the stock minimum 24. The entire odd sheet occurs by 4D periodicity. For every real s write s_i=A_i r_i+4b_i with A_i∈{±1}, 0≤r_i≤2. Put u_i=r_i−1, V=Σu_i², S=Σ|u_i|. The odd cloud gives distance²≤V+8: the zero correction if sign and sheet are correct. With correct sign parity and wrong sheet flip two signs of A, and choose their physical shifts of magnitude two with opposite relative directions; either choice has odd change of sheet and their squared costs average V+8. With wrong sign parity flip just one sign at a coordinate minimizing |u_i|; one of its two choices has the required sheet, at cost ≤V+4+4 min|u_i|≤V+8.

**Four-stock certificate.** Round y_i=1 when r_i>1, otherwise y_i=0 (ties can be chosen either way). For an ordinary codeword c∈(C∪C')\{0,1}, its nearest difference has squared cost
18+V−2S+4 Σ_{i∈supp(c+y)} |u_i|.
For each y with 2≤wt y≤16 there exist four such c's whose four mismatch supports with y are pairwise disjoint. Averaging shows min ≤18+V−S≤18. The exceptional sets for one code have size 344, exactly twice 1+18+153. The two codes together cover all but the weights 0,1,17,18, i.e. 38 exceptions.

**The four exceptional endpoint weights.** Translation by the period 2·1 interchanges low and high weights (ties harmless by selecting rounding on the transformed representative). For y=0 all r_i≤1. Write T=Σr_i², m=max r_i. Moving one largest coordinate by ±4 if the even-sheet parity requires it gives distance²≤T+16−8m. If that exceeds 18, m>0 and T>2+8m. Then 2Σr_i−T≥(2/m−1)T≥(2/m−1)(2+8m)≥10, so V+8=26−2Σr_i+T≤16. For y of weight one let its exceptional coordinate be 1+b, 0≤b≤1; the remaining a_i≤1. The even-sheet bound is T+8−8b with T=(1+b)^2+Σa_i². If this exceeds 18 then Σa_i²>9+6b−b², while V+8=25+b²−Σ(2a_i−a_i²)≤25+b²−Σa_i²<16−6b+2b²≤16. This finishes the real, every-shift statement.

**Radius qualification.** More generally, for 18<μ≤24, every inter-layer gap is at least √(μ−18), giving the bound δ₁₉ ≤ (3/4)^9(μ/24)^9 √μ/(2√(μ−18)). The bound is not uniform as μ decreases to 18; it must not be interpreted as closing all radii. On [18.1,24] its maximum is the larger endpoint (log derivative has sign μ−19), at most 0.075084687. The native-independent exact code follows.
