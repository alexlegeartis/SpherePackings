# A two-palette A2^5 stack in 11D: exact design gate

Normalize the per-block triangular period to minimum squared 4 and area 2√3. Its three deep/zero torsion labels form a cyclic order-3 subgroup; the union of those three cosets of the period is a triangular lattice of edge 2/√3, colored by the three residues. In each plane choose any 18 labels from F3^5 invariant under addition of (1,1,1,1,1), with minimum Hamming distance ≥3. Thus the six orbit triples can be chosen independently in the two planes; all 18+18 sites need not be translates of one fixed code. The two planes retain common per-coordinate label orientation and admit any real relative lateral shift and arbitrary spacing. At minimum distance 2, this family cannot attain 9/256 center density.

A point of the triangular plane lies in a triangular cell of the fine lattice, with one vertex of each color. If its barycentric coordinates are p=(p0,p1,p2), its squared distance to vertex j is (2/3)(1+Σp_l²−2p_j). The three chosen vertices provide valid representatives of those cosets, sufficient for upper bounds on squared distance to any stock of difference residues. For any two allowed motifs A,B, blockwise phase residue z in A−B specifies a horizontal cross vector. Optimize over all such z by taking the least sum of the five block distances. A point in the product cell admits a nearest denominator-9 barycentric submesh point within radius 2√5/27 because each triangular subcell of edge 2/(9√3) has covering radius 2/27.

Run `node verify.js` for the exact finite replay. At every such mesh point, the replay finds a possible cross vector of squared length ≤20/9, simultaneously over all allowed motifs and translations. In the quotient of F3^5 by the diagonal there are 81 orbit representatives (first coordinate zero). Two triples are compatible within a motif precisely when no symbol occurs more than twice among their five difference entries. Translating the motif lets it contain the zero orbit. Exhaustive clique enumeration gives exactly 12 normalized six-cliques. Their ordered pair differences with all translations give 243 distinct translated masks. For the mesh, cyclically permuting the three barycentric entries of a block is absorbed in the already enumerated ternary translations; simultaneously permuting five coordinates permutes the full class of motifs. Thus all 42,504 canonical denominator-9 product profiles suffice. The replay checks at least one allowed difference on every mask at each profile by pure integer arithmetic with common denominator 3·9²=243. At five triangle centers every individual block color has squared nearest distance 4/9, so mesh maximum 20/9 is attained. Two independently implemented enumerations (untranslated and translated-mask forms) agree.

By the triangle inequality, the inter-plane squared depth for arbitrary real shift is at most (√(20/9)+2√5/27)²=2000/729. Any consecutive plane gaps must be at least √(4−2000/729)=√916/27; this is necessary only and does not assert a matching construction. Therefore the center density is at most 18/[288√3·(√916/27)]=27/(16√2748). Its squared ratio to 9/256 is 192/229<1. This does not exclude planes lacking common diagonal symmetry, different within-plane continuous motifs, or arbitrary 11D packings.
