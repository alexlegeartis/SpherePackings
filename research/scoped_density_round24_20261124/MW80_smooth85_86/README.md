## The horizontal reduction

Let \(L\) be the Mordell--Weil lattice of \(y^2=x^3+1+t^{42}\)
over \(\mathbb F_{41^2}(t)\).
[Shioda (1991), Theorem 1.1](https://doi.org/10.2307/2374791)
gives rank 80, Gram determinant \(41^{12}\) and squared minimum
14. The 42 roots of \(1+t^{42}\) are simple in characteristic 41,
each contributing an irreducible type-II fibre. At infinity the
equation is \(y'^2=x'^3+1+s^{42}\) with \(x'=s^{14}x\),
\(y'=s^{21}y\), \(s=1/t\); that fibre is smooth. The height formula
has no reducible-fibre corrections. Thus every nonzero section
has \(\|P\|^2=14+2(P\cdot O)\), with nonnegative integral
intersection multiplicity. There is no nonidentity torsion.
Consequently the kernel of good-fibre specialization at \(t=0\)
has squared minimum at least 16.

Use \(\mathbb F_{41^2}=\mathbb F_{41}[u]/(u^2-3)\), and encode
\(a+bu\) as \(a+41b\). The reduced curve \(y^2=x^3+1\) has
group \(G=(\mathbb Z/42)^2\) generated by \((3,328),(50,14)\).
The verifier counts the finite curve and checks the generator
coordinates bijectively. The polynomial sections
\[
 x(t)=-(at+b)^{14},\qquad y(t)=(ct+d)^{21}
\]
are attained for any \(b,d\) of combined Hermitian norm one
by choosing \(a=-d^{41},c=b^{41}\).
The identity follows from \((at+b)^{42}\) expanded by Frobenius.
They are disjoint from \(O\), including at infinity, so their
heights equal 14. Their specializations include two independent
elements in each of the 2-, 3- and 7-primary components and
therefore generate the full \(G\). This proves surjectivity
of specialization from literal polynomial sections.

## Graph lattices

For either vertical lattice \(V\), define
\(M=\{(P,z)\in L\oplus V:e_0(P)=C(z)\}\), where \(C\) is the
listed color homomorphism into \(G\). The index is \(|G|=1764\),
since \(e_0\) is onto. A mixed nonzero vector has squared norm
at least \(14+2=16\); horizontal-only vectors have the kernel
bound above. The vertical-only kernel is checked up to norm 14,
covering 2,330 and 9,972 signed vectors, respectively.
Exact mixed contacts give equality.

For D5 use the simple basis \(e_0-e_1,e_1-e_2,e_2-e_3,e_3-e_4,e_3+e_4\)
and colors
\[
 (25,23),(0,13),(21,37),(2,16),(9,13).
\]
For E6 use the Cartan matrix of the path \(0,1,2,3,4\) with
extra edge \(2,5\), and colors
\[
 (8,18),(12,1),(15,1),(25,21),(0,2),(9,23).
\]
The polynomial mixed contacts, complete exact vertical exclusions
and density floors are reproduced by [verify.py](verify.py),
which uses only the Python standard library. The occupied
density equals the volume of the unit ball times \(2^d\)
divided by the covolume.
