# Constructive density round 24, 24 November 2026

**No numerical record changed.** Two distinct parent constructions:

- [10D: an 18-site nonlinear translation motif over five A2 blocks](A2_five_blocks/README.md).
  It ties the density of the best known lattice, not the unrestricted
  [10D benchmark](https://cohn.mit.edu/sphere-packing/). The exact
  finite Fourier bound is 18 for third-torsion, and the weaker
  sixth-torsion bound is 20. Twenty sites in that carrier would
  exceed the unrestricted record.
- [85D and 86D: smooth-fibre gluing of MW80](MW80_smooth85_86/README.md).
  True lattices with exact squared minimum 16 and explicit colors and
  contacts, below the current lower bounds. The 86D tail is E6, not D6.
  This is not a claim of minimum 16 for a proper quotient of the
  smooth-fibre group. The proofs use the full group.

The record tables are unchanged.
