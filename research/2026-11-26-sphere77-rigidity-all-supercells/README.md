# The 19D CHLWW sliding family is locally density-maximal in every fixed finite supercell

**Theorem.** In the 19-dimensional six-coset Chen–Hu–Li–Wang–Wu packing, each of the two triangular banks of the Sphere76 slide is infinitesimally rigid in every nontrivial unitary translation-character sector. Consequently, for each fixed finite-index subgroup L' of the original period lattice L, every sufficiently small L'-periodic perturbation, allowing an arbitrary change of periods and arbitrary independent motion of every center in the supercell, has density at most the initial density. After normalizing the separation and quotienting Euclidean motions, the only equally dense perturbations sufficiently near the starting packing are translations of the two banks subject to the cross-bank distance inequalities. This includes every baseline-preserving position of the sliding family. The neighborhood size may depend on L'. No global optimum or uniform neighborhood over unbounded periods, nor an improvement of the packing record, is claimed.

Read together with the primitive-cell positive rational stress and rank certificate in [the base-cell stress proof](primitive_cell.md) and [its replay](verify_primitive.py). This result resolves the supercell qualification in that note. The independent verifier is [verify_supercells.py](verify_supercells.py). [Original construction, Chen–Hu–Li–Wang–Wu](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02394v1#S3).

**1. A three-vertex Fourier argument.** For one bank, write each oriented pair shell as S_ij ⊂ w_ij + L. It contains 2011 vectors, all of squared norm 52/15. In a unitary character χ of L a displacement mode has three complex vectors u_i in H_C, H=W-perp, and the bar constraints are w·u_i = χ(w−w_ij) w·u_j for w∈S_ij (one may take the inverse character instead by convention). In particular |w·u_i|²=|w·u_j|². On every one of the six oriented pair shells, the real symmetric matrices wwᵀ span the full 190-dimensional Sym(H); the exact verifier checks this modulo the prime 1009 in an integer coordinate chart. Thus Re(u_i u_i*)=Re(u_j u_j*). Equality of these real positive forms means that the two-column real matrices [Re u_i, Im u_i] differ by an orthogonal transformation of their two columns (also in rank 0 or 1). All three u_i therefore equal one common u up to phase and optional complex conjugation. A triangle has a pair with the SAME conjugation choice. On that edge, χ has one fixed value on every contact shift w−w_ij with w·u≠0 (after conjugating the definition of u if necessary).

**2. Finitely many possible directions despite arbitrary characters.** For each edge we choose a short list of roots d∈L that generate the ENTIRE lattice L, not merely its real span. If χ is nontrivial, some such d on the same-conjugation edge has χ(d)≠1. Whenever w,w+d are contacts of that edge, at least one of w·u,(w+d)·u must vanish. At the midpoint m=w+d/2 this says m·u=±d·u/2. The midpoints are perpendicular to d; each selected d has 18 independent midpoints, hence they span d-perp in H. If d·u=0 then u=0. Otherwise normalize d·u/2=1. On 18 independent real midpoints the values are signs. Together with d the rows are a real basis, so the covector u is real up to a common complex scalar; there is no longer a genuine conjugation choice at ANY of the three sites or edges. Further, because all rows are rational in the chosen chart, this normalized covector has rational coordinates, with denominators invertible modulo 1009 via the selected pivots.

Use the integer row 60d for the normalizing equation and rows Z=2z+60d for the midpoints, where z=60x−(qB)W and x,d are raw Leech vectors before division by sqrt(8). They are all scaled consistently by 120sqrt(8). To prune exactly, exhaust the 2^18 signs on 18 independent midpoint rows, requiring every other midpoint value to be ±1 modulo 1009. The program initially keeps 2^17 possibilities up to global MIDPOINT sign, then restores BOTH sign choices before adjoining the normalizing row 60d with prescribed value +1. This detail matters; failure modulo 1009 excludes a rational possibility, whereas surviving modular classes are merely necessary, not assumed liftable.

**3. Why every candidate forces the character trivial.** For any surviving projective modular covector b, on each of the three edges retain just those contacts with z·b nonzero mod 1009. Such contacts are provably nonzero on the genuine normalized rational covector if it exists. Their corresponding phases χ(w−w_ij) must all agree WITHIN that edge, because every site velocity is a scalar multiple of the same real direction. Thus χ is identically one on all differences of supported contacts from a common edge. These are actual periods: in raw Leech coordinates they are x−x' with the identical normal label q. It suffices to exhibit any collection of such differences, from any of the three within-edge pools together, generating L. The code verifies this by an integral Hermite normal form for every candidate. Hence χ=1, contradiction. No discretization, bound on character order, or eigenvalue tolerance occurs.

**Integer index normalization.** In raw coordinates select a set I of 19 coordinates that restrict independently to H, and let D be its complementary set of five columns. In this paragraph D denotes an index set. The row Gram of W is 8G, det G=300. Because the Leech lattice is unimodular and the normal slice is primitive, physical covol(L)=sqrt(300) (primitivity follows from five unit pairing profiles already witnessed by Leech roots). Consequently |det([W;L_basis])|=300*8^12. Expanding the block determinant using W_D invertible and W L_basis^T=0 shows that the coordinate image of L on I has covolume |det(W_D)|*8^7. The HNF program checks both exact divisibility by this integer and index 1, for each list of generators d and each list of contact differences. There is no presumed saturation. Independently, modulo-1009 quadrics use the rank-19 chart, so a nonzero value indeed certifies a nonzero rational dot product.

**Exact output.** In bank A={0,4,5} the three respective root lists have sizes 20,21,20, each of index one. Across the three alternatives 1,526 distinct projective modular covectors survive the midpoint test. For every one a sample of at most 65 actual contact differences has exact index one. For bank B={1,2,3}, root list sizes are 21,20,21; all 1,637 surviving modular covectors are killed by at most 36 such differences each. All witnesses are selected deterministically by the seeded replay; all proof checks are integer checks. Checked both restored sign branches. Sample sizes only describe witness lengths, and are not estimates of a failure probability.

**4. From infinitesimal to genuinely local optimality.** In a fixed finite supercell, propagate the strictly positive balanced isotropic base stress to all translated contacts. Suppose arbitrarily small perturbations in the reduced chart (independent rigid translations of banks removed) retained or increased density with the minimum held fixed. Divide by the norm of the perturbation and take a convergent subsequence. Each contact's first variation is nonnegative, and the trace of the common strain is nonpositive. The stress makes their positive weighted sum a positive multiple of the trace, forcing all first variations to vanish. The zero-character part gives only translations by the base-cell exact rank 228; all nonzero character parts, obtained by Fourier transform on L/L', are zero by steps 1–3. This contradicts unit norm. Cross-bank edges were not needed, so extra contacts at endpoints of a slide do not invalidate the conclusion.

## Running the checks
Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python verify_primitive.py` for the exact positive base stress and `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python verify_supercells.py` for the full character certificate. Both require NumPy, SciPy, and SymPy. The mathematical implications of each integer/rational check are explained above. Baseline data are also available in the [preceding contact-transition packet](../2026-11-26-sphere76-contact-transition/).

A further [analytic addendum](uniform_escape.md) obtains a single positive threshold in the supremum of the changes of the original within-bank bond vectors, independent of finite superperiod. It does not claim uniformity in an unlabeled geometry metric or exclude finite-amplitude deformations.
