# A local rigidity theorem for the flexible 19-dimensional CHLWW packing

This is not a new density record or a global optimality assertion. It resolves whether the disconnected contact graph and actual slide found in Sphere76 permit an infinitesimal determinant/shape gain. For background, see the original [Chen–Hu–Li–Wang–Wu construction](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02394v1#S3) and the accompanying archived Sphere76 base construction.

**Theorem (six-mark primitive-cell deformations).** Write the six-site packing as banks A={0,4,5}, B={1,2,3}, in the numbering 0,e1,...,e5 of the five normal labels, over the fixed rank-19 period lattice. For every relative translation for which the packing retains its center separation sqrt(52/15), it is locally density-maximal among arbitrary deformations of the period metric and all six motif positions, in the chart with these six marks (or modulo motions of the whole space). At density at least the present one, every sufficiently small admissible perturbation keeps the period metric and the shape of both individual three-site motifs fixed. Only independent translations of the two banks remain, subject to their mutual inequalities. At an interior slide there is thus a genuine local plateau of nonisometric packings, NOT a volume-lowering strain. This primitive-cell argument alone does not cover enlarged fundamental domains. The accompanying [character certificate](README.md) supplies the missing argument for each fixed finite supercell.

**Exact certificate.** The period complement is H=W-perp in R^24, where the five integral Leech rows W and their normal Gram inverse B/60 appear in the standalone Python verifier. The Leech raw-coordinate metric is divided by eight. Number the edges of each bank by the three oriented pairs i<j; for each pair q there are 2011 shortest lifts x, norm squared four. Project to w=(60x−(q B)W)/(60 sqrt(8)) in H. Each |w|²=52/15. For each bank the verifier establishes:
1. The 6033 linear functionals (E,t1,t2) → w^T E w+2w·(t_j−t_i), t0=0, span the dual of Sym(H)⊕H⊕H, dimension 190+38=228.
2. Strictly positive rational weights p, one per contact, satisfy force balance at each site and isotropy sum p w w^T=(52/285)(sum p)I_H. After numerical grouping into 173 classes, only 14 exact scalar constraints are independent in the constructed grouped certificate. All candidate weights are corrected to rational values and all 228 original equations are verified over the rationals. Smallest weights with mean approximately one exceed 0.983 in BOTH banks. Float guesses play no proof role.

For the exact computation of isotropy, let z=60 sqrt(8) w, I a set of 19 independent coordinate functionals on H, C=480 I_24−W^T B W=480 times the orthogonal projector onto H. On the chosen coordinate pairs, each weighted centered second moment uses the integer 19 z_a z_b−208 C_ab. The force constraints use the signed z_a. Exact modular row rank of the uncentered 6033×228 matrix over the prime 32749 is 228. Invertible changes of coordinates, nonzero column scalings, or doubling off-diagonal symmetric entries do not affect that rank. The code only accepts after exact rational positivity/equality checks. The helper programs are small; stock single-thread invocation: OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python verify_primitive.py.

**Nonlinear argument.** Normalize separation to the initial value by rescaling any comparison packing. Work modulo Euclidean motions, write the period metric as I+E, and use position displacements in the original Euclidean coordinates. Gauging one displacement to zero per bank removes the independent bank translations. For an initial contact the squared-distance constraint expands to w^T E w+2w·(t_j−t_i)+O(epsilon²)≥0, epsilon the norm of all these reduced variables. A density at least the initial one requires det(I+E)≤1, hence tr E≤O(epsilon²). If nontrivial admissible reduced perturbations with such density accumulated at zero, their unit-normalized vectors would converge along a subsequence. Every linearized constraint is then nonnegative and the trace is nonpositive. Sum the constraints with the positive balanced isotropic weights, separately in each bank. All nonnegative terms must vanish identically; full rank forces the normalized strain and both banks' internal displacements to be zero. This contradicts unit norm and proves the stated strictness transverse to the translation freedom. The proof uses no assumption that the affine first-order observation alone implies isolation.

**What changes for supercells.** A first-order negative trace is still excluded for any finite-index superperiod: simply translate the base stresses onto every site in that supercell and sum, or average the velocities over cells of each base label. But an internal nonzero mode can have zero average, so a second-order det improvement is not thereby excluded. Nonlocal changes of carrier or number of sites remain entirely possible.
