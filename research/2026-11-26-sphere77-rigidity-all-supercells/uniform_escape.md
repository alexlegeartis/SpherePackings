# Uniform strengthening: no small-bond high-period route out of the 19D CHLWW plateau

This is an analytic addendum to the two exact certificates in [primitive cell](primitive_cell.md) and [all supercells](README.md), not a separate density record. Let L denote the rank-19 period lattice, and w_e=p_j−p_i+l_e the finitely many oriented base contacts in one bank; l_e∈L. The full-sign replay rules out nonzero bar modes at **every nontrivial unitary character**, not only characters of finite order. This extra strength gives a uniform conclusion.

**Theorem.** There is epsilon_*>0 depending only on the fixed two triangular banks and their initial contacts, not on finite L'⊂L or the baseline-preserving relative bank slide, such that the following holds. Identify centers in an L'-supercell with their initial labels. Allow an affine deformation of its period lattice and an L'-periodic displacement of every center. Normalize center separation to its original value and apply a global orthogonal transformation so that the affine period map F is symmetric positive definite. If the displacement Δ_e(s) of EVERY original within-bank contact vector is smaller than epsilon_* and density has not decreased, then F=I and the displacement field is constant on each bank. Thus a density improvement in any such periodic motif requires a genuinely finite distortion of some original bond, uniformly in the supercell. We do not give a numerical epsilon_* or exclude a finite-amplitude route, changes of site count, or general global competitors. The notion of closeness is in matched bond vectors after an overall rotation, not just bond lengths or Hausdorff distance without a labeling.

**Uniform discrete Korn inequality.** For a bank and a character χ_k(l)=exp(i k·l), write D_e(k)u=χ_k(l_e)u_j−u_i and R_e(k)u=w_e·D_e(k)u. Uniformly for k≠0 in the compact dual torus,
    sum_e |D_e(k)u|² ≤ K sum_e |R_e(k)u|² .
Away from the trivial character this follows from full column rank of R(k), continuity and compactness. For k→0 suppose instead ||D(k_n)u_n||=1 and ||R(k_n)u_n||→0. For any two contacts on the same oriented edge with lattice difference d, subtracting their D rows gives |χ_k(d)−1| |u_j|≤2. Each edge has a fixed finite set of such d spanning L (the stronger integral generation was checked in the replay). Thus max_d|χ_k(d)−1|≥c|k| for small k; consequently |k| |u_j| remains bounded. Connectivity and bounded D imply the same for every site, and that all relative u_j−u_0 stay bounded. Pass to a subsequence with k/|k|→n (a real unit covector), |k|u_0→v, u_j−u_0→b_j. For each edge the limiting D row is b_j−b_i+i(n·l_e)v. Absorb −i(n·p_j)v into b_j to write this as b'_j−b'_i+i(n·w_e)v. Its projection onto w_e vanishes. The primitive-cell rank 228 applies after complexification to symmetric strain E=sym(i v⊗n) and relative b'_j, so E=0 and all b'_j agree. E=0 forces v=0 because n is real and nonzero (apply E to n); hence every limiting D row is zero, contrary to ||D||=1. This proves the uniform inequality.

For the trivial Fourier mode allow symmetric A=F−I and Δ_e(0)=Aw_e+u_j−u_i. Base rank 228 implies finite-dimensional estimates sum |Δ_e(0)|²≤K_0 sum |w_e·Δ_e(0)|² and |A|²≤C sum |Δ_e(0)|², after removal of bank translation. For every finite-index L' with N cells, decompose the periodic displacements into the characters of L/L'. All nontrivial modes are covered uniformly and the affine map occurs in the trivial mode only. Parseval therefore gives
    Q := sum_{s,e}|Δ_e(s)|² ≤ K sum_{s,e}|a_e(s)|²,   N |A|² ≤ C Q,
where a_e(s)=w_e·Δ_e(s). Constants are independent of L'. These estimates hold per bank. In particular small maximum bond displacement implies F close to I, by the second estimate and the fixed number of contacts per cell.

**Nonlinear step; a uniform finite escape threshold.** Propagate the exact positive weights p_e from the primitive-cell certificate. By force balance and isotropy, sum p_e a_e(s)=N c tr A with c>0. Rigid rescaling to common separation makes non-decreasing density equivalent to det F≤1. For F=I+A symmetric positive and sufficiently close to I, log det F≤0 gives tr A≤C'|A|². Nonoverlap at the original contacts gives a_e(s)≥−|Δ_e(s)|²/2. Therefore
    sum p_e |a_e| = sum p_e a_e+2 sum p_e max(0,−a_e) ≤ C'' Q .
All weights are fixed and positive. Also |a_e|≤sqrt(52/15) max|Δ_e|. Uniform Korn now gives Q≤K' (max|Δ_e|) Q. Taking epsilon_* sufficiently small forces Q=0. The mean estimate yields A=0, and either the Fourier argument or the known connectivity implies that all center displacements are constant within each bank. Repeat for the other bank. Any extra contacts between the banks only restrict relative translation, so the constant used here is independent even of where in the sliding plateau one starts.

This argument addresses why a small singular value near k=0 is not by itself an escaping soft mode: the relevant comparison is between contact-length variation and the **bond** variation, not between contact-length variation and the absolute displacement of a very long wave. The rank-one acoustic strain is seen by the base-cell rigidity certificate.
