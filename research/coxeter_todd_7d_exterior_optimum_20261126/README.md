# A sharp seven-dimensional exterior theorem for Coxeter–Todd fiber packings in 19D
November 26, 2026. No new record. The exact maximum of the center density in the following model is √2/16 = 0.0883883476483184, below Chen–Hu–Li–Wang–Wu's 19D 0.0889581021292277 (https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02394v1#S3). This is a result for an explicit architecture, not a global sphere-packing upper bound. The preceding round fixed four D4 coordinates and excluded Bravais choices in the last three; here all seven exterior coordinates, all 28 real Gram coefficients, and the entire additive ternary discriminant map are free, with no D4 sublattice assumption. Nonadditive colorings, non-Bravais exteriors, arbitrary real internal torus shifts outside the discriminant, a change of the fiber lattice, and giving the fiber a minimum strictly greater than the advertised minimum remain outside the result.

## Exact problem
Fix the norm-four Coxeter–Todd lattice K, covolume 27. The horizontal squared depths times 3 of its K*/K ternary discriminant colors in V=F3^6 are: h(0)=0, h(c)=4,6,8 for q(c)=1,0 (nonzero),2, where q(c)=Σcᵢ². See Conway–Sloane, The Coxeter–Todd lattice, the Mitchell group, and related sphere packings (1983), https://neilsloane.com/doc/Me107.pdf ; and the preceding companion note. Place one complete translated K over each z∈Z⁷ of raw exterior Gram G>0, with color F(z) for any homomorphism F:Z⁷→V. Divide exterior coordinates by √3. At advertised minimum squared four this is feasible iff G[z]≥12−h(Fz) for all nonzero z. Its center density is 3^(7/2)/(27√detG)=√(3/detG). We prove detG≥384 and give attaining (G,F) in the companion replay.

## Reduction to an ordinary E7 perfect cone
Assume detG<384. Every nonzero exterior vector has G[z]≥4. Minimize tr(G⁻¹ P) over the ordinary minimum-four Ryshkov polyhedron. A minimizing perfect-form vertex P exists: in a bounded trace section, bounded eigenvalues and the ordinary Hermite bound on determinant force compactness, and only finitely many short-vector constraints can occur there. Since G itself is feasible, tr(G⁻¹P)≤7 and detP≤detG by AM–GM. The only ordinary minimum-four perfect form in dimension seven of determinant <384 is E=twice the E7 root Gram, determinant 256. This uses the published complete 33-form classification of D.-O. Jaquet-Chiffelle, https://doi.org/10.5802/aif.1320, and the density-ordered catalogue https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/homes/Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/perfect.html ; the next determinant is 64(4/3)^7>384 (E7*, see https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/P7.2.html). Choose that integral basis, P=E. By the normal cone condition, G⁻¹ is a nonnegative linear combination of rrᵀ over E7 root lines r (equivalently this is the standard Voronoi perfect-domain decomposition). Note this statement is at the chosen minimizer of the ordinary LP, not an assumption of eutaxy for G.

Let λ be the positive eigenvalues of E⁻¹G. Then Πλ<3/2 and Σ1/λ≤7, implying Σλ<33/4. Proof: maximize Σλ over these inequalities. Scaling upwards makes Πλ=3/2 at maximum; positivity and boundedness follow from both constraints. Unless all λ are equal or the harmonic constraint is active, Lagrange gives all equal. With both constraints active, each λ solves the same quadratic, so k eigenvalues are x>1, and the others y=(7−k)x/(7x−k). Both xᵏy^(7−k) and kx+(7−k)y increase with x>1. For k=1..6 take the respective rational majorants x=275,189,159,143,131,121 divided by 100. Each product exceeds 3/2 and each sum is <33/4, verified with integers in the replay. The all-equal case is smaller.

## The 63-root ternary constraint
Realize E7 roots, at their usual norm two, in the sum-zero subspace of R⁸: 28 lines e_i−e_j and 35 lines ε/2 for sign vectors with four pluses and four minuses, modulo overall sign. Use G relative to E=2I on this subspace. Every A7 subsystem of 28 root lines is a tight frame: Σ_{r∈A7} rrᵀ=8I on this subspace; equivalently Σ G[r]=16 tr(E⁻¹G). Set the true discrepancy p(r)=(12−h(Fr)−4)/2∈{0,1,2,4}. From the trace bound, for every A7 subsystem, Σp(r)≤9 because Σ2p≤16(tr(E⁻¹G)−7)<20. Averaging the Weyl translates over the transitive 63 root lines gives total discrepancy ≤⌊9·63/28⌋=20, so some A7 has discrepancy at most 8. Make it the displayed e_i−e_j subsystem.

On E7/3E7 the ordinary form of the unscaled roots takes the constant 2. Define H=q∘F−q_E7 over F3. Its values on the 28 A7 roots, written as ordinary residues x_ij∈{0,1,2}, are lower bounds for p(r), equal except that an actual zero color has p=4 instead of 1. The 28 values uniquely determine H (A7 has index two in E7, invertible mod3). In fact for sum-zero c, H(c)=−Σ cᵢcⱼ xᵢⱼ. In particular H(ε/2)=−Σ εᵢεⱼ xᵢⱼ mod3. Enumerate all 28-entry ternary edge labelings of total ≤8, 23,779,362 inputs. Relabel coordinates to give nonincreasing vertex degrees (sum of incident edge labels); 43,164 inputs remain, with no valid orbit lost. Compute the 35 other residues, and discard candidates with total exceeding 20. The pullback q∘F must have rank ≤6. If rank six, the nonsingular quotient determinant is 1 mod3, and the radical is precisely ker F; correct its zero-root penalties. For rank <6, ignoring zero-color corrections only weakens rejection. Compute the sums in the standard A7 chart and the 35 charts obtained by reflecting it in the half roots. A cross-cut edge maps to the half-root sign string toggling its two coordinates; a within-part edge remains fixed. If the sum of even the uncorrected residues exceeds 9 in any such chart, reject.

Seven degree-sorted patterns survive (no need to assert inequivalence or prove an orbit count). Each has rank six and no zero-color root. Construct G* by assigning its 28 A7 root differences exactly the required 4+2xᵢⱼ. In the basis e_i−e₈ the Gram has entries (N_i8+N_j8−N_ij)/2 for N_ij=4+2xᵢⱼ, N_ii=0. For each of the seven patterns the replay verifies G*>0, det G*=1536 in the A7 basis, hence det G*=384 on E7 (index 2). It verifies that G* attains all 35 other required root values exactly too. Therefore G[r]≥G*[r] on all 63 root lines. From the normal cone condition tr(G⁻¹(G−G*))≥0. Equivalently tr(G⁻¹G*)≤7, which implies detG*≤detG by AM–GM. This contradicts detG<384 and finishes the bound. The attaining G,F are included and tested in the companion file using a complete integer enumeration bounded by the inverse-Gram diagonal; 92,64,436 signed active exterior vectors at respective lengths 4,6,8 and none at 12. Together with K this is a genuine norm-four packing.
