# Sphere95, 26 November 2026: unrestricted one-dimensional relayering of 46D sections cannot beat CHLWW47 for D≤68

**Result, not a new density record.** Take any extremal positive definite even unimodular 48D lattice P of minimum 6. For any primitive rank-two section K=P∩U of determinant D≤68, let L=P∩U⊥. In dimension 47 place complete translates of L, their offsets chosen from the canonical glue classes K*/K, at arbitrary real heights. Heights need not be equidistant; colours need not follow any periodic or group rule, and several distinct fibres may sit at the same height. No such packing exceeds the 47D center density δ*=47^(47/2)/2^118, occupied-density floor 2.26696032293109e−8, of Chen–Hu–Li–Wang–Wu [https://arxiv.org/html/2505.02394v1#S3], 2025. Not about arbitrary continuous horizontal translates, partial fibres, a changed carrier, normals of larger determinant, or all sphere packings in dimension 47. It includes the two-helix case as a special case.

The Sphere89 archive's third LP row component for each nonzero colour d is E_d, whereby the actual minimum of the horizontal difference fibre is at most 6−E_d; conservative costs still give valid necessary constraints. **Reduction to a finite word theorem.** For positive squared packing diameter μ write b=6−μ, c_d(μ)=√max(0,E_d−b), h(μ)=(μ/4)^(47/2)/(√D δ*), h²=(2/D)(8μ/47)^47. Order the full layers (ties arbitrary). Necessarily z_j−z_i≥c_(s_j−s_i)(μ) for i<j. Our theorem on words: for every normal and every 27/5≤μ≤6 there is k∈{1,…,6} such that EVERY word of k+1 colours has an increasing subsequence, starting at the first colour, whose consecutive clearances sum to at least k h(μ). Thus z_(i+k)−z_i≥kh for every i, regardless of all other layers. Iterating gives uniformly at most W/h+O(k) layers in any height interval of width W (derive by grouping sorted indices modulo k); taking arbitrarily long windows gives the desired center-density bound. Each single colour vertically repeats only at distance ≥√μ; thus for μ≤27/5 the upper density is ≤√D(μ/4)^23/2≤√68(27/20)^23/2<δ* by exact squaring. This also establishes local finiteness at every positive radius and handles arbitrarily small μ, without a positive lower-radius hypothesis.

**All radii, not just a grid.** Divide [0,3/5] for b at every E_d in range and optional rational subdivisions. For any fixed chain, each active normalized edge c_d/h equals a common positive constant times t^23√(1−m_dt), t=1/μ, m_d=6−E_d; inactive edges contribute zero identically. Thus the sum for that one chain is log-concave in t: its nonnegative square-root sum is concave, and t^23 is positive log-concave. Endpoint validity of the SAME chain proves validity throughout, including zero terms by continuity; we do not use log-concavity of a maximum over chains. The replay enforces every E_d is outside the open interval under examination and exact coverage of the whole [0,3/5].
