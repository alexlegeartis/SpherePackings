# The runner-up over the 39-dimensional section

Let `L` be the 39-dimensional complement of the normal section `K9` in the specific extremal unimodular lattice `P48p`, in the [Dorofeev–Sun–Wang certificate P48p-adc3124f](https://github.com/wcgbg/sphere-packing/blob/main/packings_out/records/dim39-delta18/P48p-adc3124f.cert.txt). Its minimum squared distance is 6 and its center density is `δ₀=(3/2)^(39/2)/√21870=18.3592594841895…`, the 39D comparator from their construction, not a new density claim. Its occupied density is approximately `1.68028177365432·10^-7`. We investigated a constructive way to beat it: superpose complete translated copies, choose the radius **after** the true closest contacts are known, and allow *any*, not necessarily additive, collection of the `21,870` canonical phases in `L*/L`.

**Sharp theorem for this carrier.** The best packing center density over all non-singleton canonical motifs, at their actual maximal common sphere radius, is exactly
$$
  9(8/9)^{39/2}\,\delta_0 = 0.9052495585758812\ldots\,\delta_0 .
$$
Nine translates attain it. The singleton therefore wins strictly. No global record was improved, and this is not an upper bound on other 39D constructions. Neither putting independently selected points inside individual sheets nor arbitrary translates on the full 39D torus is covered. Other embeddings of the same normal, such as the `P48q` and `P48n` sections, have the normal-diameter obstruction below, but **need not** have this true metric.

### Why we had to compute genuine distances

Write `q(c)=30 min|u|²` for the least squared norm, in the indicated units, of the paired normal discriminant class. For `h(c)=min_{x∈c+L}|x|²`, unimodular gluing implies `30 h(c) ≥ 180−q(c)` and `30 h(c) ≡ −q(c) (mod 60)`: pair a shortest normal lift with a shortest complementary lift, which remain a vector of the even ambient lattice of minimum 6. The intrinsic estimate is only a lower bound. In fact **1,475 nonzero classes have true squared minimum larger by exactly 2**, and the other 20,394 attain the estimate. The exceptional counts by normal numerator are:

| q | 84 | 85 | 89 | 96 | 105 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| phases with excess 2 | 8 | 4 | 130 | 810 | 523 |

Thus the extra distance is real. It just cannot be assembled across enough differences at once. The two deepest true classes, mutually negative, have `h=171/30=5.7`. A two-sheet improvement would require `h>6·2^(−2/39)`.

### Exact upper bound from short vectors (the small, primary replay)

Run `node normal.js && node verify.js`; no packages, solvers, shell cache, random seeds, or network access are required. `gram.json` is the integer Gram of `L` from the source certificate above. `gens.json` coordinatizes `L*/L` as `F3^7 × Z/10`: coordinates are stored in units of `1/30` of a primitive basis of L. The verifier checks the orders, dual membership, distinctness, and determinant. For every nonzero class a, `witness.gz` supplies an integer correction vector placing a representative close to the origin. All 21,869 squared lengths are recomputed with exact integer arithmetic. Write `u(a)=30 × witnessed squared length`; the certificate requires upper witnesses, not unverified optimal vectors.

Normalize a motif by including 0. If the largest witnessed deficit `b=max_{i≠j}(180−u(a_i−a_j))` amongst its differences is b, its center-density ratio is at most `M((180−b)/180)^(39/2)`. Moreover it is a clique in the group difference graph with allowed deficits ≤b. The graph search fixes 0 by translation and works on its neighbors. Vertices are greedily partitioned into independent sets, which provides a rigorous coloring upper bound, and standard reverse-order branching recursively explores each remaining vertex unless the color count makes the target impossible. No node-limit exit is accepted as a proof. The test uses an integer binary search for the first `M` violating `M²(180−b)^39 ≤ 81·160^39`. Thus the bound holds for arbitrary motifs at the true radius, regardless of whether all upper witnesses attain their class minima. See `replay.log`.

### Normal carrier, lower witness, and perturbations

Put `E={x∈(1/3)Z^9: Σx=0, x_i−x_j ∈ Z}` (the common fractional part is 0, 1/3 or 2/3). This is `A8 + Z·ω3 ≅ E8`: an even unimodular overlattice of A8 of index 3. Let `α=(8,−1,…,−1)/3`, `|α|²=8`, `h₀=√(10/3)`. In orthogonal coordinates the normal is generated by `(√3 E,0)` and `(α/√3,h₀)`. `normal.js` explicitly matches eight roots and the last vector to the published 9×9 Gram; their E8 basis has determinant one. A dual point is `(x/√3, j/√30)` with `x∈E`, `j+x·α≡0 mod 3`. Write `y=x−jα`. Then `y∈A8`, its residues modulo 3 define a word of length nine and sum zero, up to adding a constant word; `j mod 10` gives the cyclic coordinate. The normal periods translate y by `3E` or `−9α∈3E` and j by 0 or 10. Conversely these are exactly the identifications: the ternary space has size `3^7`, times 10 gives the determinant. S9 permutes nine coordinates, preserves α modulo 3E, and acts as ordinary permutations on y with unchanged j; this also follows directly from the residue-and-norm formula.

To compute the 70 orbit norms, use `X=3x`: integral, sum zero, `X_i ≡ −j mod 3`, `y_i ≡ (X_i+j)/3 mod 3`. Then
`30|u|²=10Σ X_i²/9+j²`. Sorted-coordinate enumeration up to numerator 105 reaches every orbit, proving completeness without a covering assumption. For the lower construction use j=0, fix the first ternary digit to zero, record the next seven digits least significant first as a base-3 integer, and recover the final digit from sum zero. Take indices `0,1,3,9,27,81,243,729,2186`. Each nonzero difference has normal numerator 20. Since the ambient is unimodular all nine paired horizontal classes exist; each true horizontal separation squared is at least `6−2/3=16/3`. This attains the upper bound. The normal argument separately closes all radii even for other embeddings; a small clique enumeration and four rational averaged character certificates are given in `normal.js`. The [original paper, Remark 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.20359v4) had explicitly left diameters squared above 1 undecided.

**Robustness.** Every distinct canonical pair has true squared separation at least `5/2` by the maximal normal numerator 105. If each translate moves by norm ≤1/500, the old closest pair can increase its distance by at most 1/250, so density is multiplied by at most `(1+(1/250)/√(5/2))^39 < (396/395)^39`. The integer inequality `81·8^39·396^78 < 9^39·395^78` proves that even independently perturbed non-singletons remain strictly below the record. This is a certified safe radius, not the optimal perturbation threshold.

### Optional independent audit of the exact atlas

`python3 atlas.py` requires NumPy, SymPy, a C++17 compiler and Node. It rebuilds the anti-isometry of the two discriminant groups in the *specified* P48p embedding, checks its match to the group coordinates, and recovers the lower half of the 39D dual shell directly. One needs only vectors of `30|v|² ≤100`, **not** the 52,416,000-vector 48D shell. All surviving expected normal lower levels higher than 100 already have exact attaining witnesses; in the enumerated range absence of a lower level and the congruence modulo 60 settle the rest against their explicit upper witnesses. The enumeration uses a verified unimodular basis change, rational LDL factors rounded down/out, integer branch lower bounds, coordinate caps from Cauchy–Schwarz, and exact integer leaf tests. Antipodal signs are counted once then restored. The replay traversed 860,001,814 nodes with 33,596 nonzero vectors and found 5,600 nonzero phases in that range. See `atlas_replay.log`. The main sharp density certificate is independent of this larger replay.

The group map in `glue.js` uses the Pless symmetry-code model of P48p in units `1/√12`. The doubled signed `[I|S]` code rows belong to the even sublattice of Construction A (each has norm 8), as do the doubled scaled `D48` vectors: `6(e_i−e_48), 12e_48`. Every such generator is thus in the ambient P48p; their projections already furnish all 21,870 classes, so no other ambient generator or uniqueness hypothesis is needed. The signed Paley matrix and coordinates agree with the [source construction](https://github.com/wcgbg/sphere-packing/blob/main/sphere_packing/lattices/ternary_codes.cc). Coordinate integers in `embedding.json` are the normal and horizontal matrices of the same public certificate.

**Constructive next gate.** Keep a different rank-9 normal only if its determinant and multi-sheet budget can beat the above comparator, or genuinely abandon the discrete phases. For off-grid doubling on this particular L, a new hole must have squared distance `>6·2^(−2/39)≈5.790470` from L; even the deepest discrete class gives only 5.7. Another 21,870-class subset search on this exact L is ruled out.
