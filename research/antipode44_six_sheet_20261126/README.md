# 44D normal-diameter antipode certificates: six sheets cannot improve the five-sheet record

This is a rigorous extension of the [sharp at-most-five-sheet result](../antipode44_five_sheet_20261126/README.md) to six discriminant classes, in the standard normal-diameter antipode **certificate** based on any even unimodular min-6 parent in dimension 48. It says nothing about unrestricted 44D packings, the true horizontal separations of these motifs, or seven or more translates. The [Chen–Hu–Li–Wang–Wu comparator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02394v1) is δ₀=5·(157/110)^22/√605.

Let K be the rank-four normal lattice, V=covol(K), β the squared torus diameter. The advertised center density of six sheets is 6·((6−β)/4)^22/V. If this were at least δ₀, Hermite gives V≥18, hence β<209/500<2/3 (substitute 209/500 and square). Every packet lifts coherently by the at-most-five proof's shortest-to-one-vertex argument, which uses only the bound β<2/3, not cardinality.

## Affine ranks below four

For affine rank r, let Z=K* intersect the span of the differences and W=K intersect its orthogonal complement. Then 1/V=covol(Z)/covol(W). Rank one is no better than the two-site bound because 6/(6−1)≤2 in the spacing estimate. In rank two, triangulate all six sites using at least four nondegenerate lattice triangles; the bounding square has area ≤β, so covol(Z)≤β/2 while covol(W)²≥27. In rank three select any five sites of affine rank three. If one tetrahedron from those five has index at least two, the regular tetrahedron bound gives covol(Z)≤β^(3/2)/(2√2). Otherwise the unique affine dependence on the five sites has coefficients zero or ±1, summing to zero, so support exactly four and its points form a parallelogram. Its area is ≤β/2 and the fifth site's altitude ≤√β, hence covol(Z)≤β^(3/2)/2; covol(W)²≥6. Thus squared density is at most β²/3·((6−β)/4)^44 and (3/2)β³·((6−β)/4)^44 respectively. These bounds maximize at β=6r/(44+r) and are both strictly below δ₀² by rational substitution. Affine rank zero is impossible with six distinct sites.

## Full affine rank

For any five affinely independent sites and their edge index j in K*, form the integral facet normals v_i as in the five-sheet proof. At least δ₀ with six sheets implies ||v_i||²≤(36/25) times the five-sheet facet bound, hence <12. Thus every squared normal length is 6, 8 or 10. Along an edge p_i−p_j we have (v_i−v_j)·(p_i−p_j)=2j. The nonzero vector v_i+v_j is in K, so ||v_i−v_j||²=2(||v_i||²+||v_j||²)−||v_i+v_j||²≤34. It follows that j≥2 implies β≥16/34>209/500, impossible. Consequently every five-subset is unimodular or affinely dependent.

The unique affine circuit of the six sites is the list of signed five-minors in lattice coordinates. Its entries are in {0,±1} and sum to zero. Its support is four or six, since support two would identify sites.

* **Support four:** after translating, the six sites are 0,u,v,u+v,w,t. The four vectors u,v,w,t form a unimodular basis of K*. Its dual basis elements w*,t* vanish on u,v and are dual to the projections of w,t onto span(u,v)⊥. Their rank-two lattice inside K has minimum squared ≥6, hence Gram determinant ≥27. The projected w,t edge parallelogram has area ≤1/√27. The u,v parallelogram has area ≤β/2 from its two diagonals. Therefore covol(K*)≤β/(2√27), giving δ≤β/√3·((6−β)/4)^22. The maximum at β=6/23 is less than δ₀.
* **Support six:** the sites form two triangles with equal centroid. Call them a,b,c and d,e,f, with a+b+c=d+e+f. The lattice generated by two edges from each triangle has index 3 in K*: relative to the basis a−c,b−c,d−c,e−c, use the triangle edges a−c,b−c,d−f,e−f, whose last 2×2 block is [[2,1],[1,2]]. Each edge parallelogram has area ≤√3β/2. Hence covol(K*)≤β²/4 and δ≤(3/2)β²·((6−β)/4)^22. This peaks at β=1/2 and is also less than δ₀.

All inequalities at stated maxima may be checked by squaring and comparing rationals. Thus the known five-sheet record remains the sharp maximum among certificates with at most six sheets.
