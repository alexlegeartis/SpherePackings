# Four dyadic checkerboard blocks in 17D (26 November 2026)

**Outcome: no new packing record.** The benchmark, 17D center density 1/16, is the classical laminated lattice in [Cohn's table](https://cohn.mit.edu/sphere-packing/); the opening project comparison is [the pinned 17D occupied-density row](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/SpherePackings/blob/6656d8659f0c78e2c754d6dfd9de48516dc30814/LOWER_BOUNDS_1_96.md). We test a different construction, allowing a separate local phase in each of three 4D blocks rather than a single global integral/half-integral phase. The squared minimum is normalized to four. This is a statement about a specified carrier, not all 17D packings. The equal-norm arithmetic neither asserts a new 256-site packing up to isometry nor a literature priority claim about the method.

### Four free scales; one target

Write D_n for the index-two even-sum checkerboard sublattice of Z^n. Let L=(D4+D4+D4+D5)/sqrt(2) (orthogonal sum). The centers are ANY union of L-cosets modulo 2L, followed by multiplying the squared metrics in the four blocks by arbitrary positive a,b,c,s. Pure periods require all scales >=1. The group L/2L has 2^17 elements. Its period covolume is 4096 sqrt(2) (abc)^2 s^(5/2), and at minimum four the center density of an M-point motif is the reciprocal of that covolume times M. An improvement requires M>256 sqrt(2) (abc)^2 s^(5/2).

In a D_n block the parity vector p of the raw coordinates of x has even weight; add a sheet bit sum_i(x_i)/2 mod 2. Both are linear on D_n and together specify D_n/2D_n. A nonzero parity pattern of weight w has squared depth w/2 in either sheet: choose signs on its nonzero coordinates to hit the required signed half-sum mod two. At zero parity the two depths are 0 and 2 (use 2e_i in the nonzero sheet). In D4 the three depth-two classes plus zero therefore form a subgroup of four. Regard its four cosets as colors, each with four internal labels. The distance between different colors is one; between unequal labels of the same color it is two. There are 64 phase triples, with no restriction to linear motifs. In D5 the classes of respective raw parity width 0 (zero sheet), 2, 4, 0 (nonzero sheet) have depths 0,1,2,2 and sizes 1,20,10,1.

For a fixed motif its feasible scales satisfy the box lower bounds and a*t1+b*t2+c*t3+s*t4>=4 on its actual difference profiles. Clip all scales at 4: no inequality is lost, as all positive coefficients are integers. Minimizing the logarithm of volume (concave) over the resulting compact feasible polytope has a vertex minimizer. For a vertex it suffices to intersect four independent constraints from the eight box faces and possible cost rows. Rows of coordinate sum>=4 are redundant on scales >=1; upper faces arise also as e_i·x=4. Sorting the first three scales only after enumeration gives 209 rational triples-plus-one in the enclosing box. They need not all be feasible for one given motif.

For each vertex use the normalized orbit means of the binary characters. For D4 with depth classes 0,1,2 the rows are (1,1,1), (1,-1/3,1),(1,0,-1/3). For D5 with the four classes above they are (1,1,1,1), (1,1/5,-3/5,1),(1,-1/5,1/5,1),(1,0,0,-1). Character orbits can be averaged (or check by enumerating and summing them), so a product row K_i(j) is the mean of the associated characters evaluated at any displacement in geometric class j. On arbitrary motifs the corresponding Fourier sum is nonnegative, as it averages squared character sums. Thus whenever nonnegative y_i and g>0 give sum y_i K_i(j)<=-g on every nonzero allowable j, the motif has M<=1+sum y_i/g. The standalone checker verifies every allowed inequality for all 209 vertices using rational arithmetic; the densities use exact squared comparisons. At 208 vertices the bound is below or at the benchmark. At (1,1,1,1) it gives M<=397.

**Correct quantifier:** if any choice of four scales improves the benchmark, that very motif is feasible with at least as high density at equal scales. This does not assert that an unequal interior scale cannot itself give a record. Conversely any isotropic motif of at least 363 points gives a record. Hence the range worth searching is 363–397, with no positive witness yet in that range. In particular **no additive motif**, at any of these four independent scales, gives a record: its size is a power of two and 256<363<=397<512.

### Six packets: a polynomial-size obstruction instead of random restarts

Suppose at equal scales that six distinct whole translates z_i+H of one binary subgroup H (order h) are mutually feasible. No independence of z_i is assumed. On the product of the geometry group and the even binary words t of length 6, form the subgroup of all (x,t) with x−sum_i t_i z_i in H. It has 32h elements. For t=0 nonidentity, and for weight(t)=2, the geometric cost must be at least four. Nothing is imposed on its geometric cost at weights 4 and 6; in particular x=0 there is allowed. The total counts in auxiliary weights 0,2,4,6 are h,15h,15h,h.

Use four extra character types classified by support s=0,1,2,3 modulo complementation. Their means at even weight v are Krawtchouk sums D_s(v)=sum_j (-1)^j C(s,j)C(6-s,v-j)/C(6,v). Permute the three isometric D4 blocks simultaneously in the Fourier rows to get averaging over their six permutations. If N_jv are the subgroup word counts excluding the origin, every Fourier-row sum over the N_jv is >=-1 (including the origin it is nonnegative). Their combined total is 32h−1. Fix integer dual weights y>=0 and free z_2,z_4,z_6 in dual6.json, scale S=10^8. The checker bounds above by epsilon every column of
sum y_i K_i(j)D_i(v) - sum_(u=2,4,6) z_u ([v=u]-C(6,u)[v=0]) + S[v=0].
Multiplying by N_jv and summing yields
(S−32epsilon) h <= S+sum y+15 z_2+15 z_4+z_6−epsilon.
Both the origin contribution and the three affine row totals are accounted for here. Exact result: epsilon=82/75 and h<=100517603567/1874999344<54. Consequently six packets of 64 are impossible. A record cannot have a translation stabilizer of order >=64, as it would comprise at least six order-64 packets at the isotropic vertex. This necessary condition holds for an improving motif even if originally proposed at unequal scales.

### An explicit seed and an exhaustive trade on its 32-site subcodes

The 17-bit group words in verify.py are x0 + 16*x1 + 256*x2 + 4096*o. A horizontal symbol x=0 is depth zero, x=1,2,3 are its depth-two subgroup, x=4..15 have depth one. The low four bits of o give the first four parity coordinates, the fifth being their parity; the top bit is the sheet. The eight independent generators (31181,58116,71012,35581,40360,35279,18,258) produce a verified 256-point additive seed. This is a lower example for the carrier only: its center density 1/(16 sqrt(2)) is below the benchmark. The C++ verifier separately treats **all** 97,155 dimension-five subspaces H of this particular seed. For each, partition all ambient points into H-cosets, retain the cosets compatible with H, and find whether eleven other pairwise-compatible cosets exist; it also checks every size above the known eight up to twelve. A translate can always be chosen to put H in the motif. The candidate quotient has 4096 elements and uses exact bit masks; DFS prunes only via strictly valid greedy proper colorings of its compatibility graph. It proves **no union of more than eight** such cosets exists: a larger feasible set would contain a subset of size nine. This conclusion does not require retaining the seed's centers, only using one of its H as a translation symmetry. Kernels not contained in the seed, smaller stabilizers, and motifs having no translation symmetry except 2L remain unresolved. A twelve-packet alternative would mean 384 sites and ratio 3/(2 sqrt(2)) over the 17D record. The twelve-label Fourier relaxation by itself permits an order-32 kernel; this is not an existence argument.

### A clean reason the original two-phase transfer fails even without periodic normal assignments

Use 2Z^12 rather than the three checkerboard subblocks for the horizontal period; at each normal site of D5/sqrt(2), allow arbitrary binary words in either integral or the common (1/2)^12 phase. In any translate of the 16-site even demicube (the even binary words of length 5), external squared distances are at most two. Within a fixed phase, no horizontal binary word occurs twice in those 16 rows and their union B has minimum Hamming distance at least two. Three words all adjacent to one binary word would have pairwise horizontal distance two, forcing three external binary positions pairwise at Hamming distance four: this is impossible in length five (after translation two weight-four sets meet in at least three). Thus each radius-one binary shell around a point outside B contains at most two elements of B, and contains none around a point in B. Hence 12|B|<=2(4096−|B|) and |B|<=585. Both phases combined contribute at most 1170 words per tile, averaging at most 73.125 per outer site, also with nonperiodic normal choices by averaging over translates and discarding a vanishing boundary. A 17D record needs more than 64 sqrt(2)=90.5096 per site. This exclusion concerns just these two common shifts at unit scale.

Replay: `python3 verify.py` (standard library, exact). To repeat the separate 97,155-subspace classification: `c++ -std=c++17 -O3 anchored.cpp -o anchored && ./anchored`. No solver or network is required. The optional optimization search used only to propose the bundled duals is not needed by the proof.
