# Every fixed finite-period version of the 14D Hamming–Witt stack is strictly locally optimal

**Scope.** This applies to the [specified 14D packing](README.md), with period sublattices of the stated 2Z^12 × 6A₂ period, arbitrary infinitesimal strain and arbitrary independent velocities of the sites in each fixed finite supercell. It is local only, with a neighborhood that may depend on the supercell. It does not bound global density in R^14. It does not assert rigidity under aperiodic infinite-dimensional perturbations.

Use the packing with minimum distance 2, horizontal dimension 12 and vertical triangular grid dimension two. Label the Witt half-integer sheets W and the shortened extended Hamming integer sheets B. Every W–B nearest vertical edge has an actual contact between every pair of word labels, with one unique closest horizontal translate, at horizontal difference s having all coordinates ±1/2. The binary–binary nearest edges also have actual contacts, at weight-three horizontal differences: their missing-column syndrome has covering depth exactly three (finitely verified). Hamming code cosets are affine translates of one linear code; each word on either side of a binary–binary edge has the same number of weight-three partners. For every such contact the three signs are independently available (integer horizontal coordinates, period two).

## Finite ingredients

For either code (and any binary translate), its graph using weight-four differences through any fixed coordinate is connected; at any word, every pair of coordinates lies in an incident weight-four difference. Every coordinate is balanced. For the Witt code every four-coordinate set is realized as a weight-four difference (the checker uses the explicit code in verify.py). These are exact independent finite checks: Witt 144 and Hamming 128 vertices each in every connected coordinate graph, and 495 supports for Witt. For any given coordinate j one can choose a realised Hamming four-support S containing j and a realised Witt four-support T intersecting it only in j. Thus a rectangle over two labels in each bank isolates that coordinate in any sum of sign products. These finite facts do not depend on vertical labels or period size.

## All needed contacts are equalities

It suffices to work in a rectangular supercell contained in the original given finite period: a sufficiently large product of horizontal and vertical periods is contained in any finite-index sublattice. Use this product cell for each average below.

For a fixed finite period write H for the symmetric strain after gauging rotations, and w_a for the periodic correction at site a. First-order nonoverlap is dᵀHd+d·(w_b−w_a)≥0 on each contact, where d is the original displacement. Normalize tr H≤0 (nonincreasing covolume). Decompose H into horizontal and vertical diagonal blocks H_h,H_v and off-diagonal H_hv. Averaging the length-two axial bars 2e_i over all horizontal translates of each site type gives (H_h)_{ii}≥0.

For a W–B vertical edge ν, average every cross-contact and horizontal translate. Sums of horizontal signed components vanish by balanced code labels. The Hamming code also has every two-coordinate binary projection uniform (verified by counting; the property propagates to all cosets). Thus the product sign vectors have zero off-diagonal second moment and mean zero. Horizontal velocity terms cancel for each word by balance of the opposite bank, while horizontal/vertical strain cross-terms cancel by zero mean. The resulting inequality is
(1/4)tr H_h+νᵀH_vν+ν·(m_q−m_p)≥0
with m_p the mean vertical velocity over the whole fibre and its horizontal period. At every W site the incident six normal directions sum to zero, and at every B site the three incident directions to W sum to zero; their global second moment is isotropic in the plane. Therefore averaging these inequalities gives (1/4)tr H_h+(1/2)tr H_v≥0. Since tr H≤0 and the horizontal diagonal is nonnegative, all its diagonal entries and tr H_v are zero. In the two averaged arguments all nonnegative summands with positive weights must be exact equalities, so every horizontal axial and W–B bar is an equality at first order.

In each sheet, average all 16 sign variants and all translates of any weight-four difference; now zero horizontal trace on that support and the signed cancellation give zero. Therefore these within-sheet bars are all equalities as well. At horizontal mean character, the quadratic Walsh coefficients in these signs give (H_h)_{ij}=0 for every i≠j by the coordinate-pair property, so H_h=0. For a binary–binary nearest edge average all weight-three witnesses, their signs and translations. The partner count observation makes the vertical velocities uniform in this average; thus the remaining average equals νᵀH_vν+ν·(m_q−m_p)≥0. Binary–binary edges form the honeycomb with incident directions balanced at every vertex. Their directions are isotropic at the global level, and tr H_v=0. Hence these bars too are all equalities. No assumption of an axial short contact in the vertical plane was made.

## Nonzero horizontal Fourier characters vanish

Fourier decompose in horizontal period translations only, keeping vertical sheet sites individually. For a nontrivial character χ put ξ_i=χ(2e_i), choose j with ξ_j≠1. The horizontal axial equalities kill horizontal velocity component j everywhere. On a weight-four contact containing j and any desired other horizontal coordinate i, flip just sign j; the horizontal periods of the two endpoints differ by 2e_j and the zero j-component does not change the signed linear form. Subtracting the two equations forces the same linear form at the target to be zero, for every choice of the other three signs, and then at the source too. The coordinate-pair property kills every horizontal component.

For a W–B nearest edge only the two vertical velocity projections along ν remain. At every pair of words a,b they satisfy v_a=χ(2p(a,b))v_b, up to fixed per-sheet phases and orientation. With integer word a and half-integer word b one can take p_i(a,b)=−(1−a_i)b_i. If one projection is nonzero, all on both sides are nonzero, so the matrix of phase factors has multiplicative rank one. Its rectangular identities imply ∏_i ξ_i^{(a_i−a'_i)(b_i−b'_i)}=1. Choose two word differences whose supports intersect in just j, forcing ξ_j=1, a contradiction. Thus both projected velocities are zero. Every sheet has at least two nonparallel W–B edges, so the vertical components vanish on the nonzero horizontal character as well.

## The zero horizontal character is a common translation

In each sheet the horizontal correction is independent of the word label: the linear Walsh coefficient in each site-to-site weight-four contact makes coordinate i agree along the connected coordinate-i graph. Let a_p be this horizontal correction in sheet p. On a W–B edge ν the equality for every pair of labels has the form
s·(a_q−a_p+2H_hvν)+ν·(v_{q,b}−v_{p,a})+νᵀH_vν=0.
Taking rectangular differences cancels the separated vertical point terms and isolates each component of the sign coefficient using the finite support intersections. Thus a_q−a_p=−2H_hvν. The remaining equations force the projection of vertical velocity along ν to be word-independent in both sheets. Again two nonparallel incident W–B edges at every sheet force its whole vertical correction v_p to be constant over words. The W–B graph is connected: two-step walks through a B sheet connect neighboring W sites along the generators (1,1) and (2,−1) of the W sublattice. The relation for a_p and periodicity across two independent normal periods imply H_hv=0 and a_p common.

By the W–B and B–B equalities all nearest edges of the full triangular grid preserve length under the planar field y_p=H_v p+v_p. On each elementary triangle a length-preserving first-order field is an infinitesimal rigid motion. Adjacent triangles share two vertices and hence their planar rigid motions agree; propagate through the grid. Since v_p is periodic, H_v must equal this global skew infinitesimal rotation, and being symmetric it vanishes. Thus all v_p are common. The only zero-strain correction is a common 14D translation.

Finally, if distinct feasible representatives of nonincreasing volume approached the packing modulo similarities in this finite cell, normalize the displacement vectors of all their parameters, after fixing translation and orientation. Compactness gives a nontrivial first-order tangent with all contact derivatives nonnegative and tr H≤0, contradicting the result. No nonuniform limit over growing cells is taken.
